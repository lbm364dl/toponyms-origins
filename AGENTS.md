# Repository guidance

## Canonical content

- Edit public facts and prose in their canonical source files. Never hide a
  content problem with a renderer-only replacement.
- Station copy lives in `content/stations/<id>/<lang>/`. The public narrative
  fields are `summary.short.md`, `summary.md`, and `story.md`.
- `metadata.json` is also canonical. In particular,
  `sources[].relevance_es` is displayed publicly on the Spanish site.
- `docs/` is generated by `scripts/build_site.py`. Regenerate it after source
  changes; do not hand-edit generated HTML or generated data to fix content.

## Public editorial standard

Write public fields as finished, neutral prose for a reader who wants to know
the origin of a name. They are articles, not research reports, prompts, review
notes, or messages to an editor.

- State the fact directly: `No consta una fecha oficial para el cambio` or
  `La hipótesis es probable, pero no está demostrada`.
- Distinguish verified facts, probable interpretations, disputed theories, and
  legends in the prose itself. Attribute claims naturally when attribution
  matters.
- Never mention the task, research session, draft, previous wording, editor,
  developer, card, page, public version, or how the text should be written.
- Do not use first-person research narration or process language such as
  `he encontrado`, `hemos consultado`, `se ha localizado`, `la redacción
  actual`, `la explicación de partida`, `para la ficha`, `explicación pública`,
  `conviene presentar`, or `debe formularse`.
- Do not turn uncertainty into a to-do for the reader or maintainer. Replace
  `queda por comprobar` with the actual evidential limit, such as `no consta en
  la documentación disponible`.
- Apply the same neutral standard to source descriptions. A source description
  says what the source documents and what its limits are; it does not instruct
  anyone to consult or revise something.
- Keep workflow analysis in non-public fields such as
  `current-claim-assessment.md`, `research-note.md`, `corrections`, and
  `open_questions`. Do not copy that voice into public fields.

## Language and names

- Use correct Spanish spelling, punctuation, accents, and `ñ` in every visible
  label and Spanish narrative field.
- Match official transport display names exactly. Keep IDs, route keys, and
  slugs stable even when the display name needs a diacritic.
- Preserve real distinctions between a station and its namesake. For example,
  the official station is `Sainz de Baranda`, while the person is Pedro
  `Sáinz de Baranda`.
- Use only Markdown supported by the site renderer and verify that no literal
  `**`, backticks, or Markdown links leak into generated visible text.

## Required verification

After content or site changes, run:

```bash
SITE_BASE_URL=https://nombresdemadrid.es/ python3 scripts/build_site.py
SITE_BASE_URL=https://nombresdemadrid.es/ python3 scripts/check_seo.py
```

The SEO audit is also a content-quality gate. Treat failures about public tone,
Spanish orthography, official names, or leaked Markdown as publication blockers.
