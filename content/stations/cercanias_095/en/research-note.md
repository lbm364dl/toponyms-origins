Toponomasticon Hispaniae does not appear to have a dedicated Zarzalejo entry available in search results, but its Moraleja entry is useful because it explicitly names Zarzalejo as a cognate-style example of plant-name suffixation. The Comunidad de Madrid guide is the strongest inspected station-relevant source for the exact local nuance: El Zarzalejo in some eighteenth-century documents, the Andrés Marín quote, the local legend, and the railway opening date.
