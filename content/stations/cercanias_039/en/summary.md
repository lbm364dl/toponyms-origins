The station was opened to serve the Soto del Henares area of Torrejón de Ardoz, and Renfe describes it as a halt in the neighbourhood of the same name. Linguistically, soto is a Spanish word for a wooded riverbank or grove, from Latin saltus, so the name points to the riparian landscape of the Henares. The wider name is also borne by an officially protected natural area on meanders of the Henares, declared in 2000 in Alcalá de Henares and Los Santos de la Humosa. The Torrejón urban development of Soto del Henares was underway by 2006, and archaeological work connected to that plan uncovered a major prehistoric site occupied from the Neolithic to the Early Iron Age. The station name is therefore best explained as a modern transport name taken from a local neighbourhood, whose name preserves a descriptive river-landscape term.
