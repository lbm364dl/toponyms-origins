The direct station naming is well supported by Renfe and the Ayuntamiento: the stop is in and serves the Soto del Henares neighbourhood. The meaning of soto is directly supported by the RAE, and the official natural-area decree confirms the wider Soto del Henares river landscape. Confidence is probable rather than fully verified because I did not find a formal naming resolution for the station or the original municipal act assigning the urban sector name.
