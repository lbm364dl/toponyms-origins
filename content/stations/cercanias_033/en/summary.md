Asamblea de Madrid-Entrevías began as the Entrevías halt, opened on 10 February 1977 on the Madrid-Barcelona railway corridor. Its older name comes from the barrio of Entrevías, a Madrid toponym glossed by the Instituto de Estudios Madrileños as a neighbourhood situated between two railway lines. The first part of the current name was added after the Assembly of Madrid moved to its new Puente de Vallecas seat, inaugurated on 28 September 1998. The best-supported reading is therefore dual: a modern civic landmark plus a local railway-shaped place-name. The precise administrative act for the renaming still needs confirmation in railway or official naming records.
