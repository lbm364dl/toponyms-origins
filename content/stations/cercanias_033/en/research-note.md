The station is sometimes shortened in operational contexts to Asamblea-Entrevías or Asamblea Madrid-Entrevías, while passenger-facing CRTM and Renfe/Adif records preserve the full institutional form. One secondary source says the renaming occurred in 1999, but the stronger contextual chronology points to the Assembly’s 28 September 1998 inauguration as the cause and likely naming moment. The official Assembly page locates the new seat in Madrid Sur, Puente de Vallecas; other descriptions often say Entrevías broadly, though administratively the building is commonly associated with Palomeras Bajas/Madrid Sur near Entrevías.
