Alonso de Mendoza is a MetroSur station in Getafe, opened with Line 12 on 11 April 2003. Its name comes from the adjacent Calle de Alonso de Mendoza rather than from a transport interchange or district name. The strongest local explanation identifies the street's namesake as Alonso de Mendoza y Núñez de Prado, a late-medieval/early-modern hidalgo connected with Bonilla de la Sierra and with Getafe through his charitable bequest. In his will of 5 September 1507, he ordered the endowment of hospital care in Getafe for poor sick people, prioritising those from Getafe, Pinto, and Griñón. A recurring error is to confuse him with the conquistador Alonso de Mendoza, founder of La Paz in Bolivia; the Getafe toponym is better supported as referring to the hospital benefactor.
