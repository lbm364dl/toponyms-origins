The best revision to the record is not to change the person, but to make the chain explicit: station -> adjacent Calle de Alonso de Mendoza -> local benefactor Alonso de Mendoza y Núñez de Prado. The English Wikipedia-derived conquistador explanation is contradicted by stronger Getafe-specific local history. The foundation's own wording also makes 'founder' slightly imprecise, because it says the 1507 will provided for expanding an existing hospital and giving it statutes and ordinances.
