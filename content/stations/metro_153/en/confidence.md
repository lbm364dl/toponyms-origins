The station's location and 2003 MetroSur opening are supported by official transport and regional sources, and the adjacent Calle de Alonso de Mendoza is shown on the CRTM zonal plan. Multiple local history and foundation sources identify the relevant Alonso de Mendoza as the Hospital de San José benefactor and explicitly warn against the conquistador confusion. Confidence is not absolute only because the municipal street-naming act was not found.
