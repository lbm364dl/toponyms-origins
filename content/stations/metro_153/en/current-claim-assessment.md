The existing claim is substantially correct: the station is best explained through the nearby Calle de Alonso de Mendoza, and the best-supported local identification is Alonso de Mendoza y Núñez de Prado, benefactor of Getafe's Hospital de San José. It should be nuanced because some sources say he enlarged or endowed an existing hospital, not simply founded one from nothing, and his exact death date is not directly established by the inspected sources.
