La Rambla is a Line 7 station in Coslada, opened with the MetroEste extension in 2007. The name is best understood as a local place name: La Rambla, the urban axis and facilities around Calle Honduras, Avenida de los Príncipes de España and the Valleaguado/La Espinilla area. Linguistically, rambla comes from Andalusi Arabic rámla and classical Arabic ramlah, meaning a sandy place, and Spanish dictionaries define it as a natural bed for heavy rainwater; in urban usage it can also mean a broad promenade. The stronger evidence supports the local urban toponym and the Arabic/geomorphological meaning of the word, but not the more specific claim that this exact Coslada name records a documented ancient stream.
