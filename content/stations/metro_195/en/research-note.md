The strongest correction is methodological: La Rambla is locally attested as an urban axis and set of facilities, while the hydrological explanation belongs first to the word rambla, not yet to a documented Coslada stream. A municipal 2023 account says the development of La Rambla began in 2008, after the station opened in 2007, so the pre-station source of the name may have been an already existing local area, shopping centre, park/paseo concept or planning name rather than the finished paseo as it exists today.
