Las Margaritas Universidad is best understood as a place-based name. The first half comes from Las Margaritas, a Getafe neighbourhood built in the 1970s and officially described by the town council as a small, dense area with schools, sports facilities, the Universidad Carlos III campus and the Fernando de los Ríos residence. The floral character is real but should be stated carefully: official regional documentation lists many Las Margaritas streets named after flowers, including Adelfa, Alhelí, Amapola, Azahar, Azucena, Camelias, Clavel, Crisantemo, Flor de Lis, Geranio, Gladiolo, Hortensia, Jazmín, Lila, Loto, Nardos, Pensamiento and Rosa, but not every street in the area is floral. The Universidad element points to Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, created by law on 5 May 1989 with its Faculty of Social and Legal Sciences initially placed on the Getafe campus. A secondary rail source reports the station was renamed Las Margaritas-Universidad on 28 May 1995, but the strongest current operator sources now show a naming mismatch: CRTM uses Las Margaritas - Universidad, while Adif and Renfe use Las Margaritas.
