The core claim is well supported: the station name refers to Getafe's Las Margaritas area and to the nearby Universidad Carlos III de Madrid campus. However, the claim overstates that all streets are flower-named, relies on weak evidence for the daisy streetlamp detail, and needs a 2026 caveat because Adif and Renfe now list the station simply as Las Margaritas while CRTM and Getafe still use Las Margaritas - Universidad.
