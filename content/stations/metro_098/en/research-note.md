The best-supported chain is station -> Plaza/Glorieta del Conde de Casal -> Manuel Escrivá de Romaní y de la Quintana. The Madrid open-data CSV proves the official plaza date but not the biographical dedication; the Instituto de Estudios Madrileños supplies the bridge between the street name and the person and notes the earlier unofficial Marqués de Cerralbo name. The Prado page contains one likely problematic phrase, calling him 'Alcalde de Madrid'; other sources and the street-name article support councillor/deputy mayor instead, so public copy should avoid calling him mayor unless confirmed from a municipal source.
