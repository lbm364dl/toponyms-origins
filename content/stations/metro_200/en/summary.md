Aeropuerto T1-T2-T3 is a practical transport name rather than a commemorative one. It identifies the Line 8 station that Aena lists for terminals 1, 2 and 3 of Adolfo Suárez Madrid-Barajas Airport, while a separate station serves T4. The stop opened on 14 June 1999 as Aeropuerto, and the current name was assigned on 3 May 2007 after the Line 8 extension to Aeropuerto T4. The airport itself opened to air traffic on 22 April 1931 near the then municipality of Barajas, began commercial operations in 1933, and officially became Aeropuerto Adolfo Suárez Madrid-Barajas in 2014.
