Arganda del Rey station is the eastern terminus of Madrid Metro Line 9 and is named directly after the municipality where it stands. The place-name Arganda is best explained by Jairo J. Garcia Sanchez as a Celtic or Indo-European formation from *arg-, meaning 'clear', 'white' or 'bright', a root often found in names connected with shining or clear waters. The royal qualifier 'del Rey' is historical: municipal sources connect it with Philip II's removal of Arganda from the Archbishopric of Toledo and its status as a royal town in 1580-1583. The Line 9 extension opened on 7 April 1999 and brought Metro service to Rivas and Arganda through a TFM concession.
