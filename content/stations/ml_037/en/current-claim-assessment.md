The row is broadly correct about the station being the ML3 terminus, opened on 27 July 2007, and located at the Virgen de la Paz roundabout in Siglo XXI. The deeper etymology of Boadilla is overstated: Toponomasticon Hispaniae treats the origin as unresolved between a Germanic architectural root related to vaults and a Latin-Romance bovata meaning a bovine pasture or ox-ploughed land.
