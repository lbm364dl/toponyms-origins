Puerta de Boadilla takes its name from its role as the ML3 terminus in Boadilla del Monte, beside the Virgen de la Paz roundabout and the Siglo XXI district. The line was put into service on 27 July 2007 between Colonia Jardín and Boadilla. The deeper place name Boadilla del Monte is older than the light rail and is documented in variant forms such as Bohadilla, Bobadilla and Boadilla. Toponomasticon Hispaniae does not support a single simple derivation from a small vault: it leaves two plausible roots, an architectural word related to vaults or a Latin-Romance term for bovine pasture. The added qualifier del Monte appears later than the base name and served to distinguish this Boadilla from other places with the same name while also describing its wooded, uneven terrain.
