Pan Bendito station is named after the place around it: the Pan Bendito colony and neighbourhood area in Carabanchel. The station lies under Avenida de Abrantes, at the junction with Calle Besolla, and the official transport record identifies it as a Line 11 station serving this part of the district.

The name itself is older than the Metro station. Local accounts explain it through the landscape that preceded the modern blocks: fields of wheat on the edge of the old Carabancheles, from which bread could be made. In that telling, the expression Pan Bendito joins bread with a religiously charged adjective: bread as a sacred, blessed food.

The modern neighbourhood was shaped much later by Madrid's twentieth-century housing crisis. After Carabanchel Alto and Carabanchel Bajo were annexed to Madrid on 29 April 1948, the district absorbed waves of working-class migration and rehousing. Municipal and neighbourhood-planning documents describe the Colonia de Vista Alegre and the Unidad Vecinal de Absorcion de Pan Bendito as provisional housing areas developed in the 1957-1963 period for working families and later included in barrio-remodelling programmes.

The Metro arrived at a symbolic moment in that longer urban story. The Community of Madrid records the Plaza Eliptica-Pan Bendito section of Line 11 as entering service on 16 November 1998, with three stations: Plaza Eliptica, Abrantes and Pan Bendito. Pan Bendito was the terminus until the 2006 extension towards Carabanchel Alto, so the neighbourhood's name briefly marked the end of the line.

The safest public explanation is therefore two-layered: the station is named after the Pan Bendito place-name, and the place-name is locally understood as a bread-and-wheat name with religious overtones. The specific claim that it came from the church custom of distributing blessed bread is possible, since such traditions exist in Spain, but no source was found tying that custom directly to this Madrid toponym. The wheat-field explanation is better attested in local and press accounts, though still not proven by an original naming document.
