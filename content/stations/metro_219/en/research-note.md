The previous wording's strongest supported parts are the place-name chain and the twentieth-century housing context. The weakest part is the precise explanation 'distribution of blessed bread in church': it is semantically plausible and culturally real, but the sources found for this Madrid name instead repeat a wheat-field/bread-sacredness explanation. The nearby San Benito Abad parish is important to neighbourhood history, but because it was built in 1963-1964, it should not be treated as the origin of the older Pan Bendito place-name without stronger evidence.
