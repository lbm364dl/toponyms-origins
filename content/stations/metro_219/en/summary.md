Pan Bendito is a Line 11 Metro station under Avenida de Abrantes, by Calle Besolla, named for the surrounding Pan Bendito area of Carabanchel. Official transport sources document Pan Bendito as one of the three stations in the first Line 11 section, opened on 16 November 1998. The neighbourhood name predates the Metro and is attached to the Colonia/UVA de Pan Bendito, a major social-housing and rehousing area built in the mid twentieth century. Local accounts explain the unusual name by saying that the land had wheat fields and that bread, considered sacred or blessed, gave the place its name. That explanation is plausible and locally repeated, but I did not find an archival source proving the original naming event.
