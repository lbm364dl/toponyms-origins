Recoletos is a transport name inherited from the street above it: Paseo de Recoletos. That paseo was named after the convent of the Augustinian Recollects, founded in 1592 by Eufrasia de Guzmán, princess of Ascoli. The word recoleto refers to a religious person or order following a stricter, more withdrawn observance. The convent disappeared after the 1836 disentailment, but its name remained attached to one of Madrid's great north-south boulevards. The station name is therefore religious in origin, though today it is experienced mainly as an urban place-name.
