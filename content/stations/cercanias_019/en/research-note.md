For the dataset, separate two layers: the station etymology is a verified place-name and central-location name, while the municipal toponym is uncertain. The strongest public formulation is: 'named for Getafe; Getafe is traditionally explained from Arabic Xetafe/Jata as a long settlement along the Madrid-Toledo road, but modern toponymy treats that as unproven.' Avoid presenting 'calle larga' as a literal established translation; the 1575 text says 'cosa larga' and frames it as information from tradition.
