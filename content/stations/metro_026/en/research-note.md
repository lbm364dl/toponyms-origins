The best-supported etymology is descriptive and institutional rather than inherited toponymy: the name was chosen by transport authorities to solve wayfinding ambiguity and to brand the station as part of the museum corridor. The official 2018 note says the change would 'disipar la ambigüedad' between Atocha and Atocha Renfe; this context should be preserved because in 2022 Atocha-Renfe itself was simplified to Atocha, meaning modern users again see an Atocha station nearby but the Estación del Arte name remains tied to the museum function.
