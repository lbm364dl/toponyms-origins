The station was opened in 1921 as Atocha, serving the area around the Glorieta del Emperador Carlos V and the railway station then known as Estación del Mediodía. In 2018 the Community of Madrid changed its name to Estación del Arte. Official explanations give two connected reasons: to remove ambiguity between Metro's Atocha and Atocha Renfe stations, and to identify this stop as an access point to Madrid's principal art museums. The new name is reinforced by decorative reproductions of works from the Prado, Reina Sofía and Thyssen-Bornemisza in the station's passages, platforms and halls.
