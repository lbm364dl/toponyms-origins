Official Community of Madrid documentation explicitly states that El Capricho station is named for its proximity to the historic park of the same name, and gives the opening date of the extension as 24 November 2006. Madrid City Council directly explains the origin of the park name and attributes it to María Josefa Alonso Pimentel. The only weaker point is the provisional name Parque de la Luz, which is supported by non-official or derived sources rather than a primary naming resolution.
