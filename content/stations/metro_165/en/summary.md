El Capricho is a Line 5 station opened on 24 November 2006 as part of the extension from Canillejas to Alameda de Osuna. The Community of Madrid states directly that the station received its name because of its proximity to the historic park of the same name. The park was the personal creation of María Josefa Alonso Pimentel, Countess-Duchess of Benavente and Duchess consort of Osuna, who acquired the estate and adjoining land between 1783 and 1803. Madrid’s municipal description explains that she herself gave it the name El Capricho, and that the garden contains several caprichos: whimsical architectural and landscape features such as the apiary, hermitage, fort, dance casino and Casa de la Vieja.
