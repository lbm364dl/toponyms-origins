The station-name etymology is unusually well supported because the Community of Madrid’s construction page states the reason directly. The park-name etymology is also stronger than the previous wording suggests: the City Council says the creator herself gave the garden the name El Capricho and links it to the caprichos distributed through the garden. Parque de la Luz is best treated as a projected or provisional name, not a previous public name, unless a primary naming document is later found.
