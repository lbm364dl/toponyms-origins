The input row is stronger than many station-name claims because the Community of Madrid explicitly says the street gives the station its name. The weak point is the jump from the street label to Saint Cyprian of Carthage. That is a reasonable default because he is the major saint known as San Cipriano, but Madrid’s official street directory gives location and numbering, not the dedication rationale. No evidence for a surviving Vicálvaro hermitage of San Cipriano or for a station former name.
