San Cipriano is a street-derived Metro name. The Community of Madrid states that the station accesses are on Calle de San Cipriano and that this street gives the station its name. Madrid's official historical Callejero records the current Vicálvaro Calle de San Cipriano from 22 February 1961 and the current-vials file places it across Vicálvaro barrios. The saint behind the street is most plausibly Saint Cyprian of Carthage, bishop, writer, and martyr in 258, but the exact municipal naming record has not been found. The station opened with the Pavones-Puerta de Arganda extension on 1 December 1998.
