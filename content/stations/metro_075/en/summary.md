San Cipriano is a street-derived Metro name. The Community of Madrid states that the station accesses are on Calle de San Cipriano and that this street gives the station its name. Madrid’s official street directory confirms Calle de San Cipriano in Vicálvaro, running from Calle de Ambroz to Calle del Jardín de la Duquesa. The saint behind the street is most plausibly Saint Cyprian of Carthage, bishop, writer, and martyr in 258, but the exact municipal naming record has not been found. The station opened with the Pavones-Puerta de Arganda extension on 1 December 1998.
