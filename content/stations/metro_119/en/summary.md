Lago is a straightforward place name: the station stands by the Casa de Campo Lake. The lake’s history is older and more complex than the modern recreational setting suggests. Municipal sources trace it to five artificial ponds built in the 16th century near the Meaques stream for irrigation, flood control, fishing, and royal leisure. By the 19th century, the surviving larger ponds had merged and were reshaped into the present irregular lake. The station entered service with the former Carabanchel Suburban Railway in 1961, giving Metro access to one of Madrid’s best-known park landscapes.
