The station name **García Noblejas** comes from the street setting around the stop, not from a separate Metro dedication. The Consorcio Regional de Transportes de Madrid still lists one of the station accesses as **Hermanos García Noblejas**, and Line 7 opened its first section between Las Musas and Pueblo Nuevo in 1974. In practice, the Metro name preserves the older street commemoration in the transport network.

The people behind the name were the García-Noblejas Brunet brothers, sons of Salvador García-Noblejas Quesada and Laura Brunet Goitia. Local historical research describes a wealthy family with roots in Manzanares and Madrid that moved toward far-right politics during the late Primo de Rivera period and the Second Republic. Several family members entered Falange early; one source identifies Ramón as carlist rather than falangist, an important nuance often lost in short summaries.

The Civil War turned the family name into a Franco-era symbol. José García-Noblejas Brunet was involved with Falangist militias in Madrid and was caught in the events around the Cuartel de la Montaña in July 1936. Salvador García-Noblejas Brunet was killed in Villaviciosa de Odón in September 1936; Jesús García-Noblejas Brunet later joined the Francoist army and died in 1937 after wounds received at Brunete; Javier García-Noblejas Brunet volunteered for the División Azul and died in Russia in 1941. Their father Salvador was also killed in the Paracuellos/Torrejón mass executions, but the street name refers to the brothers.

Madrid's 2017 historical-memory process made the name publicly contested again. The municipal Comisionado proposed replacing Calle de los Hermanos García Noblejas with a name honoring the Institución Libre de Enseñanza, describing the old name as linked to three falangist brothers and one carlist who supported the 1936 military uprising. The Junta de Gobierno approved the replacement on 4 May 2017, and the new street name became Avenida de la Institución Libre de Enseñanza.

Courts later annulled the street renaming. Press accounts of the judgment report that the court considered the municipal justification insufficient under the Historical Memory Law, and Telemadrid reported that Madrid restored the Hermanos García Noblejas plaques on 17 January 2022 after the TSJM ruling. The station, however, remained García Noblejas throughout: a San Blas-Canillejas district motion in June 2018 asked the Community of Madrid to rename the Metro station as Institución Libre de Enseñanza, but the current CRTM station page still uses García Noblejas.
