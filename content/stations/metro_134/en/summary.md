The Metro station is named after the street environment around it, especially the access recorded by CRTM as Hermanos García Noblejas. That street commemorates the García-Noblejas Brunet brothers, whose family became prominent in right-wing and Falangist circles before and during the Spanish Civil War. Madrid's historical-memory process tried to replace the street name with Avenida de la Institución Libre de Enseñanza in 2017, but courts later annulled that change and the city restored the street plaques in January 2022. The station itself appears not to have been renamed during the dispute.
