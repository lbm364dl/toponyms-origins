The name chain is directly supported by a Metro de Madrid station history PDF: Carpetana is located on Vía Carpetana, and Vía Carpetana is said to take its name from the Carpetani. The station opening and Line 6 chronology are supported by Metro/Community of Madrid sources. The only uncertainty is not the station etymology itself, but the exact municipal street-naming act for Vía Carpetana and the best wording for the Carpetani's ethnic-linguistic identity.
