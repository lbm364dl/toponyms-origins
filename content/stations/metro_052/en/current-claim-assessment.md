The core claim is verified: the station takes its name from Vía Carpetana, and Metro de Madrid explicitly says that the avenue's name comes from the Carpetani. Several details need correction or qualification: the station opened in 1983, the fossils were found during 2008-2009 accessibility and modernization works, not original construction, and the official museum name is Yacimiento de Carpetana rather than 'Andenes de Dinosaurios'. Calling the Carpetani simply 'Celtic' is also too strong, because an official heritage thesaurus notes that their ethnic-linguistic affiliation remains debated.
