The station name combines a local place-name, San Cristóbal, with a functional descriptor, Industrial. Official transport sources place the station at Calle Transversal Sexta in Madrid, and Renfe's station file lists it in the Polígono Industrial de Villaverde. The surrounding area is shaped by Villaverde's twentieth-century industrial history, including Colonia Marconi, La Resina and major factories such as Boetticher y Navarro and Barreiros. The best correction is to treat the name as a Madrid/Villaverde descriptive name, not as a reference to any San Cristóbal industrial estate in Valdemoro.
