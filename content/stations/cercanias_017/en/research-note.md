The strongest evidence is geographic and functional rather than a formal naming record: official sources consistently place the station in Madrid's Polígono Industrial de Villaverde and current municipal planning describes nearby La Resina, Los Ángeles and Colonia Marconi as activity areas connected to the San Cristóbal rail stop. The station may have inherited the older rail name San Cristóbal and gained Industrial to distinguish it from San Cristóbal de los Ángeles, but that renaming needs confirmation from historic timetables or an official resolution.
