Valdebebas station is named after the Valdebebas urban area in north-eastern Madrid. The stop was opened for the new residential and business district that grew beside the airport corridor, the Real Madrid training complex and the large forest park. In transport terms it is a recent name, but it did not begin as a branding exercise for a new neighbourhood.

Behind the station name is the older Arroyo de Valdebebas. Municipal and hydrological sources place this stream in the Jarama drainage system, running west to east across the northern edge of Madrid's urban expansion. A 1929 municipal sanitary report already treated the arroyo as the main natural drainage of the old Hortaleza area toward the Jarama basin.

The word itself belongs to a familiar family of Castilian place-names beginning with Valde-, a contracted form of 'valle de', or 'valley of'. The harder part is the second element. Toponymic material records older or variant forms such as Valdebeba, Valdebebas de Cristóbal, Valdebeba de Orgar and Valdevebas, but the available sources do not securely explain what 'beba' or 'bebas' meant here.

The modern district reused this old rural and hydrological name when Madrid planned the large operation known as Ciudad Aeroportuaria-Parque de Valdebebas in the framework of the 1997 General Plan and later planning documents. The forest park, inaugurated in 2015 with 470 hectares according to the Ayuntamiento, was designed over former wasteland, dumps and cultivated land, with ornamental water features and restored landscape references rather than a simple restoration of the whole old stream.

For public wording, the safest reading is therefore: the station is named after the Valdebebas district, and that district preserves the name of the Arroyo de Valdebebas and older Valdebeba rural toponyms. The 'Val-' element is clear; the meaning of 'bebas' is not. Explanations connecting it with drinking places for livestock or with a pre-Romance word should be presented only as hypotheses until a specialist source is consulted.
