Valdebebas is a modern station name attached to a much older landscape name. The Cercanías stop was put into service in December 2015 to serve the new Valdebebas development, near the Real Madrid training complex and the large forest park. Official municipal material states that the Valdebebas park and urban area lie by the Arroyo de Valdebebas, and older hydrological studies identify that stream as part of the drainage toward the Jarama. The first element is the common Spanish toponymic form Val-/Valde-, from 'valle' or 'valley'; the second element, historically seen in forms such as Valdebeba and Valdevebas, remains uncertain.
