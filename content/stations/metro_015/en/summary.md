The station is named after Gregorio Marañón y Posadillo, one of the most prominent Spanish medical and intellectual figures of the twentieth century. Official transport sources place the station at the José Abascal, Miguel Ángel, María de Molina and Paseo de la Castellana area, where the Plaza del Doctor Marañón gives the transport stop its immediate toponym. Marañón was a doctor, professor of endocrinology, writer, historian, academician and public figure whose career linked medicine with humanistic interpretation. The Metro station opened in 1998, first as part of the connection that integrated the former Line 8 into Line 10 and then as the western extension of Line 7 reached the same interchange.
