The station is named after Calle de Méndez Álvaro, the street on which the Metro, Cercanías and bus interchange is located. That street commemorates Francisco Méndez Álvaro (Pajares de Adaja, 1806-Madrid, 1883), a Spanish physician, hygienist, writer and medical journalist. Méndez Álvaro worked in public health, medical administration and vaccination, directed or co-directed influential medical publications, and became president of the Real Academia Nacional de Medicina. He also held political office, including a very brief period as mayor of Madrid in 1843, although one modern biographical source notes that this mayoral appointment has been difficult to document from municipal chronicles. The station name has been in use since the Metro Line 6 station opened in the Pacífico-Oporto extension on 7 May 1981.
