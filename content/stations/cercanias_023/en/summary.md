Pinto is a place-name station: Renfe and CRTM identify it as the Cercanías C-3 station in the municipality of Pinto. For the town name, the best current scholarly explanation is not the traditional punctum, 'point' or 'crossing', but a Latin/Romance colour adjective, *pincta/*pinctu or pinctus, meaning 'painted' or 'of a certain colour'. Toponomasticon Hispaniae records the etymon as Latin and classifies its value under colour, while García Sánchez had already proposed the same direction in 2007. The exact omitted noun, such as a hill, field, water, or other landscape feature, is not documented.
