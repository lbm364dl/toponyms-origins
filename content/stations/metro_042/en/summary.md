The station is named for Avenida de Menéndez Pelayo, the large north-south street beside the eastern boundary of El Retiro. That avenue commemorates Marcelino Menéndez y Pelayo (1856-1912), one of the major Spanish humanists and literary historians of the Restoration period. Metro de Madrid's historical material places the station under the former Calle del Pacífico, now Avenida de la Ciudad de Barcelona, between Gutenberg and Menéndez Pelayo. The station opened on 8 May 1923 with the Line 1 extension from Atocha to Puente de Vallecas. Secondary Madrid street-name sources indicate that the avenue received its current name in 1915, replacing older local names such as Calle de Muñoz, Ronda de Vicálvaro and Ronda de Vallecas along different sections.
