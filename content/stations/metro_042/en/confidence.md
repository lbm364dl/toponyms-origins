The station's location and accesses by Avenida de Menéndez Pelayo are confirmed by CRTM, and Metro de Madrid's historical file explicitly states that the station's name recalls the Cantabrian writer-scholar and gives the 8 May 1923 opening. The 1915 street-naming date and older street-section names rely on secondary street-name sources, so they are treated as supporting context rather than the basis of the station etymology.
