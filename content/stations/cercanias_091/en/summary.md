The Cercanías station is named after Valdelasfuentes, the Alcobendas area where it was built. The place-name is best read as 'valley of the springs/fountains': the RAE records val as an apocopated form of valle, and fuente as a spring of water rising from the ground. Local chronicler sources connect the name with the Arroyo de Valdelasfuentes and its springs, and report that the name appears in a Madrid Council act of 14 February 1494. The station itself opened with the Cantoblanco-Alcobendas rail extension in February 2001.
