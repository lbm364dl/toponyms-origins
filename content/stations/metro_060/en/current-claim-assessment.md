The core claim that the Metro station is named for Chamartín, historically Chamartín de la Rosa, is well supported. The deeper linguistic explanation is plausible, but the row overstates the evidence: the Toponomasticon page consulted is for Chamartín in Ávila and only cites Madrid’s Chamartín as a related case, while the 1247 form Echamartín appears to belong to the Ávila toponym, not directly to Chamartín de la Rosa.
