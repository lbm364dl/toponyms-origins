Las Rosas is the eastern terminus of Metro line 2 and was put into service on 16 March 2011 as part of the extension from La Elipa. Its name refers to the Las Rosas urbanization and the surrounding Rosas neighborhood in San Blas-Canillejas. Madrid municipal documents describe Rosas as a recent urbanized area whose name comes from containing the Las Rosas urbanization. The modern district context is especially tied to the 1985 General Plan and the Ensanche Este de San Blas, developed on Madrid's eastern edge during the 1990s. No authoritative source found confirms the popular explanation that the name derives from an old estate or rose garden.
