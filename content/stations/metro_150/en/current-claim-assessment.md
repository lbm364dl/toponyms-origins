The station-name claim is correct at the direct level: the Metro stop is named for the municipality it serves. The deeper explanation is mostly plausible but overstates certainty: municipal sources now present Vaciamadrid as probably from Manzil Mayrit, while Jaime Oliver Asín's stronger toponymic argument distinguishes Manzil Mayrit/Masalmadrit for the parador from Fahs Mayrit/Hafalmadrit for the communal meadow that evolved by folk etymology into Vaciamadrid.
