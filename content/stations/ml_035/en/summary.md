Siglo XXI is a place-derived station name, taken directly from Avenida del Siglo XXI in Boadilla del Monte. Metro Ligero Oeste describes the stop as being on the avenue of the same name, and the Consorcio Regional de Transportes gives its access as Avenida del Siglo XXI, 1. The Madrid regional project description places the end of the Colonia Jardín-Boadilla line in the new developments of Avenida Siglo XXI, beyond the M-50. The broader meaning of '21st century' fits the area's modern expansion, but the sources found prove the street and sector origin more clearly than any official symbolic explanation.
