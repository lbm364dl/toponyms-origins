Meco is a place-name station: its name comes from the Madrid municipality of Meco, shown by transport sources as the station's location and service name. The origin of the municipal name is not settled. A 1995 El Pais article summarized three traditional proposals: Celtic miccon, connected with sheep; Roman Miaccum, interpreted as a rural estate or stopping place; and Arabic maksusk, glossed as a bare hill. Stronger recent archaeological material makes the simple identification of Meco with the Roman mansio Miaccum doubtful, because El Beneficio in Collado Mediano has been proposed and interpreted as the Miaccum-related roadside site on Antonine Itinerary route XXIV. The safest public wording is therefore that the station is named after the municipality, while the municipality's own name remains contested.
