This case should not be flattened into either 'named after a saint' or 'named after a mother' without qualification. The transport name is geographically mediated by the neighbourhood and avenue. Official Madrid sources are strong for the 1971 residential-development chronology and the 1972 Avenida de Santa Eugenia street record, but the mother story currently depends on a local-memory article rather than a primary source. The strongest dataset update would mark the ultimate origin as contested/probable and preserve both the municipal saint explanation and the neighbourhood oral tradition.
