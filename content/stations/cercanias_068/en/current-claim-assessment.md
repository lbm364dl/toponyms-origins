The core claim is well supported: the station is named for the municipality, and the place-name is a Castilian compound of torre and lodones, plural of lodón, now more commonly called almez or hackberry. The medieval watchtower context is also well supported, but the inspected Toponomasticon entry cites ca. 1340 rather than 1287 as its medieval documentary form, while the municipal history page states 1287.
