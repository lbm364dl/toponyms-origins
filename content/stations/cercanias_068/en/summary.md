Torrelodones is a place-name before it is a station name: Cercanías simply adopted the name of the Madrid municipality. Toponomasticon Hispaniae explains it as a Castilian compound of torre and lodones, the plural of lodón, a tree now more commonly known in Spanish as almez. The tower in question is the Torre de los Lodones, the emblematic watchtower on a hill near the old settlement. The tower is generally linked to the Andalusi defensive landscape of the Madrid frontier, although the surviving place-name is Romance and therefore postdates the Christian conquest of Toledo in 1085. A municipal history page gives 1287 as an early documentary date, while the inspected Toponomasticon entry cites 'La Torre de Lodones' around 1340.
