Arroyo Culebro is a Line 12 MetroSur station in Getafe, located by Paseo de Juan José Rosón and Calle de las Ciencias. Its name comes from the Arroyo Culebro, a stream that rises around the Mari Pascuala lagoon in Polvoranca, Leganés, and flows toward the Manzanares in Getafe. Official and municipal sources show the same name attached not only to the watercourse, but also to transport, sanitation, parks, and urban development in the southern metropolitan corridor. Spanish culebro is an old masculine form related to culebra, from Latin colubra, so the literal sense is “snake stream.” No direct historical source was found proving that the name was coined because the stream meanders like a snake.
