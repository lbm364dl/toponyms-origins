Buenos Aires station is named for Avenida de Buenos Aires, one of its official access points on Metro Line 1. The avenue's name refers to Buenos Aires, the capital of Argentina, a city founded twice by Spaniards on the Río de la Plata. A BNE digitized issue of El Imparcial from 10 March 1880 already mentions land on calle de Buenos Aires in Puente de Vallecas. The official historical Callejero records the current Avenida de Buenos Aires from 30 April 1968 after an earlier Avenida de Cerro de Buenos Aires row; a local gazetteer still needs checking against the underlying maps for the older street sequence.
