Buenos Aires station is named for Avenida de Buenos Aires, one of its official access points on Metro Line 1. The avenue's name refers to Buenos Aires, the capital of Argentina, a city founded twice by Spaniards on the Río de la Plata. A BNE digitized issue of El Imparcial from 10 March 1880 already mentions land on calle de Buenos Aires in Puente de Vallecas. A local historical street gazetteer connects that older street with today's Calle Sierra del Cadí and says the present avenue dates from 1971, but that chronology should be treated as probable until checked against municipal naming files.
