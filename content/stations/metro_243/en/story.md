The Madrid Metro station called **Buenos Aires** is a place-name inherited from the street above it. The Consorcio Regional de Transportes lists the station's Line 1 correspondences and gives two accesses: Pío Felipe at Avenida de la Albufera 179 and Buenos Aires at Avenida de la Albufera 140. That second access explains the station name directly: it sits by Avenida de Buenos Aires in Puente de Vallecas.

The avenue's name points to **Buenos Aires**, the capital of Argentina. The Government of the City of Buenos Aires describes the city as Argentina's capital, on the western bank of the Río de la Plata, and records its two Spanish foundations: Pedro de Mendoza's settlement in 1536 and Juan de Garay's definitive foundation in 1580. In that history, the name Buenos Aires is tied to the old dedication to Nuestra Señora or Santa María del Buen Ayre/Buenos Ayres.

In Vallecas, however, the name is not only a modern tribute to a foreign capital. A digitized issue of *El Imparcial* from 10 March 1880 includes a notice about land for rent on **calle de Buenos Aires, Puente de Vallecas**. That makes the Buenos Aires name much older in the local landscape than the 1994 Metro station.

A local historical street gazetteer for Puente de Vallecas adds useful context: it says the 1880 calle Buenos Aires corresponded, according to a 1900 plan, to today's Calle Sierra del Cadí; it also places an **Alto de Buenos Aires** on a 1929 plan near today's Calle de Juliana Sancho and says the present Avenida de Buenos Aires dates from 1971. This is plausible and locally specific, but it should be distinguished from the directly consulted 1880 newspaper notice because the underlying 1900 and 1929 maps were not independently consulted.

The transport chronology is clearer than the street-naming chronology. The Comunidad de Madrid's page on the Portazgo-Miguel Hernández extension says the Buenos Aires station was built in mine because it was deeper and in more consistent ground, and that the section was inaugurated on **7 April 1994**. The previous wording's 2007 opening year is therefore incorrect for this station.
