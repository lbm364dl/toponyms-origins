Multiple authoritative sources support the chain of naming: the Museo del Ferrocarril directly states that Madrid-Delicias took its name from the Paseo de las Delicias and describes the older Paseo de las Delicias del Rio; the Comunidad de Madrid confirms the Metro extension to Delicias in 1949; Renfe confirms the present Cercanias station opened in 1996. The only nuance is chronology: some secondary accounts start the promenades under Fernando VI, while the strongest consulted museum panel emphasizes Carlos III's ordering of the area.
