The Cercanías station name is a shortened reference to El Pozo del Tío Raimundo, the neighbourhood it serves beside Palomeras Bajas. The best-supported explanation links the toponym to a well and to a man remembered as Raimundo, although the exact early documentary chain is not fully settled. Academic and official sources describe the neighbourhood as a marginal parceling and shanty settlement that grew especially between 1940 and 1956, before a long process of urban renewal from 1976 to 1986. The station itself was built by the Community of Madrid in collaboration with Renfe and entered service in October 1996 to connect El Pozo and Madrid Sur more directly with Atocha.
