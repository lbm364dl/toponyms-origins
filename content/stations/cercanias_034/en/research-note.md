At display level, keep the station etymology simple: El Pozo is named for El Pozo del Tío Raimundo. The richer history belongs to the neighbourhood name and should be framed with care: the “well of Raimundo” explanation is very plausible and widely repeated, but the strongest online archival-looking evidence is a local blog reproduction of cadastral material rather than a citable archive catalogue. The station opening is better dated to October 1996, with press evidence for 1 October 1996 inauguration, rather than merely “1996.”
