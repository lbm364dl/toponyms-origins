Leganés is the Cercanías C-5 station for the historic centre of the municipality of the same name. A 1580 testimony in the Relaciones Topográficas says the settlement was founded on a site with a lagoon where much légano formed, and that the name passed through a form like Leganar or Legamar before becoming Leganés. Toponomasticon Hispaniae accepts this testimony as valuable but warns that the derivation is linguistically difficult, especially because -és normally forms demonyms or adjectives of origin. Other possibilities, including a link with Liébana/lebanés or a Latin personal name Libanius, remain less certain.
