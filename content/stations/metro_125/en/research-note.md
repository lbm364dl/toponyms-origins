The best phrasing is that the station is named for Ángel Herrera Oria, probably mediated through the adjacent avenue, rather than only after the street. A notable data-cleaning issue is that one unofficial station page invents or confuses a 'José Herrera Oria'; this should not be used. Source traditions differ on how many school-chapels he founded in Málaga, commonly saying more than 200 and sometimes higher figures, so avoid a precise 250 unless tied to a specific cited source.
