Herrera Oria is named after Ángel Herrera Oria, a major figure in Spanish Catholic journalism, education, and social action in the twentieth century. The Community of Madrid explicitly identifies him as the figure who gives the station its name, and the station is placed by the avenue also dedicated to him. Herrera Oria directed El Debate, helped build the Asociación Católica de Propagandistas and CEU, later became a priest, and was appointed Bishop of Málaga in 1947 and cardinal in 1965. The station opened on 3 June 1983 as part of an initially separate northern section of Line 9b.
