Hospital de Móstoles is a straightforward service-place name: the station identifies the major public hospital it serves. CRTM lists the station as a Line 12 stop in fare zone B2 with access on Calle del Río Sella, while the Community of Madrid describes MetroSur as a major circular line linking the large southern municipalities. The hospital itself states that it was built by private initiative, later acquired by the Ministry of Health after local demands for a hospital, and inaugurated by Health Minister Ernest Lluch on 25 June 1983. The hospital's history also records its 1992 enlargement, later administrative annex, and transfer to the Community of Madrid health system after 1 January 2002. Móstoles City Council approved a 2020 motion asking the regional government and CRTM to rename both the hospital and the MetroSur stop after Ernest Lluch, but the station's official name remains Hospital de Móstoles.
