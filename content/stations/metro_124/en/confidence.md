The direct station-name chain is well supported: official transport and municipal sources place the station at Duque de Pastrana/Line 9 and document the 1983 opening, while municipal history identifies the current square as the old center of Chamartín. The weaker point is the exact dedication of the square: strong sources prove the title and Ruy Gómez de Silva's first dukedom, but not a municipal act naming the plaza specifically for him.
