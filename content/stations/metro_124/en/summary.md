Duque de Pastrana station was opened on 30 December 1983 on the Plaza de Castilla-Avenida de América section of Line 9B, later integrated into Line 9. Its immediate name source is Plaza del Duque de Pastrana in Chamartín, which the official historical Callejero records under that name from 7 April 1965. The same Callejero row preserves earlier names for the same plaza, including Plaza Mayor de Chamartín de la Rosa, Plaza de la Constitución, and Plaza de Calvo Sotelo. The ducal title was granted by Philip II on 20 December 1572 to Ruy Gómez de Silva, prince of Éboli and royal favourite, but Madrid’s local name is best read as a reference to the ducal house and its historic estates, not only to the first duke personally.
