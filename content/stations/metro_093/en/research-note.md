The direct station-name evidence is circumstantial but strong: Comunidad de Madrid's own station works page places Canal at Cea Bermúdez/Bravo Murillo and explicitly says the opening served Canal de Isabel II sports and exhibition facilities. The deeper etymology is primary-source secure because the 1851 decree itself states that the Lozoya-derived canal would be called Canal de Isabel II. One official Canal public page consulted contained an apparent typo saying the waters arrived in 1958; this was not used for that fact, which is securely 1858 in the Gaceta-linked historical material and municipal heritage page.
