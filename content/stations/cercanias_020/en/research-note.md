The best-supported etymology is not the Civil War material but the planning history: Sector III is a technical urban-development label that became a neighbourhood name, then a station name. The station's operator-facing spelling is 'Getafe Sector 3' or 'GETAFE-SECTOR 3', while the neighbourhood is commonly and officially written 'Sector III'. The phrase 'barrio de laboratorio' did not surface in the authoritative sources inspected here; if retained, it should be tied to a specific source.
