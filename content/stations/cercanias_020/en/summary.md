Getafe Sector 3 is named after Sector III, a residential neighbourhood in south-western Getafe. Unlike older place names, Sector III preserves the language of planning: a technical sector label from the General Urban Plan became the everyday name of the district. The area was urbanised mainly between 1979 and 1988 with extensive terraced housing, broad avenues, green areas and local facilities. Press reports from 1995 show that the Cercanías stop entered service on 28 May of that year to serve the Sector III neighbourhood, although the station lies on the railway corridor by the road to the municipal cemetery. The nearby Civil War bunkers are real and have been proposed for stronger heritage protection, but the more dramatic construction-rescue anecdote in the current row remains less firmly documented.
