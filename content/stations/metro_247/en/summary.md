Congosto is a place-name station: it takes its name from Calle del Congosto, where its official entrances are located, and from the surrounding Congosto area in Villa de Vallecas; the official historical Callejero records Calle and Camino del Congosto from 28 May 1971. The common noun congosto means a gorge or narrow mountain pass, and the RAE derives it from Latin coangustus. Toponomasticon Hispaniae confirms the same family of forms in Hispanic toponymy, usually linked to natural narrowings in the terrain, especially river gorges. For Madrid, the evidence securely proves the station-street-area chain, but not a specific local gorge in Villa de Vallecas that gave rise to the street name.
