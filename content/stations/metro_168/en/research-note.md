The station’s name is best treated as a transport spelling derived from the local toponym, not as the original form of the place-name. The strongest etymological chain is: stream Arroyo del Fresno -> urban area/PAU Arroyo del Fresno or Arroyo Fresno -> Metro station Arroyofresno. The official geographic WFS response also places a watercourse named Arroyo del Fresno very near the station coordinates, which is stronger evidence than the original Wikipedia-based wording.
