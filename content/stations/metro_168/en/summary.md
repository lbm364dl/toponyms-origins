Arroyofresno is a modern Metro name built from the older place-name Arroyo del Fresno. Official geographic data records Arroyo del Fresno both as a neighbourhood/place and as a natural watercourse near the station, and planning documents describe the watercourse as part of the Manzanares drainage system. The words mean roughly “ash-tree stream”: arroyo is a small stream, and fresno is an ash tree. The station was structurally built with the 1999 extension of Line 7 but stayed closed until 23 March 2019, when it opened as Arroyofresno.
