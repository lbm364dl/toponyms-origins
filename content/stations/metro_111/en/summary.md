Portazgo preserves the memory of a road toll on the historic route between Madrid and Valencia. The word portazgo means the duty paid for passing a set point on a road, and also the place where it was collected. For Vallecas, the strongest located evidence points to a toll established in 1793 at the Fuente de Ygares to fund maintenance of the road from Madrid to Vallecas. The name remained attached to the area after the toll was replaced or displaced by later fiscal controls, and Metro de Madrid used it for the Line 1 extension opened in July 1962.
