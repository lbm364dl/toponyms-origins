The station name is tied to a place-name older than the station but the current administrative barrio boundaries should not be treated as the whole explanation: the Puente de Vallecas district and its barrio structure are modern, while the road-place-name predates them. The best update is to foreground the 1793 Fuente de Ygares evidence and 19th-century Valencia-road documentation, while noting that 'portazgo' itself is an older generic fiscal term.
