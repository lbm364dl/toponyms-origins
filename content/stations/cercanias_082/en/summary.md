Ramón y Cajal station is a practical hospital stop: Renfe describes it as located next to the health centre of the same name, and Adif lists it on the M-607 by the hospital area. The hospital was inaugurated on 18 October 1977, deliberately coinciding with the 43rd anniversary of Santiago Ramón y Cajal’s death, and official hospital material states that it takes its name from him. Cajal shared the 1906 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Camillo Golgi for work on the structure of the nervous system. His drawings, preparations, manuscripts and instruments form the Cajal Legacy, now protected and partly exhibited by CSIC institutions.
