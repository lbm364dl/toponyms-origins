Noviciado station preserves the memory of the Jesuit novitiate that once occupied a large block beside Calle de San Bernardo. Archival authority records identify it as the Noviciado de San Ignacio, founded on 15 April 1602 and active until the expulsion of the Jesuits in 1767. The founder was Ana Félix de Guzmán, Marchioness of Camarasa, who placed newly acquired land at the disposal of the Society of Jesus. After later confiscations and institutional changes, the site became the historic home of Madrid's Universidad Central, whose surviving complex is still known as the Caserón de San Bernardo.
