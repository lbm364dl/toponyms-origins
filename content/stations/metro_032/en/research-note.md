A notable source discrepancy appears in some UCM-derived text, where Ana Félix de Guzmán is called 'marquesa de Camarena'; PARES, the historic guide, and multiple scholarly/heritage references support 'marquesa de Camarasa'. The station itself is not a multi-line station in the narrow platform sense, but it has an interchange connection to Plaza de España for lines 3 and 10; the previous wording line value '2' is appropriate for the station.
