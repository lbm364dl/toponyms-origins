Torre Arias is best explained as a place-name station: it is named after the Quinta de Torre Arias, the historic estate and park beside Calle de Alcalá in San Blas-Canillejas. The estate began as the Quinta de Canillejas, a country property whose first house is believed to have been built around 1580 by García Alvarado Velasco, first Count of Villamor. Its current name came much later, when María de los Dolores de Salabert Arteaga inherited the estate in 1895 and was married to Ildefonso Pérez de Guzmán el Bueno, VI Count of Torre Arias. The Metro station opened on 18 January 1980 with the Line 5 extension from Ciudad Lineal to Canillejas.
