The most important dataset issue is not the basic origin but the classification and chronology. The station name is a place-name from the adjacent Quinta/parque, while the estate’s own name is a secondary reference to the noble title of the Count of Torre Arias. The official BIC decree is stronger than Wikipedia and most tourism summaries, and it corrects the century error: the decisive inheritance was in 1895, not the late 1700s. The decree also uses cautious language for the 1580 origin and describes the municipal transfer as the 1986 agreement taking effect after the last countess’s death in 2012.
