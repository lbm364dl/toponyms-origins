The main claim is well supported: the station takes its name from the nearby Quinta de Torre Arias, whose present name derives from the Counts of Torre Arias. The row needs correction because the Torre Arias title reached the estate at the end of the 19th century, not the late 18th century, and the 1986 transfer to Madrid City Council is described in official sources as an agreement with usufruct rather than simply an immediate donation.
