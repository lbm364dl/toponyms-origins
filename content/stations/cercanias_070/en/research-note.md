The most important dataset update is not the saintly etymology, which is sound, but the distinction between linguistic origin and station-naming evidence. Official sources verify the current station and its location; onomastic sources verify San Yago/Sant Iago as part of the Santiago name family. What remains missing is a primary local source proving whether the railway halt was named directly for the saint, for a pre-existing local place called San Yago, or for another nearby feature.
