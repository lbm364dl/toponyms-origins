San Yago is a small railway halt in Galapagar, by Colonia España, whose name points to an older form of Santiago. Linguistic sources explain Santiago as a contraction of Sant Iago, from Latin sanctus and Iacobus, the name behind James/Jacob. The station therefore carries a religious name, but probably through a local place name rather than through a direct dedication of the railway facility. Official transport sources confirm the station's present name and location, while secondary railway sources note that the halt was not among the original 1861 stations of the Madrid-El Escorial line. No primary document has yet been found giving the exact date or reason for the railway naming.
