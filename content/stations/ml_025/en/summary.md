Ciudad del Cine is a descriptive station name tied to the cinema and audiovisual landscape of Ciudad de la Imagen in Pozuelo de Alarcón. The station serves the area around Kinépolis Madrid Ciudad de la Imagen, opened in 1998 with 25 screens and more than 9,200 seats. Guinness World Records recorded Kinepolis Madrid as the largest cinema complex by seats on 17 September 1998. The broader business park also includes audiovisual institutions and companies such as ECAM, Telemadrid-related facilities and Filmoteca Española conservation facilities. The station opened with Metro Ligero Oeste line ML3 on 27 July 2007.
