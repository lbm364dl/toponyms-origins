The name **Ciudad del Cine** is best understood literally: this is the light-rail stop for the cinema end of Pozuelo de Alarcón's Ciudad de la Imagen. It is not named after a person or an old village, but after a modern audiovisual landscape, with Kinépolis Madrid Ciudad de la Imagen as the most visible landmark beside the station.

Kinépolis opened its Madrid Ciudad de la Imagen complex in 1998. The company describes it as its first cinema in Spain, with 25 screens and more than 9,200 seats, and Guinness World Records lists Kinepolis Madrid as the largest cinema complex by seats on 17 September 1998. That scale explains why a station name meaning “City of Cinema” made sense for a stop serving this part of the park.

The wider place-name behind the station is **Ciudad de la Imagen**, a business and leisure complex with an audiovisual theme in Pozuelo de Alarcón. Pozuelo's municipal information describes it as a business park, while the town history notes the concentration there of audiovisual and telecommunications activity, including RTVE-related facilities, Telemadrid, production companies and the regional film school.

The area's film vocabulary is visible beyond the station name. Nearby streets and stops recall Spanish cinema figures such as José Isbert and Juan de Orduña, and ECAM is located in Ciudad de la Imagen among production, post-production and equipment companies. Filmoteca Española's conservation facilities also belong to this audiovisual geography, reinforcing the idea that the name is tied to an ecosystem rather than to one screen alone.

The most precise correction is geographical: Ciudad del Cine is a Pozuelo de Alarcón station, not a Boadilla del Monte facility, although ML3 continues west to Boadilla. No earlier station name was found; available transport sources point to the current name from the opening of Metro Ligero Oeste's ML3 on 27 July 2007. The etymology is therefore well supported as a modern descriptive place-name for the cinema/audiovisual area around Kinépolis within Ciudad de la Imagen.
