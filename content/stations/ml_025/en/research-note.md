The station-specific evidence is strongest for a practical descriptive origin: Kinépolis itself names the stop as the ML3 access to its Ciudad de la Imagen cinema, and municipal sources define the surrounding park as audiovisual. No evidence was found that 'Ciudad del Cine' was an older toponym or a separate municipality/district; it appears to be a transit/place label for the cinema-facing part of Ciudad de la Imagen. The existing wording's reference to Boadilla likely comes from the ML3 corridor or from roads toward Boadilla, not from the station's municipality.
