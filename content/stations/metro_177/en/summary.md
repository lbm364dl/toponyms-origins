San Lorenzo is a Line 4 station in Hortaleza, located under Avenida de la Barranquilla. Comunidad de Madrid states directly that the station is in the barrio from which it takes its name, and dates the Mar de Cristal-Parque de Santa María extension to 15 December 1998. The San Lorenzo name was already attached to a local residential colony or barrio: the COAM architectural guide records Colonia San Lorenzo as a Hortaleza housing development dated 1967, with works in 1968-1972. The ultimate religious reference is Saint Lawrence of Rome, the third-century deacon and martyr celebrated on 10 August, but the station name should be explained first through the local barrio.
