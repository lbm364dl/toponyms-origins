The station-to-neighbourhood naming is verified by the official Community of Madrid Line 12 page, which places Loranca station inside the Loranca urbanization, and by CRTM's 2003 report documenting MetroSur's opening. The link from the modern place name to the old Loranca despoblado is supported by the Relaciones Topograficas tradition quoted in official and academic sources. Confidence is lower only for the ultimate linguistic derivation of Loranca.
