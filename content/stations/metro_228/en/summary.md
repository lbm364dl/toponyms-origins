Loranca is a place-name station: MetroSur placed it inside the Loranca urbanization of Fuenlabrada, on Calle de la Alegria. The name is older than the modern neighbourhood. Sixteenth-century testimony in the Relaciones Topograficas says Fuenlabrada was founded by people from two nearby depopulated settlements, Loranca and Fregacedos, probably in the fourteenth century. Archaeological evidence also shows a much older rural occupation in the Loranca area, including late-Roman and Visigothic remains. The deeper linguistic origin of Loranca is not fully settled, but the best consulted specialist source derives it from a Celtic personal name or cognomen, Laurus, with the suffix -anca, rather than from the weakly sourced Arabic or tree explanations in the current row.
