Ascao station takes its name from Calle de Ascao, the street above the Line 7 stop in Ciudad Lineal. The transport authority lists the access at Calle de Ascao, 29, and Madrid's official street directory places the street between Calle del Puerto de Canfranc and Camino de Valderribas. The station name is therefore not a direct description of the railway site, but a local street-name carried onto the Metro map.

The older name behind that Madrid street points north, to Bilbao. Sources on Bilbao toponymy describe Askao or Ascao as an old place-name near the convent of Santa Cruz, associated with a stream, nearby lots and the former gardens of Ascao. It is better to treat it as a historic Bilbao toponym rather than as a modern municipality or a simple neighbourhood label.

The Basque explanation most often cited derives Askao from *aska-aho*, usually glossed as "mouth of the watering trough" or the opening of a trough. That interpretation appears in onomastic work linked to Alfonso Irigoien and is supported by Euskaltzaindia material treating Askao as a long-standing Bilbao place-name. The exact Madrid naming act has not yet been located, so the chain should be stated carefully: station -> Calle de Ascao -> probably Bilbao's Askao/Ascao.

The surrounding Madrid street pattern strengthens that interpretation. The Pueblo Nuevo/Barrio de Bilbao area includes several street names connected with Bilbao or Biscayan geography, such as Arriaga, Portugalete, Santurce, Achuri and Recalde. Ascao fits that cluster, although not every name in the area is a municipality: some, including Ascao/Askao and Achuri/Atxuri, are older Bilbao place-names.

The Metro station opened with the first section of Line 7 in July 1974, when the line began operating between Pueblo Nuevo and Las Musas. Because the station's immediate source is certain but the original municipal street-naming file has not yet been checked, the best degree of confidence is **probable**. A stronger future update would cite the Madrid council agreement that assigned the name to Calle de Ascao.
