Ascao is a Madrid station name inherited from the street above it: Calle de Ascao, officially listed in Ciudad Lineal and used as an access name by the transport authority. The street belongs to an area where several names recall Bilbao and Biscayan geography, including nearby Arriaga, Portugalete, Santurce, Achuri and Recalde. The older source is Askao or Ascao in Bilbao, described by Auñamendi as a place near the convent of Santa Cruz and connected with a stream and the old gardens of Ascao. Basque onomastic writers explain Askao as aska-aho, usually rendered 'mouth of the watering trough'. The Madrid link is probable rather than fully verified because I did not find the original municipal naming act for Calle de Ascao.
