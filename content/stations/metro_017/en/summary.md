Manuela Malasaña station on Line 12 in Móstoles bears the name of Manuela Malasaña Oñoro, a young Madrid embroiderer associated with the Dos de Mayo uprising of 1808. Later tradition turned her into a fighting heroine at the Monteleón artillery park, but archival discussion preserved by the Biblioteca Nacional de España and Madrid municipal material points to a harsher, more ordinary story: she was returning from embroidery work when French soldiers found her scissors and executed her under Murat’s order against anyone carrying objects that could be used as weapons. The station is also near the public IES Manuela Malasaña, officially listed by Móstoles at Calle Desarrollo 50, but the stronger etymology is the historical person rather than the school as an independent origin.
