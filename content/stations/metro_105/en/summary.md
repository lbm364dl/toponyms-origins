Alonso Cano station, opened on 16 October 1998 on Metro line 7, takes its name from nearby Calle de Alonso Cano. The street commemorates Alonso Cano, born in Granada in 1601 and dead there in 1667, one of the most versatile Spanish Baroque artists. The Museo del Prado describes him as a polymathic artist who practiced painting, sculpture and architecture, trained in Seville with Francisco Pacheco, and knew Diego Velázquez from that setting. His life included a documented criminal investigation after the violent death of his second wife, but reliable public interpretation should treat that episode cautiously and not let it eclipse his artistic importance.
