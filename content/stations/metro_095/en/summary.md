Puerta de Toledo station, on Line 5, is named for the Puerta de Toledo at the Glorieta de la Puerta de Toledo, where CRTM lists the station access. The monument is the last monumental city gate built in Madrid and was completed in 1827. Its immediate predecessor was an unbuilt triumphal arch commissioned by Joseph Bonaparte in 1811-1812 from Silvestre Perez. After the French left Madrid, the city council commissioned Antonio Lopez Aguado to design the present gate in 1813; its dedication shifted from National Sovereignty and the Constitution of Cadiz to the restored King Ferdinand VII. The existing explanation is verified, but it should be tightened to distinguish the unbuilt Bonapartist project from the current Aguado monument.
