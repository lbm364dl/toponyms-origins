The dataset's broad story is sound, but the best municipal heritage source gives a more precise sequence: Joseph Bonaparte's project was a direct predecessor by Silvestre Perez, not the present gate; the present gate was commissioned in 1813 to Antonio Lopez Aguado, initially connected with National Sovereignty after the Constitution of Cadiz, and later rededicated under Ferdinand VII. A secondary municipal building-record page/PDF repeats some useful catalogue facts but contains an obvious '1927' typo, while the fuller municipal monument page, the same PDF metadata and tourism page all support 1827.
