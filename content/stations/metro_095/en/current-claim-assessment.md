The row is substantially correct: the station is named for the nearby Puerta de Toledo and the present monument was completed/inaugurated in 1827. The main nuance is that Joseph Bonaparte's unbuilt predecessor was a Silvestre Perez project from 1811-1812; the current Antonio Lopez Aguado gate was commissioned by Madrid's city council in 1813 and passed through liberal, absolutist and restored-Fernandine meanings before completion.
