Colonia de los Ángeles is a place-name station: it is named after the Pozuelo de Alarcón residential colony beside the M-502. Municipal history records that the Colonia de los Ángeles was founded and parcelled by its owner in 1926, and that the Sociedad Colonia de los Ángeles was created three years later. Its story belongs to the suburban and summer-residence growth that followed Pozuelo's rail connection with Madrid in 1861. The religious layer of the name is visible today in the local feast of Nuestra Señora de los Ángeles, celebrated by colonos from 1942, but I found no primary source proving why the 1926 colony first received that name.
