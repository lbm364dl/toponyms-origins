The core claim is right: the station name refers to the historic village and former municipality of Fuencarral. The detailed literal etymology is weaker: older evidence supports a name built around a fountain used by cart-drivers, while an academic discussion prefers an older form Foncarral from fonte plus carra, probably meaning a stony road or place, not simply 'cart road'. The annexation date also needs nuance: the decree was dated 10 November 1950 and published on 13 December 1950, but the effective incorporation is often given as 20 October 1951.
