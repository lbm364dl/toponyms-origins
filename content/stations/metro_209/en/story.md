Fuencarral station takes its name from Fuencarral itself: the historic village and former municipality that occupied this northern edge of Madrid before becoming part of the capital. The Metro stop opened with the northern metro corridor of 1982, and the Cercanias station also preserves the same local place name in the rail network.

The traditional explanation of the name is old and local. In the sixteenth-century Relaciones Topograficas ordered under Philip II, Fuencarral residents reportedly said the village was so called because there was a fountain where cart-drivers watered their animals. That makes the first element, fuen- or fuente, secure as a reference to a spring or fountain.

The second element is more difficult. Popular accounts often turn carral into a cart road, producing a neat meaning such as 'spring on the cart road'. A more cautious academic discussion notes the older form Foncarral and connects carral with carra, perhaps a 'stony road' or a pre-Roman root related to stone. The safest public explanation is therefore not a single literal translation, but a place name built around a fountain and an old road landscape.

Fuencarral was not originally just a district label. It was a separate municipality with its own council records, rural land, roads toward northern Madrid, and settlements such as El Goloso, Valdeconejos, Manoteras, Llano Castellano and Valdelatas. The BOE decree approving its annexation to Madrid is dated 10 November 1950 and was published on 13 December 1950; other municipal and historical sources record the effective incorporation as 20 October 1951.

The name also has a transport history. The present line 10 section was first the old line 8 between Fuencarral and Nuevos Ministerios, opened for the northern corridor and later absorbed into line 10 when lines 8 and 10 were connected in 1998. The evidence is strong that the station is named after the former village; the remaining uncertainty lies in the exact linguistic value of carral and in keeping decree, publication and effective annexation dates distinct.
