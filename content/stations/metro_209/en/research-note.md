The strongest station-specific conclusion is simple: Fuencarral is a place-name inherited from the old municipality. The previous wording's attractive 'fuente + carral = spring on the cart road' should be toned down because the sources support both a traditional cart-driver fountain story and a more technical toponymic reading of carral as possibly 'stony road/place'. The annexation wording should also avoid implying that 10 November 1950 was the only annexation date; it is the decree date, while BOE publication and effective incorporation have separate dates.
