The current explanation's 'verified' confidence is justified for the station being named after the municipality and for the basic roza etymology. The strongest improvement is not a different origin but a more careful formulation: roza is lexically clearing or breaking land for cultivation; burning may occur in some agricultural systems, but the station wording should not make slash-and-burn the defining proof. The 1861 railway date is useful for station history because municipal material says Las Rozas was among the initial stations of the first Northern Railway section, but no separate station naming decree was found.
