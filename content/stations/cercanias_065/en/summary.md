Las Rozas station takes its name from the municipality it serves. The place-name is usually explained from Castilian roza, a piece of land cleared of brush and prepared for cultivation. Municipal material and lexicographic evidence support the agricultural reading, while older forms such as Roças and Rozas show the name's long documentary life. The 16th-century growth story is plausible as local historical context, but it should be presented separately from the etymology itself.
