Parque Europa is a Line 12 MetroSur station in Fuenlabrada, with its access on Calle de Francia. Its name comes from the nearby Parque Europa, also appearing in some local and official contexts as Parque de Europa. The surrounding map makes the European theme very visible: the park sits among streets named after countries and cities such as Francia, Bélgica, Italia, Portugal, Holanda, Suiza and Viena. The station opened with MetroSur in 2003, while the park is described in local route material as a 17,454-square-metre green space inaugurated on 4 May 1983.
