Canillas station opened on 27 April 1998 as part of the Line 4 extension from Esperanza to Mar de Cristal. Memoria de Madrid states directly that the station sits in the barrio of Canillas and receives its name from that barrio. The barrio preserves the name of the former village and municipality of Canillas, documented as an aldea in 1252 and incorporated into Madrid by a 1949 annexation decree that took effect on 30 March 1950. Official heritage material describes medieval origins, a small agrarian population in the sixteenth century, and later transformation into an urban district. The station-name origin is verified; the deeper linguistic derivation of Canillas should be treated as uncertain.
