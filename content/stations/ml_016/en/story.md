Pozuelo Oeste is a straightforward modern place name. It marks a Metro Ligero Oeste stop on line ML2 in Pozuelo de Alarcón, located by the Carretera de Carabanchel, the M-502, opposite the fire station and the 112 emergency facilities. The word Oeste distinguishes this stop from the wider municipality and from other Pozuelo transport references.

The deeper origin lies in Pozuelo de Alarcón itself. Toponomasticon Hispaniae explains Pozuelo as the Castilian diminutive of pozo, 'well', using the older suffix -uelo. That fits the local historical memory: the town council and regional heritage documents connect the name with the area's wells, springs and water sources.

The place is documented in the medieval frontier geography of Madrid. The town council cites 1208, when Alfonso VIII fixed boundaries between Segovia, Toledo, Madrid and Alamín, and the name appears in the formula 'et Pozolos remanet de parte de Madrid'. The settlement later belonged to the alfoz of Madrid, within the sexmo of Aravaca, which explains the earlier name Pozuelo de Aravaca.

The second half of the name came from a family and a lordship. In 1632 the royal jurisdiction was sold into seigneurial hands connected with the Ocaña y Alarcón family, and the municipality's name shifted from Pozuelo de Aravaca toward Pozuelo de Alarcón. Sources differ in how they present the buyer: the town council and Toponomasticon name Gabriel de Ocaña y Alarcón, while a 2024 regional heritage declaration says the sale was to Luis de Ocaña y Alarcón, who made it a mayorazgo for his first-born son Gabriel.

The station belongs to the 2007 light-rail expansion that linked Madrid with Pozuelo and Aravaca. Official transport sources list Pozuelo Oeste among the 13 stops of ML2, and the Community of Madrid describes this particular stop as part of the section near La Finca, green areas and emergency-service facilities. The station name is therefore well supported as a local, directional place name, while the exact administrative act that chose the station name was not found.
