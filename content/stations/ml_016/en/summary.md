The station name is modern and geographic: Pozuelo Oeste identifies a Metro Ligero Oeste stop in Pozuelo de Alarcón, beside the M-502 and near the fire station and 112 emergency facilities. Its deeper name story belongs to the municipality. The town name Pozuelo is best understood as a Castilian diminutive of pozo, connected with the local abundance of wells, springs and water systems. The medieval settlement was known as Pozuelo de Aravaca because it belonged to the sexmo of Aravaca, and the Alarcón element was attached after the 1632 sale of the lordship to the Ocaña y Alarcón family.
