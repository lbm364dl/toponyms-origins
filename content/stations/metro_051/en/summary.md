Francos Rodríguez is named after the street by the same name, which commemorates José Francos Rodríguez (Madrid, 1862-1931). Francos studied medicine but became best known as a journalist, writer and politician, directing newspapers such as El Globo and Heraldo de Madrid. He served as mayor of Madrid from 1910 to 1912, later held ministerial office, and entered the Real Academia Española in 1924. The station opened on 12 February 1999 as part of the Line 7 extension from Canal to Valdezarza.
