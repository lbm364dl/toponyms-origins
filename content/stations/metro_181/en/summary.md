The Metro station is a functional place-name: it identifies the Hospital Universitario del Henares, the public hospital beside the access on Camino Labor in Coslada. The hospital opened its doors on 11 February 2008 to serve Coslada, San Fernando de Henares, Mejorada del Campo, Loeches and Velilla de San Antonio. The deeper toponym is Henares, the river and corridor east of Madrid. Toponomasticon Hispaniae explains Henares as the plural of henar, a Castilian word for a place covered with hay, from Latin fenum through a Romance collective formation. The existing wording should keep the hospital origin but tighten the opening chronology and treat the Arabic Guadalajara note as background rather than the direct origin of this station name.
