The strongest source for the etymology is Renfe's own location text, which directly connects the name to the nearby natural environment. Chronology is less clean: CRTM's historical map gives 1961 for Pinar de las Rozas as a support station, railway chronologies often cite the 1964 Chamartin/Pinar works, and 1989 is specifically tied to the completion of the Triangulo de Pinar and El Tejar/Cercanias reconfiguration. keep the etymology as verified but downgrade or rewrite any claim that the station itself 'dates from 1989' unless a more precise source proves a public passenger reopening/remodelling that year.
