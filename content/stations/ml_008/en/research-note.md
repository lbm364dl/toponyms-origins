Several non-primary transport pages and even some official or semi-official materials vary between Palas de Rey and Palas del Rey; CRTM currently uses Palas de Rey for the station and access, while the Madrid municipal sports page title says Palas del Rey but its address says Calle Palas de Rey. The previous wording also says the Galician town is about 65 km from the cathedral; CVC gives 63 km for the Palas de Rei-Santiago stage, so a rounded distance is acceptable but should not be central to the etymology.
