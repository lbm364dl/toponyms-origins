The station name comes from Campamento, a Madrid place-name rooted in the Campamento Militar de Carabanchel. Municipal planning documents state that the Carabanchel military camp had formed in the nineteenth century and that the area belonged to Carabanchel Alto until its incorporation into Madrid in the mid-twentieth century. Local-history accounts add that houses, taverns and services grew around the barracks, and that by 1885 the mayor of Carabanchel Alto named a local administrator for the barrio de Campamento. The modern metro stop therefore preserves a military landscape name rather than commemorating a person.
