The main claim is well supported: the station name refers to the Campamento area, whose name comes from the Campamento Militar de Carabanchel and the wider military use of these former Carabanchel Alto lands. Some detailed dates in the row, especially the 1856 manoeuvre claim and the 1885 municipal appointment, are plausible but rest on secondary local-history sources rather than a primary archival document inspected here.
