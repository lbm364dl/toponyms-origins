The best-supported public wording is that Campamento station preserves the name of the Campamento Militar de Carabanchel and the surrounding Campamento area. There is a minor geographic nuance: some secondary station descriptions place the station itself in Aluche, close to the boundary with Campamento, while the name points to the adjacent historic military toponym. No inspected primary source for the 1885 regidor appointment was found, so that detail should remain qualified.
