The station name comes from Calle de Núñez de Balboa, one of the main north-south streets of Madrid's Salamanca district. The street commemorates Vasco Núñez de Balboa, born in Jerez de los Caballeros around 1475 and executed in Acla, Panama, in January 1519. Balboa is remembered for crossing the Isthmus of Panama and reaching the sea then called the Mar del Sur in September 1513. The Metro stop first opened with Line 5 in 1970; Line 9 was added in 1986, creating the long interchange that still characterizes the station.
