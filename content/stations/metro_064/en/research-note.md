The strongest direct toponymic chain is: CRTM station access and name -> Calle de Núñez de Balboa in the official Madrid street register -> Madrid City Council commemorative plaque identifying Vasco Núñez de Balboa. The Ayuntamiento's 2013 note itself repeats the common formula that Balboa was the first European to sight the Pacific from its eastern coast, while some modern web summaries introduce a companion-first nuance; this should be treated as historical nuance rather than a reason to downgrade the station-name origin.
