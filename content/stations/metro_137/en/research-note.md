The claim is stronger as a two-step etymology: station -> San Blas area -> probably Blas Pérez González. Treating the station itself as directly named after the politician overstates the evidence. There is also a date issue in transport sources: the Community of Madrid page says Line 7 entered service on 22 July 1974, while some rail chronologies list 17 July 1974; this does not affect the name origin but should be normalized separately if exact opening dates are maintained.
