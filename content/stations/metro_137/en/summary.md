San Blas station was opened on Line 7 in 1974 and was named for the surrounding San Blas district and neighbourhood area in eastern Madrid. That urban name belongs to a large social-housing operation begun in the late 1950s around the former municipality of Canillejas, after its annexation to Madrid. Several local histories say the name San Blas was chosen as a tribute to Blas Pérez González, Franco's Minister of Gobernación from 1942 to 1957, by adding the saintly prefix to his given name. The stronger documentary sources verify the housing project, the district context, and Pérez González's biography, but they do not yet provide a primary naming decree proving the tribute. The safest public wording is therefore: named for the San Blas area, with a probable but not fully documented political eponym behind the neighbourhood name.
