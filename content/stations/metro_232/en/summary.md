Parque de los Estados is a descriptive station name: it points to the nearby public park of the same name in Fuenlabrada. Official transport sources place the station at the confluence of Calle Venezuela and Calle Habana, with current accesses also on Avenida de los Estados. The park is listed by Fuenlabrada City Council among the municipality's large public green spaces, and a local green-route description gives it 16,730 square metres, created in 1980 and later expanded. The surrounding street names explain the theme: Avenida de los Estados anchors an urban area with names such as Venezuela, La Habana, Lima, Bolivia, Brasil, Caracas and other American places. La Pollina is a nearby municipal green and environmental space, but I did not find direct evidence that it was the former name of this station site.
