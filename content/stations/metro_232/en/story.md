The name **Parque de los Estados** is literal and local. MetroSur gave the station the name of the nearby Parque de los Estados, a public green space in Fuenlabrada, rather than the name of a person, event or old village. The Community of Madrid's MetroSur page locates the station at the meeting of Calle Venezuela and Calle Habana, and the regional transport consortium lists station accesses on Avenida de los Estados, Calle de La Habana and Calle de Venezuela.

The park name belongs to a wider urban naming pattern. Around the station, Fuenlabrada's street map gathers names from the Americas: Venezuela, La Habana, Lima, Bolivia, Brasil, Caracas, Ecuador, Peru, Uruguay, Puerto Rico and the Dominican Republic appear in the same sector. In that context, Avenida de los Estados is not an isolated street name but the spine of a themed neighborhood landscape.

The park itself predates MetroSur. A local green-route description records Parque de los Estados as a 16,730-square-metre park created in 1980 and enlarged in later years, at the confluence of Avenida de los Estados and Calle Venezuela. Fuenlabrada City Council's statistical yearbook confirms that Parque de los Estados is one of the municipality's large parks, over 10,000 square metres.

The station opened with Line 12, MetroSur, on 11 April 2003. MetroSur was built as a circular line for the southern metropolitan municipalities, linking Alcorcon, Mostoles, Fuenlabrada, Getafe and Leganes and connecting them back to the Madrid Metro and Cercanias networks. In Fuenlabrada, Parque de los Estados became one of the five original MetroSur stations serving the city.

One detail in the existing dataset should be treated carefully: La Pollina is indeed a nearby municipal green and environmental space, documented by the city council, but I did not find a direct source proving that the station area was formerly called La Pollina. The strongest public explanation is therefore simpler: the station is named for the park, and the park name reflects the American-place naming theme of the surrounding streets.
