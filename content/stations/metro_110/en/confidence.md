The immediate station etymology is well supported: official transport and archive sources identify the Metro station and its 1962 opening, while archival newspapers and the provincial bulletin document Nueva Numancia as a Vallecas place name from the 1860s and in the 1873 segregation petition. Confidence is not marked verified because no primary naming act has been found explaining why the 1860s barrio was called Nueva Numancia, and a local-history source explicitly raises an alternative possible influence from the frigate Numancia.
