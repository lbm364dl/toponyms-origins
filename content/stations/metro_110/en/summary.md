Nueva Numancia is best explained first as a local place name: the Metro station opened on 2 July 1962 beside Avenida de la Albufera and revived the name of an older Puente de Vallecas neighbourhood. Archival notices show the barrio of Nueva Numancia in use by 1866, before Cayetano Agustí y Giner’s 1873 attempt to have the hamlet separated from Vallecas as a municipality. That petition was denied in September 1873 because the settlement did not meet the required population threshold. The deeper reference is probably ancient Numancia in Soria, conquered by Scipio in 133 BC and later turned into a Spanish symbol of tenacious resistance. The exact motive for choosing the name in the 1860s is not fully proven, and one local historian has suggested the contemporary frigate Numancia as another possible influence.
