Cotos is the terminal station of line C-9 at the Puerto de Cotos, a high mountain pass between the Valsain and Lozoya valleys. The place-name is best explained by the stone boundary markers known as cotos that marked the limits of the Reales Pinares de Valsain. A Community of Madrid heritage catalogue states that two eighteenth-century stone markers at the pass gave their name to the Puerto de Cotos, previously known as Puerto del Paular. Carlos III's purchase of the Valsain woods in 1761 and their demarcation with boundary stones provide the historical context for the name. The station itself dates from the 1964 extension of the Guadarrama electric railway from Puerto de Navacerrada to Cotos.
