Alsacia is a place-name station: it was opened on 16 March 2011 as part of the Line 2 extension from La Elipa to Las Rosas and was named for Plaza de Alsacia. Official Madrid and regional sources place the new station and intermodal area at that square. The square’s name refers to Alsace, the historic region on the upper Rhine, today in eastern France, long shaped by both French and German political and cultural worlds. The common explanation that Alsace passed between France and Germany in 1871, 1919, 1940 and 1945 is broadly right, though 1648 should be described more carefully because French control developed in stages after the Peace of Westphalia.
