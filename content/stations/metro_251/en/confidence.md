The station’s direct name source is well supported by official transport and municipal documents placing Alsacia station at Plaza de Alsacia and recording its opening on 16 March 2011; the official Callejero dates the plaza name to 23 February 1996. Confidence is marked probable rather than fully verified for the ultimate plaza-name motive because the available sources did not include the original municipal naming decree or an official explanation of why the square was named Alsacia.
