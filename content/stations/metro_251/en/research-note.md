The station-name evidence is stronger than the plaza-name evidence. Official sources confirm the station was built and opened at Plaza de Alsacia, and the official Callejero records Plaza de Alsacia from 23 February 1996, but the available sources did not include the municipal naming rationale for the plaza. The existing wording's European-reconciliation narrative is historically meaningful, but it should be separated from the documented naming chain: station -> Plaza de Alsacia -> Alsace.
