Puerta del Sur is best understood as a descriptive transport name, not as a commemorative or older place-name. It marks the point in Alcorcón where Line 10 meets Line 12, MetroSur, the circular line built to connect Alcorcón, Móstoles, Fuenlabrada, Getafe and Leganés. Official and technical sources describe the station as the link or interchange between Madrid Metro and MetroSur. The station opened with the MetroSur project on 11 April 2003. I found no reliable evidence that the station was named after a commercial centre.
