The station name refers to Infante Don Luis, Luis Antonio Jaime de Borbón y Farnesio (1727-1785), son of Felipe V and Isabel de Farnesio and brother of Carlos III. In 1761 he bought the lordship of Boadilla del Monte from the Marquise of Mirabal and developed a major residence there. The Palacio del Infante Don Luis, associated with Ventura Rodríguez and completed in the 1760s, fixed his name in the local landscape. The ML3 stop opened with Metro Ligero Oeste service on 27 July 2007 and serves the modern area where that historic name survives in local toponymy.
