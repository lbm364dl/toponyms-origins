Móstoles Central is a place-name station: it identifies the central interchange of Móstoles, where MetroSur Line 12 connects with Cercanías C-5. MetroSur entered service on 11 April 2003, and municipal transport information lists Móstoles Central among the municipality's Line 12 stations. The town name is much older than the station: a secure medieval reference from October 1144 mentions turrem de Monsteles. A respected toponymic explanation links Móstoles to Latin monasterium, perhaps a locative plural *monisteriis, suggesting a Christian monastery or basilica before Islamic rule, but this remains a hypothesis rather than a settled fact.
