The station is named after the street rather than directly by a royal dedication plaque found at the stop. There is a possible ambiguity in the name 'Mary Tudor' because Henry VIII's sister Mary Tudor (1496-1533), queen consort of France, also bore that name; however, Madrid public transport context and Spanish historical usage overwhelmingly identify 'María Tudor' here with Mary I of England, especially because of her marriage to Philip II and her presence in Spanish royal art collections.
