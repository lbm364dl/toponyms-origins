María Tudor is a Metro Ligero ML1 station whose name comes from Calle de María Tudor, beside the stop in Sanchinarro. The name is best understood as a reference to Mary I of England (1516-1558), also known as Mary Tudor, daughter of Henry VIII and Catherine of Aragon. Her Spanish connection is unusually direct: Catherine was a daughter of the Catholic Monarchs, and Mary married the future Philip II in 1554. The marriage linked Tudor England and Habsburg Spain for a short, politically sensitive period, although English arrangements limited Philip's independent authority. The station opened with ML1 on 24 May 2007.
