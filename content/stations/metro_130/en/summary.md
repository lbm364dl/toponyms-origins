Suanzes is named for Juan Antonio Suanzes Fernández, a Ferrol-born naval engineer who became one of the central figures in Spain's state-led industrial policy under Franco. He was minister of Industry and Trade in 1938-1939 and again in 1945-1951, and he presided over the Instituto Nacional de Industria from its foundation in 1941 until 1963. Franco granted him the title Marqués de Suanzes by decree in 1960, while Suanzes was still alive. The Metro station opened on 18 January 1980 as part of the Line 5 extension from Ciudad Lineal to Canillejas. The name has been politically contested because of Suanzes's role in the Franco regime.
