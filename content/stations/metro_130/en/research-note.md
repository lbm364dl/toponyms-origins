The strongest correction is chronological: Suanzes died in 1977, while the marquisate was created in 1960. The 2017 district minutes contain spelling inconsistency, using both Suanzes and Suances, but the station and BOE title use Suanzes. The opening chronology also shows a small source discrepancy: some transport chronologies list the inauguration on 17 January 1980, while memoriademadrid and contemporary press distinguish official inauguration on 17 January from public use/opening on 18 January; for a public station opening date, 18 January 1980 is preferable.
