Pío XII is best explained as a street-derived Metro name: the station lies at Avenida de Pío XII, and CRTM lists both station accesses on that avenue. The older commemorative name belongs to the avenue, which is recorded in Wikidata as named after Pope Pius XII, Eugenio Pacelli. Pacelli was pope from 1939 to 1958, a pontificate marked by World War II, the Holocaust, postwar reconstruction and the Cold War. The station itself entered service on 30 December 1983, when the Plaza de Castilla-Avenida de América section of Line 9 opened. The Franco-era Catholic context is relevant, especially given the 1953 Concordat between Spain and the Holy See, but a specific municipal naming act for the avenue was not found in this search.
