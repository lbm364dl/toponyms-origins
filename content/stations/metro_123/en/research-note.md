The strongest evidence chain is not a single municipal etymology note but a convergence of station geography, transport chronology and structured street data. The avenue's name was in use by the end of 1950, during Pius XII's lifetime and before the 1953 Concordat; therefore the avenue was not a posthumous dedication. This makes the Franco-era Catholic context plausible and useful, but the exact motive and approval date for the avenue should remain open until the Ayuntamiento street-naming file or period Callejero can be checked.
