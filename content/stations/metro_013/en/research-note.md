The station's public etymology should avoid implying that 'Príncipe de Vergara' was a personal name or that the title was granted immediately in 1839. The strongest wording is that the station is named for the street, the street honors Espartero through a title granted in 1872, and the title commemorates his role in the 1839 Vergara settlement. One low-quality web source wrongly attributes the name to Juan Prim; this is unsupported and contradicted by the official title decree and municipal street history.
