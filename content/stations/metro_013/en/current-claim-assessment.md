The core claim is correct: the station takes its name from Calle del Príncipe de Vergara, a street honoring Baldomero Espartero through his princely title. The chronology needs correction: Madrid's official street-history dataset gives the General Mola period as 12 December 1940 to 25 January 1980, not 1939-1981, and press evidence says the Metro station name changed back effective 27 June 1983.
