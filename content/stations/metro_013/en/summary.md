The station is named after Calle del Príncipe de Vergara, at its junction with Calle de Alcalá. The street honors Joaquín Baldomero Fernández-Espartero Álvarez de Toro, the liberal general and later regent of Spain who was granted the title Prince of Vergara by King Amadeo I on 2 January 1872. The title refers to Espartero's role in the 1839 settlement with the Carlist general Rafael Maroto: the convention was signed at Oñate on 29 August and took effect at Vergara on 31 August in the episode remembered as the Abrazo de Vergara. The street already bore this name from 21 July 1880, was renamed General Mola from 12 December 1940, and officially recovered Príncipe de Vergara on 25 January 1980; the Metro station followed in June 1983.
