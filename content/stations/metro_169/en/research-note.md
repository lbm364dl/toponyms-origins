The best public wording is that the station was named from the local Lacoma area/Colonia Lacoma, which in turn preserves Margarita Gonzalez Lacoma's surname. This avoids overstating a formal station dedication while still recognizing the woman behind the place-name. A large PDF titled 'Propuestas de rehabilitacion y regeneracion de los espacios abiertos interbloque' has source details saying Colonia Lacoma began construction in 1949 and was promoted by Marcudos S.L.; it was too large for full consultation, but it is worth checking for future source strengthening.
