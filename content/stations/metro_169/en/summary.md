Lacoma is named for the local place-name created by the Colonia Lacoma, a workers' housing development associated with Margarita Gonzalez Lacoma. Lacoma first became known in Madrid through Casa Lacoma, a fashion salon that opened on Gran Via in 1925. After the Civil War, her business interests moved into housing through Marcudos, formed with Pilar Cudos Velasco, and the Lacoma development became one of the early urban nuclei of Peñagrande. The Metro station inherited that neighbourhood name when the Valdezarza-Pitis extension of Line 7 opened on 29 March 1999. The exact details of Margarita's origins remain less securely documented than the link between her surname, the colony, and the station.
