Urgel is best explained as a street-derived station name: the Metro stop was opened on Line 5 in 1968 near Calle de Urgel, an official Madrid street in Carabanchel. Urgel is the Spanish form of Catalan Urgell, a place-name with medieval, ecclesiastical, and regional meanings in the Lleida area. The strongest historical context is the County of Urgell, a Carolingian-era county that developed its own ruling line and was later absorbed into the Crown after the defeat of Jaume II 'el Dissortat' in 1413. For display, the safer wording is that the station is named after Calle de Urgel, whose name refers to Urgell, rather than asserting an officially documented dedication to the medieval county alone.
