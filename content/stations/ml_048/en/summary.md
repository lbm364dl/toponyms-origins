Tierra is a dual Tranvia de Parla stop, with platforms listed by CRTM as Tierra Norte and Tierra Sur. Its name is best explained by its setting beside Calle Planeta Tierra, the official municipal street name in Parla Este. The neighborhood's street pattern uses a broad astronomical vocabulary: Avenida de los Planetas, Avenida de las Estrellas, Avenida del Sistema Solar, Avenida de las Galaxias, planet streets, star streets and constellation streets. The stop opened with the Parla Este phase of the tram in September 2007, linking the new residential development to the rest of Parla. The existing etymology is therefore sound, but it should use the full street name and avoid presenting the 'third orbital position' idea as documented fact.
