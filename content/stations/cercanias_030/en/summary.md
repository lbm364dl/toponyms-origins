Alcobendas-San Sebastián de los Reyes is a compound station name: it identifies the shared Cercanías stop for two neighbouring municipalities north of Madrid. Renfe places the station on Avenida de España and notes that Alcobendas and San Sebastián de los Reyes form a continuous urban area divided there by municipal boundaries. The second half of the name is well explained: San Sebastián de los Reyes grew around an existing hermitage of Saint Sebastian and under the legal protection of the Catholic Monarchs in 1492. The first half, Alcobendas, is much older in the record, appearing as Alcovendas in 1208, but its linguistic origin is unresolved; the strongest current source recommends caution rather than a firm translation.
