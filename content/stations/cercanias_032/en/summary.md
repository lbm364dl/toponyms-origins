Colmenar Viejo station is named for the Madrid municipality in which it stands. Toponomasticon Hispaniae analyses Colmenar as a collective or locative formation from colmena, meaning a place where beehives are found, and Viejo as the transparent Spanish adjective from Latin vetulus. A 16th-century local explanation in the Relaciones Topográficas says the settlement grew near the beehives and house of an old man on the Alcalá-to-Segovia road, but modern toponymic commentary treats that as possibly popular etymology. The safest public explanation is therefore 'old apiary/place of beehives', with the beekeeper story presented as tradition, not as proven origin.
