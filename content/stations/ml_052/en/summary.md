Polígono Industrial Ciudad de Parla is a descriptive place-name: the station identifies the industrial estate beside it. CRTM lists the stop as a surface station at Calle Berlín, 52, in fare zone B2. The name reflects Parla's need to connect residential districts, commercial areas and local employment zones after decades of rapid demographic growth. The stop entered service on 8 September 2007, when the tram was extended from Parque Parla Este to Polígono Industrial Ciudad de Parla; the circular route was completed in May 2008.
