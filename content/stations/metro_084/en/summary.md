Montecarmelo is a Line 10 station created with MetroNorte to serve the northern residential developments of Madrid. Its name comes from the Montecarmelo urban area, documented in municipal planning as UZI.0.07 Montecarmelo and served by the 2007 MetroNorte extension. The deeper origin is best described as a local place name, Monte Carmelo, probably connected with a stream and with the religious vocabulary of Mount Carmel. Nearby, the Sanctuary of Nuestra Señora de Valverde provides important historical context, but it does not by itself prove the station’s name. The official Callejero confirms the PAU-era street chronology: Avenida del Montecarmelo is recorded from 27 July 2000, before the 2007 Metro station.
