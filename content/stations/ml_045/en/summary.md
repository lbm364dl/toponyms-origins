Isabel II is a surface stop on the Parla tram, listed by the Madrid Regional Transport Consortium at Calle Reyes Católicos, 73. Its name most plausibly comes from the nearby Calle Isabel II, part of a local group of streets with royal names such as Reyes Católicos, Alfonso X, Fernando III and Felipe II. The person behind the name is Isabel II of Spain, born in Madrid in 1830 and queen from 1833 to 1868. A direct municipal naming file has not been found, so the station-to-street link is probable rather than fully documented. The railway story in the existing wording is historically relevant to her reign, but it should be treated as background rather than the reason for the stop name.
