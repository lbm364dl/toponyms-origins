Hospital de Fuenlabrada is a straightforward service name: the Line 12 station identifies the public hospital it serves. The station was part of MetroSur, put into service on 11 April 2003, and is located on Camino del Molino with accesses marked for the hospital and the Rey Juan Carlos University campus. The hospital itself was created as a public entity in December 2002, finished construction in 2003, began outpatient activity in January 2004, and opened fully in June 2004. Its creation answered a long-standing healthcare gap in Fuenlabrada, whose residents had previously been served by Hospital Severo Ochoa in Leganés.
