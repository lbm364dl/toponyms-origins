Colombia is best explained as a street-derived station name: the stop is at Príncipe de Vergara by Calle de Colombia, and its accesses include Calle de Colombia itself. The street belongs to the Hispanoamerica toponymic landscape of Chamartin, where names such as Chile, Puerto Rico, Nicaragua, Costa Rica, República Dominicana, Ecuador, Lima and Cuzco appear nearby. The underlying referent is the Republic of Colombia, the South American country. The station first opened on 30 December 1983 as part of Line 9b between Plaza de Castilla and Avenida de América. Its present interchange role dates from 21 May 2002, when Line 8 was extended between Nuevos Ministerios and Mar de Cristal and Colombia was rebuilt as part of that connection.
