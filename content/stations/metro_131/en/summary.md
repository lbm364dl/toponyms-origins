The station name is a place-name, not a commemorative name for a person directly. It refers to Ciudad Lineal, the Madrid area shaped by Arturo Soria y Mata's late nineteenth-century plan for a city organized along a transport axis. Soria published his Proyecto de Ciudad Lineal alrededor de Madrid in 1892, and the Compañía Madrileña de Urbanización was created in 1894 to build it. The plan imagined a long, narrow city structured by public transport, houses with gardens, hygiene, greenery, and social mixing. The station opened on 28 May 1964, originally on the Ventas-Ciudad Lineal extension then operated as part of Line 2 and later incorporated into Line 5.
