The station etymology is best stored as a place-name derived from an urban project. For human review, the dataset should avoid making the current district the sole naming source, because Ciudad Lineal functioned as an urban and transport toponym before the modern district framing. The station's local node also has a memory-history layer: the plaza was long associated with Cruz de los Caídos/La Cruz, but this is contextual to the location rather than a former station name.
