The available sources provide good evidence for the immediate naming chain station -> Avenida de Abrantes / Abrantes neighbourhood, and good evidence that the Palacio de Abrantes was named through the dukes. No authoritative municipal record tying Avenida de Abrantes in Carabanchel to the Dukes of Abrantes was found; because the neighbouring street names are Portuguese toponyms, the place-name classification is the more defensible dataset update.
