Abrantes is a Line 11 station opened on 16 November 1998, built on the first Plaza Elíptica-Pan Bendito section. Its immediate name source is Avenida de Abrantes, recorded in the official historical Callejero from 8 January 1954 and also giving identity to the Abrantes neighbourhood. The best-supported ultimate explanation is a place-name one: Abrantes is a Portuguese town and municipality, and nearby streets include other Portuguese toponyms such as Oporto, Portalegre, Elvas, Cintra, Viseo and Estoril. The Dukes of Abrantes and Madrid's Palacio de Abrantes are historically real, but no direct source proving that the Carabanchel avenue or the Metro station was named to honour the noble title.
