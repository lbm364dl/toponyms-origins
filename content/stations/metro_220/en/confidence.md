The immediate source of the station name is well supported by transport and municipal sources: the station was opened on Line 11 in 1998 and is tied physically to Avenida de Abrantes, recorded officially from 1954, and the Abrantes neighbourhood. The ultimate explanation as a Portuguese place-name is probable because of the local cluster of Portuguese toponyms, but the available sources did not include a definitive municipal naming record for Avenida de Abrantes.
