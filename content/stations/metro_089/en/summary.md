The station name comes directly from the Banco de España headquarters beside its entrances on Calle de Alcalá and Plaza de Cibeles. The bank’s current name dates from the Law of 28 January 1856, and its Cibeles headquarters was inaugurated on 3 March 1891 after a project by Eduardo de Adaro and Severiano Sainz de la Lastra. Metro de Madrid’s historical sheet records that the station opened on the inaugural Sol-Ventas section of Line 2 on 14 June 1924. It also documents that an original project name, Castelar, was changed during construction to Banco, tying the stop to the institution rather than to the old name of the square.
