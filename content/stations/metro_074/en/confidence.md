The station being named after Vicálvaro as a place is verified by its location, the line 9 extension documentation and municipal district history. Confidence is only probable for the deeper etymology because official Madrid material presents two hypotheses, Vicus-Alvar and Vicus-Albus, rather than proving one. These rows reinforce Vicálvaro as a long-standing official place/direction name in Madrid’s road network, but they do not decide between Vicus-Alvar and Vicus-Albus.
