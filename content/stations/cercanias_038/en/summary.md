Torrejón de Ardoz station is named after the municipality it serves. Toponomasticon Hispaniae explains Torrejón as a tower-related formation, ultimately connected with Latin turris through Romance forms such as turricula/torreja, while local history links the early settlement to a defensive tower on the frontier around Alcalá. The second element, Ardoz, is the name of the local stream, but its etymology is not securely known. Historical spellings such as Torrejón de Hardoz in 1491 and Torrejón de Ardoz in 1528 show the compound was already established before the town became a villa in 1554.
