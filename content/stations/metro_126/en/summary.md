Barrio del Pilar station opened on 3 June 1983 with the northern, then separate, Line 9B section between Herrera Oria and Plaza de Castilla. Its direct namesake is the surrounding Barrio del Pilar, officially the municipal neighbourhood of Pilar in Fuencarral-El Pardo. The neighbourhood was developed in the early 1960s by José Banús, who built large subsidised housing estates on land formerly linked to the Jesuits. The best-supported popular explanation is that Banús named the development for his wife, Pilar Calvo y Sánchez de León; this is repeated in local-history and press sources, while official records independently confirm her full name and her marriage to Banús. Because no primary naming document was found, the ultimate personal dedication should be presented as probable rather than proven beyond dispute.
