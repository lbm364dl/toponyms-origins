The existing row is broadly well supported: the station name comes from the Barrio del Pilar / El Pilar neighbourhood, and several secondary sources state that the neighbourhood was named for José Banús's wife, Pilar Calvo y Sánchez de León, not directly for the Virgin of the Pillar. The claim should be softened from fully verified to probable because I found no primary naming act or contemporary Banús document explicitly assigning the neighbourhood name for that reason.
