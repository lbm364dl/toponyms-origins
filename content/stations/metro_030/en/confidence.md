The direct etymology is verified by Madrid's official heritage catalogue, which places the Obeliscos-Pirámides at Glorieta de Pirámides, dates them to 1831, names Francisco Javier de Mariategui, and explains their role in the Puente de Toledo-Puerta de Toledo axis. Transport chronology is supported by Comunidad de Madrid and Memoria de Madrid. The exact 1944 naming date remains less directly sourced because the original municipal agreement was not consulted.
