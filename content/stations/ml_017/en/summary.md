Bélgica is an ML2 Metro Ligero Oeste station in Pozuelo de Alarcón. Its name comes directly from Calle de Bélgica, next to the station at Vía de las Dos Castillas. Official mapping shows the street within the Avenida de Europa area, alongside a compact group of European place names including Francia, Bruselas, París, Lisboa, Estrasburgo, Luxemburgo, Holanda and Mónaco. The station opened with Metro Ligero Oeste on 27 July 2007. The European naming pattern is clear, but a specific municipal naming act or stated ideological motive for Calle de Bélgica was not found.
