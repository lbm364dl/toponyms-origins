The most direct official source says the station serves the Barrio de Santa María and takes its name from it. That area is part of the official municipal neighbourhood of Pinar del Rey, so “Parque de Santa María” is best understood as a local or informal place-name rather than one of Madrid’s administrative barrios. The area name is documented around the streets of Santa Susana and Santa Virgilia, where housing in the Barrio de Santa María appears in architectural records from 1970. The nearby parish of Santa María del Parque is real and important locally, but the available evidence does not prove that the neighbourhood was named after the parish.
