The strongest inspected etymology source is Fernández Talaya's AIEM article, which cites Archivo de Villa material and gives a clear chain: the gate began as Puerta de los Pozos de la Nieve, became Puerta de San Fernando in 1821, was dedicated to Bilbao in 1837, and was later demolished during the Ensanche, leaving its name to the plaza. Some web retellings give 11 January 1837 as the agreement date and 19 March 1837 as the inauguration, but the exact agreement date should ideally be verified against ASA 2-356-58 before being treated as archival fact.
