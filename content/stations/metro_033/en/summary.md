Bilbao station opened with Madrid Metro's first line on 17 October 1919 and was named for the Glorieta de Bilbao above it. The square's name comes from the Puerta de Bilbao, the former gate in Madrid's Felipe IV wall. That gate had earlier been known as Puerta de los Pozos de la Nieve and then Puerta de San Fernando, before Madrid dedicated it in 1837 to Bilbao's defenders and liberators during the First Carlist War. The name therefore points both to a Madrid urban landmark and to the Basque city whose resistance became a liberal symbol in 1836-1837.
