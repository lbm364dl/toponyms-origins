The main claim is well supported: the station takes its name from the Glorieta de Bilbao, whose name ultimately commemorates Bilbao's liberal resistance in the First Carlist War. The wording should be tightened: the 1837 act renamed the former gate, not the modern metro station or necessarily the roundabout itself, and authoritative sources describe an Ayuntamiento decision/inauguration rather than proving the exact phrase 'municipal decree'.
