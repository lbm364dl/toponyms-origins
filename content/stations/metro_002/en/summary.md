Gran Vía is a place-name: the station is named for the avenue at the Red de San Luis, where Gran Vía meets Montera, Fuencarral and Hortaleza. Metro de Madrid's historical material records the Line 1 station opening on 17 October 1919 and the later Line 5 interchange opening in 1970. The avenue itself was the result of a long-planned interior reform intended to connect Alcalá with the west side of the city and relieve the congested old centre. During the Franco period the station was renamed José Antonio; it recovered Gran Vía in June 1983, after the city's street names had already been changed back in the democratic period.
