The strongest revision is to replace Wikipedia-only sourcing with Madrid's municipal street database and the RAH-Metro biography page. The official street database is especially useful because it separates three relevant records: Plaza de Legazpi, Estacion de Metro Legazpi, and Intercambiador Legazpi. For public text, avoid describing Legazpi as the sole creator of the Manila Galleon; the defensible formulation is that the return route was achieved by Urdaneta within Legazpi's expedition, and that Manila later became the hub of the transpacific system.
