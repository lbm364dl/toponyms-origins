The origin is verified because Madrid's official street database separately records Plaza de Legazpi and the Metro station Legazpi, with the station located at the plaza, and the Real Academia de la Historia-Metro collaboration explicitly associates the station name Legazpi with Miguel López de Legazpi. The remaining uncertainty is only the exact administrative act date for the plaza, since secondary sources cite 14 July 1926 while the official street record gives 16 July 1926 as the existence date.
