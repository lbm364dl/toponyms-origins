The station name points directly to Madrid's major trade-fair and congress district: the IFEMA MADRID fairgrounds on Avenida del Partenón. The Community of Madrid announced in June 2017 that Campo de las Naciones, on Line 8, would become Feria de Madrid from 26 June, arguing that the new name helped passengers identify the IFEMA venue more easily. The old name referred to the wider Campo de las Naciones urban and cultural complex, developed in the northeast of Madrid around IFEMA, the Palacio Municipal de Congresos and Parque Juan Carlos I. The station itself opened to the public on 24 June 1998 and was the Line 8 terminus until the airport extension opened in 1999.
