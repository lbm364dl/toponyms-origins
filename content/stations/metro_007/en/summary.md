Chueca is named after Plaza de Chueca, which the Madrid City Council dedicated to Federico Chueca in 1943. Chueca, born in Madrid in 1846 and deceased there in 1908, was one of the emblematic composers of the género chico and of Madrid-themed zarzuela. His best-known works include La Gran Vía, with Joaquín Valverde, and Agua, azucarillos y aguardiente. The Metro station received the name Chueca when the Line 5 Callao-Ventas extension entered service in 1970. Administratively the area is part of Justicia, but the station and the square helped popularize Chueca as the name of the neighborhood.
