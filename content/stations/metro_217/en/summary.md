The station name is a direct place-name. It refers to Carabanchel Alto, historically also called Carabanchel de Arriba or de Suso, distinguished from Carabanchel Bajo by its higher position. Madrid City Council records confirm that Carabanchel Alto was a separate municipality until its annexation to Madrid by the Ministry of the Interior decree of 9 January 1948. The Metro station opened with the Line 11 extension on 18 December 2006, not in 2010. The older etymology of 'Carabanchel' is less certain: scholarly and local accounts propose Celtic or pre-Roman personal-name roots, a 'carab' landholder explanation, caravan-related folk etymologies, or stony-ground/garbanzo associations. The official Callejero adds post-annexation street evidence for the same local name, with Carretera de Carabanchel Alto a Cuatro Vientos from 1949 and Avenida de Carabanchel Alto from 27 September 1985.
