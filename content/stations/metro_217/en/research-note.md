The explanation should keep 'place' as the etymology type and 'Carabanchel Alto' as the named-after value, but the English and Spanish summaries should avoid presenting any single ancient derivation of 'Carabanchel' as settled. A useful public nuance is that 'Alto' is strongly evidenced by the old paired geography of Suso/Yuso or Arriba/Abajo, while the word 'Carabanchel' itself belongs to a harder and still contested layer of Madrid toponymy. The BOE annexation PDF's OCR appears to duplicate 'Carabanchel Bajo' in one article line, but the title and context make clear the decree concerns both Bajo and Alto. The prison material is now strongly supported by the 2025 BOE democratic-memory declaration, but it should be secondary here because the station is not named after the prison.
