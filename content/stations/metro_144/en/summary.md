Avenida de América is a place-name station: it takes its name from the Madrid avenue at the junction with Francisco Silvela, María de Molina and Príncipe de Vergara. The avenue name refers to América, the American continent, rather than to a person. Metro service began here on 26 March 1973 with the Line 4 extension from Diego de León to Alfonso XIII, and the station was already planned as a major future interchange. Lines 7, 6 and 9 were added later, and a large underground bus interchange opened on 7 January 2000. The exact municipal act that assigned the avenue name remains to be checked, so claims about a specific commemorative motive should be phrased cautiously.
