The strongest evidence is transport-historical rather than municipal-toponymic: Metro's own 1973 note explicitly names Avenida de América and describes it as a planned multi-line node. The official Callejero supplies the 11 April 1951 alta date for Avenida de América, but no direct official source found here says that the avenue was named to honor Spain's ties with Latin America; that part should remain interpretive unless a street-naming file is found.
