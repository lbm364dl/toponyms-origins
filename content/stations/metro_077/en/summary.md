Almendrales opened on 21 April 2007 as part of the Line 3 extension from Legazpi to Villaverde Alto. Its name comes from the adjacent Almendrales neighbourhood in Usera, served by the new station along Avenida de Córdoba. In Spanish, an almendral is a place planted with almond trees, which fits the local tradition that part of the area was once rural land with almond trees. The modern neighbourhood was shaped by several waves of housing: the early Colonia de los Almendrales from the 1940s, informal low houses in Almendrales Bajos around the 1950s, and the Poblado de Almendrales designed from 1959. The almond-grove interpretation is probable, but the specific 'Cerro de los Almendros' and 1954 parcel-sale details need stronger archival confirmation.
