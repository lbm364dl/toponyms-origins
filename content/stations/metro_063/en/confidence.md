The dedication is supported by the official municipal callejero, a contemporary 1921 press transcription identifying the previous name Glorieta del Cisne, CRTM station access data placing the station at the Glorieta de Rubén Darío, and Metro archive material for the 1970 Line 5 opening context. The only nuance is whether to describe the station as directly named after the poet or indirectly named through the square; the latter is better supported.
