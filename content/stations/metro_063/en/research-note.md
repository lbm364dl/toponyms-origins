The strongest record correction is not the identity of the honoree but the chain of naming: station -> Glorieta de Rubén Darío -> Rubén Darío. The municipal callejero gives 07/10/1921 for the street-name entry, while monument-oriented sources sometimes say the old Glorieta del Cisne was renamed in 1922; a 22 October 1921 press item already says the City Council had approved the change, so 1921 is the better-supported working date unless the municipal minutes show a later formalization.
