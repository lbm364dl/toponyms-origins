The Madrid Metro station is named for the adjacent Glorieta de Rubén Darío, whose municipal street record dates the present name to 7 October 1921. The square had previously been known as the Glorieta del Cisne, according to contemporary press accounts. Its namesake is Rubén Darío, the pen name of Félix Rubén García Sarmiento (1867-1916), the Nicaraguan poet, journalist and diplomat regarded as the central figure of Spanish-language Modernismo. Darío first came to Madrid in 1892 with Nicaragua's delegation for the Columbus centenary events, returned as a major literary presence, and served as Nicaragua's resident minister in Madrid from 1907 to 1909. The Metro station opened on Line 5 in 1970 and inherited this already established urban name.
