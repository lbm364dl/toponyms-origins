For display purposes, the clearest wording is that Arturo Soria station is named after Calle de Arturo Soria, which honours Arturo Soria y Mata. This avoids overstating a direct Metro commemoration while preserving the person-based etymology. The current row's broad narrative is strong, but its most vulnerable elements are the personal attribution of the 1897-1932 magazine run and the unverified Wright influence. The 1979 opening date should be handled cautiously because the Community of Madrid gives 5 January for the extension, while several station lists give 4 January for Arturo Soria specifically.
