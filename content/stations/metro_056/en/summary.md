The Metro station takes its name from Calle de Arturo Soria, the long avenue that preserves the memory of Arturo Soria y Mata (1844-1920). Soria conceived the Ciudad Lineal as a new urban form organised along public transport, low-density housing, gardens and a long infrastructural axis. Madrid's official heritage material places the project in 1892, the Compañía Madrileña de Urbanización in 1894, and the first urbanisation works in 1897. Only a little over five kilometres of a much larger scheme were built, but the name remains embedded in the street, district and station.
