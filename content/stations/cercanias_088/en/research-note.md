The strongest correction concerns railway chronology. A local article repeats an 1861 date, while Wikipedia variants often say 1851; the specialist Docutren paper is more specific for this site and says Villaverde-Alicante opened on 1 June 1893, was renamed in 1906, and became a station in 1931. The public text should avoid the unsourced and problematic 'two theories' material found on the Villaverde Bajo Wikipedia page and rely instead on Toponomasticon Hispaniae.
