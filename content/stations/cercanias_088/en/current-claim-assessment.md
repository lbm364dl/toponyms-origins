The basic claim is correct: the Cercanias station is named for Villaverde Bajo, a local place name within Madrid's Villaverde district. However, several historical details need correction or qualification: the documented railway name Villaverde Bajo was assigned by Royal Order on 19 July 1906, the earlier railway site is better documented as the Villaverde-Alicante siding opened in 1893, and the Madrid-Aranjuez line opened in 1851, not 1861.
