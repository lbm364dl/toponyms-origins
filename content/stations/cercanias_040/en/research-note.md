The best dataset update would separate two layers: immediate namesake = modern La Garena district/urban consortium; deeper historical referent = La Garena/La Garcena estate associated with Carlos de Lucena and his descendants. The Lucena/alumbrado material is historically relevant but should not be over-weighted as the reason for the station name. The garenne explanation remains a plausible linguistic hypothesis, especially because the old estate was described as a seigneurial property, but no dedicated toponymic authority proving it for Alcalá.
