La Garena station takes its name from the La Garena district of Alcalá de Henares, on the city’s western edge. The current transport name is therefore a local place-name: the stop was built for a new residential, commercial, and business area developed under the La Garena urban consortium.

The name is older than the 21st-century neighbourhood. Historical research on the Lucena family records an estate near Alcalá called La Garena or La Garcena, bought around 1495 by Carlos de Lucena. A recent municipal history article by the official chronicler of Alcalá also presents La Garena as a large estate and notes that Miguel de Portilla’s 1725 history of Alcalá described it as having a substantial country house.

The estate was associated with the Lucena-Castillo family, a well-off converso family in 16th-century Alcalá. The place appears in the history of the alumbrados because Juan del Castillo, Petronila de Lucena, and other relatives were investigated by the Inquisition; one account says forbidden letters and a book were hidden at the La Garena estate. This does not mean the station is named after the alumbrados, but it gives the place-name an unusually dense historical background.

The railway stop is modern. A 2002 BOE notice records the contract award for works on the Cercanías station La Garena, and contemporary press reported its inauguration in May 2004 in the La Garena district. Today the Consorcio Regional de Transportes de Madrid lists it on Cercanías lines C-2, C-7, and C-8, with access from Calle Fausto Elhuyar.

The deepest linguistic origin is less certain. The most plausible explanation links La Garena with French garenne or medieval Latin warenna, a word for a seigneurial hunting reserve or rabbit warren; French dictionaries document that meaning and its medieval forms. But I did not find a primary Alcalá document or specialist toponymic entry explicitly deriving this particular La Garena from garenne, so the historical place-name is well supported while the etymology should be marked probable rather than fully verified.
