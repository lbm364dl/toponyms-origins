La Garena is a modern Cercanías stop, opened in 2004 to serve the new residential, business, and commercial development on the western side of Alcalá de Henares. The name came from the La Garena urban sector promoted by the Community of Madrid and the City Council. Behind that modern sector is a much older place-name: a large estate called La Garena or La Garcena, bought around 1495 by Carlos de Lucena, a member of a converso family established in Alcalá. Later sources describe the estate as a Lucena seigneury and connect it with university festivities and with the inquisitorial history of the alumbrados. A link with Old French garenne, 'hunting reserve' or 'rabbit warren', is linguistically plausible, but the exact route by which the word became this Alcalá place-name remains unproven.
