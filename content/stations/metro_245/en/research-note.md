The existing English and Spanish summaries are usable if reframed: keep the station-street-mountain chain first, then add the monastery as cultural background. The strongest revision is the correction of the opening year to 1999. The date conflict appears because later Line 1 extensions to La Gavia, Las Suertes and Valdecarros opened in 2007, but Sierra de Guadalupe belonged to the earlier 1999 extension to Congosto.
