Sierra de Guadalupe is best explained as a local street-derived station name. Madrid's official street list records Calle de la Sierra de Guadalupe in Villa de Vallecas, and the Line 1 extension documentation places the station and its Cercanías interchange there. The deeper place name points to the Guadalupe/Villuercas mountains of Extremadura, a landscape tied to the town of Guadalupe and the Royal Monastery of Santa María de Guadalupe. The monastery and Marian tradition explain why Guadalupe became a nationally and internationally resonant name, but they are background to the mountain and street name rather than the station's immediate namesake.
