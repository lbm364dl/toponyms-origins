The core claim is well supported: the name refers to Isabella I of Castile and Ferdinand II of Aragon, the Catholic Monarchs, and it is especially apt in San Sebastián de los Reyes because the town traces its foundation to their royal protection in 1492. The row should add that the station name most directly follows the local Avenida de los Reyes Católicos, and it should soften the statement that their marriage 'unified' Castile and Aragon, since the crowns remained institutionally distinct.
