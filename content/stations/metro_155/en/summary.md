The Metro station is most directly named after the nearby Avenida de los Reyes Católicos in San Sebastián de los Reyes. That avenue and station name refer to Isabella I of Castile and Ferdinand II of Aragon, the monarchs whose joint rule shaped late medieval Spain. The connection is not merely generic: the municipality itself links its foundation in 1492 to the royal protection granted by the Catholic Monarchs to settlers gathered by an older hermitage of Saint Sebastian. The station opened with MetroNorte on 26 April 2007, carrying a name that ties a modern suburban interchange to the town’s foundational memory.
