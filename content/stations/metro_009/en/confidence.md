The station's immediate naming source is secure: it was named for Plaza de Lavapiés and the existing place name. The deeper etymology is probable rather than verified because direct medieval spellings are ambiguous and no source proves the exact moment when the descriptive phrase became a fixed toponym. The topographical explanation is nevertheless much better supported than the Jewish-fountain or inn theories. The official vial chronology strengthens the plaza/street origin but does not settle the older Avapiés/Lavapiés documentary problem.
