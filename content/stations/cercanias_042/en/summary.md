The station is named for the Universidad de Alcalá, especially the university campus area reached from the Carretera del Campus. Alcalá’s university tradition began with a Studium Generale in 1293 and was refounded in 1499 through Cisneros’s humanist project, authorized by a papal bull of Alexander VI. The old university was moved to Madrid in the nineteenth century and became part of the history of today’s Universidad Complutense de Madrid. A new public university with its seat in Alcalá was created by Royal Decree in 1977, giving modern force to the name that the station carries.
