The strongest evidence is convergent rather than a single naming decree: station geography, station-name practice, and municipal biography all point to the architect. The current record should preserve the person etymology but avoid overstating 'self-taught' or royal-office claims. A good public version should emphasize the street-to-person chain and the Salón del Prado fountains, while noting that Rodríguez designed the projects and other artists carved much of the sculpture.
