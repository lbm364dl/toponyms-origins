The Metro station is named after Ventura Rodríguez, the street it serves near Calle de la Princesa, and ultimately after Buenaventura Rodríguez Tizón (1717-1785). Rodríguez was born in Ciempozuelos and became one of the major architects of eighteenth-century Madrid, especially through his municipal work as maestro mayor and his designs for the Salón del Prado fountains. Madrid City Council sources identify him as the designer of Cibeles and link him to the Cibeles-Neptuno-Apolo programme, although the sculptures themselves were carved by specialist sculptors. The station belongs to Line 3 and dates from the Sol-Argüelles extension of July 1941.
