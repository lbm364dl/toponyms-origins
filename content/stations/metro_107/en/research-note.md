The source trail strongly supports the station-to-square link and the square's mid-20th-century urban formation, but not the dataset's precise Crown of Castile wording. CRTM's panel says the large circular square was planned at the intersection of the new Avenida del Generalísimo and the old road to France and was known as Plaza de Castilla; it does not explain why 'Castilla' was chosen. Given the plaza's role as Madrid's northern gateway toward the Burgos/A-1 corridor and the broad symbolic use of Castile in Spanish geography, 'Castile' is reasonable, while 'Crown of Castile' should be marked as unverified.
