Julio Romero de Torres station is named for its location on Calle Julio Romero de Torres in Parla. The street commemorates, by name, Julio Romero de Torres, a Spanish painter born and deceased in Córdoba in 1874 and 1930. The Museo del Prado describes his path from regionalist painting toward symbolism and a highly personal mature style rooted in Andalusian popular feeling and Renaissance pictorial tradition. He was appointed professor of drapery at the Academia de Bellas Artes de San Fernando in Madrid in 1916. No earlier station name or specific street-naming resolution was found.
