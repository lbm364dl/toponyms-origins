Alonso Martínez station is named for Plaza de Alonso Martínez, where the Metro platforms were built. The square honors Manuel Alonso Martínez (1827-1891), a Burgos-born jurist and liberal politician who served as minister, deputy, president of the Congress of Deputies, and leading figure in Spanish legal codification. His name is especially linked to the Civil Code published in 1889, although the final code was produced through the Law of Bases and the Commission of Codes rather than by a single personal draft. Metro de Madrid's historical station sheet confirms that the square had been the Glorieta de Santa Bárbara and took its present name in 1891.
