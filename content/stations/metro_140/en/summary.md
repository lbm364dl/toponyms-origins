Bambú is best explained as a station named after its immediate street, Calle del Bambú. CRTM identifies the station access at Calle del Bambú 14, and the official Madrid street gazetteer lists Calle del Bambú in Chamartín. Nearby streets such as Calle de la Yuca, Calle de la Hiedra and Calle de la Buganvilla show a local botanical naming pattern, though I did not find the original municipal naming act for that pattern. The station opened on 11 April 2007 as part of the Line 1 extension from Chamartín to Pinar de Chamartín.
