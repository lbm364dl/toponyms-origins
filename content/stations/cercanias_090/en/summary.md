Universidad P. Comillas is a functional railway name: it points passengers to the Cantoblanco campus of Universidad Pontificia Comillas. Adif lists the station as Universidad Pontificia de Comillas, and the university itself tells students that the Cercanías stop for Cantoblanco is Universidad Pontificia Comillas or Universidad P. Comillas. The institution was founded in Comillas, Cantabria, by papal brief of Leo XIII on 16 December 1890, under the patronage of Antonio López y López and Claudio López Bru, the first and second Marquesses of Comillas. It became able to confer academic degrees in 1904 and moved gradually to Madrid after authorization in the late 1960s. The station opened in 2001 with the Cantoblanco-Alcobendas-San Sebastián de los Reyes Cercanías branch.
