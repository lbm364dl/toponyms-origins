Sol station is named for Puerta del Sol, Madrid's central square and one of the city's most durable place names. The strongest official source, Madrid's heritage catalogue, explains the name as coming from a postern in the medieval wall that was decorated with a sun. That small gate marked an outside edge of old Madrid before urban growth turned the area into the capital's symbolic center. Metro reached Sol with the first line on 17 October 1919, and the Cercanías station opened below the square in 2009. The station briefly carried sponsored names in 2012 and 2013-2016, but the historic name Sol was restored.
