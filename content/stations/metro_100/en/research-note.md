The best-supported explanation is a two-step etymology: station from street, street from Francisco Serrano y Domínguez. The municipal heritage source is stronger than Wikipedia for the street dedication and adds nuance missing from the previous wording: earlier names and the plaque at C/ Serrano 11/14. Metro's own historical sheet gives the station context and notes the public opening nuance: the official inauguration was on 23 March 1944 and public service began at 6:30 the following day.
