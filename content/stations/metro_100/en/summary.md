Serrano station, opened on Line 4 in 1944, was named from the nearby Calle de Serrano at Goya. The street commemorates Francisco Serrano y Domínguez, Duke of la Torre, a military and political figure who helped lead the 1868 revolution against Isabel II and was later appointed regent of the kingdom. Madrid municipal heritage notes that he lived and died in a house on this street, marked by a city plaque. The street's name also records a political turn in the Salamanca district: earlier references to Isabel II and Narváez gave way to Serrano after the revolution.
