Universidad Rey Juan Carlos station takes its name from the URJC campus on Calle Tulipán in Móstoles, not from a separately named street or district. The deeper origin is institutional: Madrid regional Law 7/1996 created the Universidad «Rey Juan Carlos», named for King Juan Carlos I. The university began academic activity in October 1997, initially with only a few hundred students and without its own completed buildings. Line 12, MetroSur, opened in 2003 as a circular metro for the southern metropolitan municipalities, and this stop served the new university area from the beginning. For the dataset, the cleanest classification is a place or institutional landmark, with Juan Carlos I recorded as the secondary eponym behind the university's name.
