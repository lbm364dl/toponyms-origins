Manoteras is a place-name station: it was named for the Manoteras area around the station, now largely within the administrative neighbourhood of Apóstol Santiago in Hortaleza. The name appears in local historical accounts as Las Manoteras in July 1807, in connection with land auctions between Fuencarral, Hortaleza and Chamartín. A widely cited philological explanation, attributed to Emilio Nieto Ballester, derives the name from Latin elements meaning high hills, with intermediate forms such as Lomaotera and La Manotera. The station opened on 11 April 2007 during the extension of Metro line 4 toward Pinar de Chamartín.
