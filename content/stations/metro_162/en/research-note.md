The strongest split is between two evidence levels: the station-level name and date are well supported by transport/municipal material, while the deeper linguistic chain is supported through secondary citation rather than direct inspection of Nieto Ballester. Local map-based history also shows that Manoteras was not always geographically identical with the current Apóstol Santiago neighbourhood: older references include paths, houses, upper/lower Manoteras and nearby rural features across the former municipal edges of Fuencarral, Hortaleza and Chamartín.
