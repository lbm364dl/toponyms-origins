Parla is a place-name station: Renfe's current Cercanías stop takes its name from the municipality of Parla, south of Madrid. Specialist toponymists, including Jairo J. García Sánchez in the Centro Virtual Cervantes and Emilio Nieto Ballester's dictionary, explain Parla as a development from Latin (villa) Parila, the estate of a Roman owner called Parilus. A second, older local explanation, associated with Fernando Jiménez de Gregorio and repeated in a Comunidad de Madrid publication, links the name to Latin padule/palus, meaning lagoon, marsh or pond, and to local wet-place names. The popular story that Parla comes from a miraculous act of speaking after drinking from a fountain is a folk etymology, useful as legend but not as linguistic evidence.
