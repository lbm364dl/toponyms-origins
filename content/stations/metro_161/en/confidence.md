The immediate naming source is verified by official and archival material: the Metro station opened in 2007 as Hortaleza and the district/former municipality is documented by Madrid municipal sources and the BOE annexation decree. Confidence is reduced to probable because the deeper medieval etymology of Hortaleza is contested: the previous wording's hortus/orchard claim is not the strongest sourced explanation, while the fortaleza hypothesis is linguistically stronger but still does not prove a specific fortress. The vial chronology strengthens the place-name continuity but does not settle the fortaleza versus huertas etymological issue.
