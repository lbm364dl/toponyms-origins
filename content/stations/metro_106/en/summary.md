Cuzco station takes its name from the roundabout above it, Plaza de Cuzco, where Paseo de la Castellana meets Avenida de Alberto Alcocer. Madrid's official street list records the square as Plaza de Cuzco in the districts of Chamartín and Tetuán. The name points to Cusco/Cuzco in Peru, the historic Inca capital and a UNESCO World Heritage city. The station opened on 10 June 1982 as part of the former Line 8 and later became part of Line 10 in the 1998 reorganization.
