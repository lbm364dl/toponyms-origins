Nuevos Ministerios station is named after the large ministerial complex that occupies this stretch of Paseo de la Castellana. Madrid City Council records that Indalecio Prieto, Minister of Public Works, commissioned Secundino Zuazo in 1932 to design the complex on the former hippodrome grounds. Construction began in 1933, stopped during the Civil War, and was completed after Zuazo had been removed from the project by the Franco regime. The railway stop opened first, in 1967, as part of the Atocha-Chamartín tunnel; Metro arrived with Line 6 on 11 October 1979, and later lines made it one of Madrid's major interchanges.
