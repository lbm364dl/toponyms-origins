Miguel Hernández is a Line 1 station whose name comes from the nearby Avenida de Miguel Hernández in Puente de Vallecas. That avenue commemorates Miguel Hernández Gilabert, the Orihuela-born poet and playwright whose work became one of the emblematic voices of twentieth-century Spanish poetry. The station opened on 7 April 1994, when Metro extended Line 1 from Portazgo to Miguel Hernández through Buenos Aires and Alto del Arenal. Hernández's life gives the name a strong literary and historical charge: after fighting on the Republican side in the Civil War, he was imprisoned, sentenced to death, had the sentence commuted, and died of tuberculosis in Alicante prison in 1942.
