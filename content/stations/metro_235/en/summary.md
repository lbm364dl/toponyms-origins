Getafe Central was opened with MetroSur on 11 April 2003 and serves the central area of Getafe, interchanging with Cercanías line C-4. Its name is best understood as a transport-place name: the central station of the municipality of Getafe. The older place name Getafe appears in early modern forms such as Xetafe and is linked in local tradition to medieval Xatafi or Satafi. A 1576 response to Philip II's Relaciones says residents had heard that Arabic jata meant 'long thing', referring to settlement along the Madrid-Toledo road, but Jairo J. García Sánchez cautions that this Arabic derivation cannot presently be verified.
