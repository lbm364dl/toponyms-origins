The Metro station is named for Plaza Elíptica, the ellipse-shaped plaza and road junction above it. Madrid's own historical-memory documentation explains that the name came from the plaza's form when the area was reorganized after Carabanchel Bajo was incorporated into Madrid. In 1954 the plaza was renamed Plaza de Fernández Ladreda, but the popular name Plaza Elíptica endured and was even kept by the Metro station opened in 1981. The city formally restored Plaza Elíptica as the official street name by a Junta de Gobierno agreement of 4 May 2017.
