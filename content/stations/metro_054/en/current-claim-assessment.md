The main chain is correct: the station name is a local place-name ultimately derived from the historic Finca or Quinta de Vista Alegre in Carabanchel. However, the wording should be corrected because the name Vista Alegre predates María Cristina's ownership, the 1832 transfer is more precise than a simple private purchase by the queen, and the claim about a 1904 bullring on the estate is not well supported.
