The Metro station is named for the Vista Alegre area of Carabanchel, whose name comes from the Finca or Quinta de Vista Alegre. Official heritage records state that Pablo Cabrero Martínez and Josefa Martínez Artó transformed an earlier property into a public pleasure estate and baptized it Vista Alegre before opening it in 1824. In 1832 it passed to Fernando VII and María Cristina de Borbón, who enlarged it and turned it into the Real Posesión de Vista Alegre. The estate later passed to Isabel II, the infanta Luisa Fernanda and José de Salamanca, and today survives as a fragmented but protected historic garden and public heritage site.
