The best evidence indicates a two-stage naming history: first, Vista Alegre as a promotional or descriptive name for a public pleasure quinta created by Cabrero and Martínez in the 1820s; second, Real Posesión de Vista Alegre after the 1832 royal transfer and expansion. The station likely reflects the surrounding barrio/toponym rather than a direct commemorative naming for María Cristina. The current namesake value should remain a place, preferably Finca de Vista Alegre or Quinta de Vista Alegre, not the queen.
