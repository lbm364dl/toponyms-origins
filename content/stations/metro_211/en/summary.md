La Granja is the first MetroNorte station in Alcobendas when travelling north from Madrid. Its access points are officially listed on Calle de Sepúlveda and Calle de la Granja, and municipal material places the station at Calle la Granja, 14, in the heart of the Alcobendas industrial estate. The name therefore appears to come from the street and surrounding industrial area. 'Granja' means a rural farm or livestock property, which makes an agricultural reading natural, especially in a municipality that changed rapidly in the twentieth century. However, the specific claim that the street was named to commemorate the area's former farmland remains an inference unless a municipal street-naming file is found.
