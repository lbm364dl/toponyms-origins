La Granja station is named for its immediate place: Calle de La Granja, in Alcobendas. The CRTM lists the station access by Calle de Sepúlveda and the lift by Calle de la Granja, and the municipal location catalogue gives the station address as Calle la Granja, 14. For public-facing etymology, the strongest documented origin is therefore the street and the industrial-estate setting around it.

The Spanish word 'granja' means a rural farmstead or a property used for raising animals. That meaning makes the name feel older than the present landscape of warehouses, offices and business parks. Still, no inspected official source identifies a particular historical farm called La Granja at this site, so the word's agricultural resonance should be presented carefully.

The station belongs to MetroNorte, the Line 10 extension built to connect northern Madrid with Alcobendas and San Sebastián de los Reyes. Community of Madrid material describes MetroNorte as serving areas including the industrial estates of Calabozos and Vereda de los Pobres, and lists La Granja among the eleven new stations. Contemporary press coverage reported the opening of the new extension on 26 April 2007, while a Community of Madrid infrastructure page gives 23 April 2007 as the puesta en servicio date.

The urban context is important. Municipal planning documents and location guides describe this part of Alcobendas as an industrial estate, with Calle de La Granja appearing as a real street in that fabric. Modern references sometimes speak of the 'polígono de La Granja', which shows how the station and street name have become shorthand for this working and business district.

The current row is strongest when it says the station was named from a local street in the Alcobendas industrial area. It is weaker when it presents the agricultural explanation as fully verified, because that step depends on the meaning of the word and broader urban history rather than on a street-naming act. The planned name 'Alcobendas Industrial' appears in Wikipedia-derived material, but I did not find an independent official project document confirming it.
