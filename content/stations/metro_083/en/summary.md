Tres Olivos is best explained as a place-name: the Metro station was named for the residential area where it was built. Madrid planning records identify the sector as 'Los Tres Olivos' and as API.08.05, with the basic partial plan processed in 1988 and a later modification in 1993. An architectural-planning article describes the sector north of old Fuencarral, between major roads and the older Nuevo Toboso development, with a central Plaza de los Tres Olivos. The literal Spanish meaning is 'three olive trees', but no available authoritative source confirms that three individual olive trees physically gave the place its name. The station opened on 26 April 2007 as part of the MetroNorte extension of Line 10.
