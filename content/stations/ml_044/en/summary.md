Reyes Católicos is a surface stop of the Tranvía de Parla located at Calle Reyes Católicos, 37. Its name comes directly from that street, which forms part of a local cluster of royal names such as Isabel II, Felipe II, Fernando III el Santo and Jaime I el Conquistador. The historical namesakes are Isabel I of Castile and Fernando II of Aragon, whose 1469 marriage joined two royal houses and whose reigns shaped late-medieval and early-modern Spain. The papal title 'Catholic Monarchs' was formally granted by Alexander VI in the bull Si convenit of 19 December 1496. The existing explanation is therefore verified, with the caveat that the marriage should be described as a dynastic union rather than the immediate creation of a unified modern Spain.
