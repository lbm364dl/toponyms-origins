Santo Domingo is a place-name inherited from the square at the west end of the station. That square grew out of the open space beside the monastery of Santo Domingo el Real, one of medieval Madrid's most important religious houses. The Dominican community is traditionally linked to Saint Dominic of Guzmán's visit to Madrid around 1219, although modern sources differ on the exact foundation sequence. The old convent was demolished in 1869 after the 1868 revolution, and the community later continued in Claudio Coello street. The station opened on 21 October 1925 as part of Line 2's Sol-Quevedo extension.
