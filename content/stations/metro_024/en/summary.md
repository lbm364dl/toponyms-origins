Concha Espina is a Line 9 station in Chamartín, opened with the Plaza de Castilla-Avenida de América section on 30 December 1983. Its name reflects the nearby Avenida de Concha Espina, an official Madrid street running from Paseo de la Castellana to Calle del Príncipe de Vergara. The avenue and station preserve the public memory of Concha Espina, the pen name of Concepción Espina y Tagle, born in Santander in 1869 and dead in Madrid in 1955. Her literary reputation rested on novels such as La niña de Luzmela and La esfinge maragata, and the Nobel Foundation records 25 nominations for her in Literature between 1926 and 1954.
