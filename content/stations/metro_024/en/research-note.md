The strongest station-specific evidence found is the contemporary 31 December 1983 El País report naming Concha Espina among the six stations of the new Plaza de Castilla-Avenida de América section, plus CRTM's current station listing. The strongest biographical authority is RAH's Historia Hispánica entry, which uses the form Concepción Espina y Tagle; other sources and catalogues use fuller or married-name variants such as Concepción Rodríguez-Espina y García-Tagle and Concha Espina de la Serna. the public namesake can remain Concha Espina, with the canonical biographical cue added in notes.
