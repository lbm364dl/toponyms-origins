The station's name, opening date and placement in the Line 7 Valdezarza-Pitis extension are confirmed by Community of Madrid transport documentation and contemporary press. The connection from the station to the avenue is direct from the name and location; the connection from Ilustración to the Enlightenment is supported by the RAE definition. The exact municipal naming resolution for the avenue was not located, so the Carlos III-specific interpretation should remain background rather than the named-after value.
