Avenida de la Ilustración station takes its name from the nearby avenue, not directly from a person. The avenue was designed in the 1980s as the north-western closure of Madrid’s M-30 and opened to traffic on 14 April 1992. Its name invokes the Enlightenment, defined by the RAE as the 18th-century philosophical and cultural movement that emphasized human reason and belief in progress. The station opened on Line 7 on 29 March 1999 as part of the Valdezarza-Pitis extension.
