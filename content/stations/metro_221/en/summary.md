The station name is local before it is international: it names the Parque Lisboa/Parque de Lisboa area of Alcorcón, not a direct interchange with Lisbon. Municipal planning documentation places the start of Parque Lisboa in 1969, between San José de Valderas and the historic town center, during Alcorcón's rapid transformation from rural municipality to metropolitan residential city. The name ultimately points to Lisboa, the Portuguese capital, and the surrounding street grid preserves that Portuguese/Atlantic flavor with names such as Avenida de Lisboa and streets beginning with Porto. The Metro station opened with the rest of Line 12, MetroSur, on 11 April 2003.
