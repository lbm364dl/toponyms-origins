The station is officially listed as Parque Oeste on Line 12 and official regional transport information places it in Alcorcón beside Calle Estambul. Urban-planning and BOE sources show that the surrounding Área de Centralidad was already known as Parque Oeste de Alcorcón before the Metro station opened. Confidence is limited to probable because no primary Metro naming resolution explicitly saying 'the station is named after the area' was found.
