Parque Oeste is best understood as a station named after a local place: the Parque Oeste area or barrio of Alcorcón. Official planning documents describe the former Área de Centralidad of Alcorcón as already known in the 1990s as Parque Oeste de Alcorcón. The area evolved from a strategic urban-development project into a mixed district with large retail premises, housing, the Universidad Rey Juan Carlos campus, hospital facilities and urban parkland. The Metro station opened with Line 12, MetroSur, on 11 April 2003 and its access is placed by official transport information beside Calle Estambul. No direct official station-naming resolution was found, so the origin is highly probable rather than fully documented.
