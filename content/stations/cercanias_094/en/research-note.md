There is a useful distinction for dataset quality: the railway naming is a place-name transfer, while the underlying place-name is probably faunal/descriptive. The official and semi-official record alternates between Las Zorreras and Las Zorreras-Navalquejigo; the latter is strongest for the station, while Las Zorreras is the older local toponym. The municipal road inventory's alternative name Los Tejones is relevant because both zorreras and tejoneras belong to the same kind of rural animal-den vocabulary, but it does not prove that foxes, rather than a family name or another local usage, created the toponym.
