Bulevar Sur-Miguel Ángel Blanco is a double place-and-person name. “Bulevar Sur” identifies the southern boulevard area of Parla, while “Miguel Ángel Blanco” commemorates Miguel Ángel Blanco Garrido, a 29-year-old People’s Party councillor in Ermua. ETA kidnapped him on 10 July 1997 and demanded the transfer of ETA prisoners to Basque prisons within 48 hours; after the ultimatum expired he was shot near Lasarte and died on 13 July. The public reaction, remembered as the “Spirit of Ermua,” became one of the major civic mobilisations against ETA. Parla’s municipal plenary assigned his name to the boulevard in January 2019, and Tranvía de Parla renamed the stop on 22 March 2022.
