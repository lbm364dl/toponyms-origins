The existing claim is substantially correct about Miguel Ángel Blanco and the commemorative origin, but it misses the station-specific chronology: Parla assigned the boulevard name in January 2019 and Tranvía de Parla applied it to the stop on 22 March 2022. It also compresses the death chronology: Blanco was shot after the ultimatum, found still alive, and died in the early hours of 13 July 1997.
