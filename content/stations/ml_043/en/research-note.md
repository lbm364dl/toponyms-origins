This station is best treated as a commemorative renaming layered onto a pre-existing descriptive stop name. The most direct station source is the operator’s 2022 notice; CRTM lagging behind with “Bulevar Sur” should not override the operator evidence but is worth retaining as a display-name discrepancy. The January 2019 municipal act is especially valuable because it fixes the place: the Bulevar Miguel Ángel Blanco was the former Bulevar Sur section between Travesía Severo Ochoa and the Police Station roundabout.
