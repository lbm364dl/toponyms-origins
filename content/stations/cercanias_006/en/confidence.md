The immediate naming chain is well supported by official transport and street-directory sources: the station is on Camino/Avenida de la Fuente de la Mora and local history records the older Camino de la Fuente de la Mora. Confidence is not 'verified' because the precise origin of Mora for this specific fountain has not been found in a direct primary or scholarly source. The Callejero rows strengthen the official street-name evidence, but they still do not identify the physical spring or settle the meaning of Mora.
