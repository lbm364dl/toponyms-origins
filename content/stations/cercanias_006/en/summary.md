The station name is best explained as a local place-name: Fuente de la Mora, preserved in the official street names Camino de la Fuente de la Mora and Avenida de la Fuente de la Mora in Hortaleza. Local historical writing on old Hortaleza roads identifies the Camino de la Fuente de la Mora as a surviving rural road name later absorbed into Sanchinarro's urban fabric. Fuente is straightforward: a spring or fountain. Mora is less certain; a pre-Roman mor- meaning stony ground is plausible in wider Spanish toponymy, but no available source proves that derivation for this exact Hortaleza fountain. The station was planned for Cercanías as Manoteras, but opened under the existing Metro Ligero/toponymic name Fuente de la Mora.
