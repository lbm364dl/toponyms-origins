Venus is a paired north/south stop on the Parla tram, named from Calle Planeta Venus. The current CRTM map places Calle Planeta Venus between Avenida de las Estrellas and Avenida de los Planetas, in the astronomical street-name area of Parla Este. The name therefore comes first from a local street, and behind that from the planet Venus. NASA identifies Venus as the planet named for the Roman goddess of love and beauty, famous as one of the brightest objects in the sky. The exact municipal act assigning the street or stop name was not located, but the naming relationship is strongly supported by transport maps and local municipal records.
