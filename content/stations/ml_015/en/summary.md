Somosaguas Centro names the stop by its position in the Somosaguas sector of Pozuelo de Alarcón, near La Finca and the Zoco de Pozuelo. The deeper name is medieval: a linguistic study cites Somosaguas as somas aquas in the Fuero de Madrid and links it with the old Castilian somo, from Latin summum, meaning the highest part or summit. Local history also records San Juan de Somosaguas as one of the early hamlets or farmsteads associated with Pozuelo, later depopulated during the fourteenth-century conflict between Pedro I and Enrique II. The modern stop opened with ML2 on 27 July 2007, so the public-transport name is recent but rests on a much older landscape name.
