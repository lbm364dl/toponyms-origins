The Cercanias stop is named after El Goloso, the place where it stands, beside the M-607 and the military installations of the same name. Evidence points to El Goloso as a pre-existing locality name: a military-cartographic series already had a sheet titled El Goloso in 1930, before the first stone of the Cuartel del Goloso was laid on 30 January 1933. The later base made the name prominent, but it probably did not create it. In ordinary Spanish, goloso means fond of sweets, desirous, or appetizing, but no authoritative source was found that explains why this rural Madrid place bore that name.
