The strongest correction is geographic and chronological: El Goloso should be treated primarily as a place-name in Madrid's Fuencarral-El Pardo area, not as a Tres Cantos name, and not as a name coined by the completed military base. The military base is central to present public recognition, but the 1930 cartographic reference and the 1933 first-stone date imply an older locality name. I did not find an authoritative etymology for why this place was called El Goloso.
