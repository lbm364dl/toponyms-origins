The existing explanation is substantially correct: the station takes the district name, and the district name is ultimately tied to Marcelo de Usera y Sánchez and the early twentieth-century urbanization of lands inherited by his wife, Carmen del Río Fernández. The main improvements are precision: the RAH identifies him as a lieutenant colonel, not simply a colonel, and the direct source of the land was his wife's inheritance from José del Río, known as Tío Sordillo.
