The strongest direct source for the place-name narrative is the Ayuntamiento diagnostic PDF, which reproduces the usual local explanation in a municipal context. The RAH biography is stronger for Marcelo Usera's biographical facts but does not by itself prove the station naming. Some popular retellings call him 'coronel'; the more exact RAH wording is lieutenant colonel. The Chinatown material is relevant public context for present-day Usera but should not be confused with the etymology.
