The previous wording is strongest when it treats Carabanchel as a place-name inherited by the Metro station. Its deeper etymology should be softened: Menéndez Pidal’s pre-Roman stone-root grouping is important, but the accessible academic text by Caridad Arias explicitly promotes a Carabantius/Caravantius personal-name explanation and still connects the group with broader ancient Celtic/Illyrian/Ligurian material. The municipal sources are useful for chronology and urban history, but they do not decide the linguistic question.
