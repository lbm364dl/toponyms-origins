Humanes is a place-name station: Renfe and the Madrid Regional Transport Consortium identify it as the C-5 station located in the municipality of Humanes de Madrid. The older name Humanes is documented in the twelfth century, when Alfonso VII donated the nearly deserted villa to Pedro Brimonis or Pedro Brimon. Its linguistic origin is less settled. One modern line of explanation connects Humanes with an ancient hydronymic base, while an inspected Madrid toponymy article derives it from Latin humidus, supported by local wetland, stream, well and pond names. The public-facing safest wording is that the name is probably water-related, not that one single prehistoric derivation is proven.
