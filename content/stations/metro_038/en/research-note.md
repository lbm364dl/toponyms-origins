The best-supported direct etymology is not that Metro independently named a station after the politician, but that the station took the already established square name. The RAH/Historical Biography page is available at https://historia-hispanica.rah.es/biografias/5845-manuel-becerra-y-bermudez and source summaries identify him as mathematician, minister, politician, revolutionary and senator; the page itself was not available as full text. The Real Academia de Ciencias PDF gives 1823 as the birth year, while Prado and the RAH source summary give 1820; the dataset's 1820 is better supported by the modern biographical authorities.
