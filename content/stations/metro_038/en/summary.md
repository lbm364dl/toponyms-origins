Manuel Becerra station is named after the Plaza de Manuel Becerra, the major crossroads above the platforms. The square was formerly the Glorieta de la Alegría and was renamed by municipal agreement in 1905 for Manuel Becerra y Bermúdez, a Galician-born mathematician and politician who served as deputy, senator, Minister of Ultramar and Minister of Fomento. Metro used the name from the opening of Line 2's Ventas-Sol section on 14 June 1924. The surrounding square was renamed Plaza de Roma in 1961 and recovered the Manuel Becerra name in 1980, while the Metro station kept the established name.
