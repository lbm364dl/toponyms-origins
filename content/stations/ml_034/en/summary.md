Nuevo Mundo station takes its name directly from Avenida Nuevo Mundo, the avenue on which Metro Ligero Oeste places the stop. In Spanish, 'Nuevo Mundo' is the historical 'New World', the European name applied to the Americas after the Atlantic voyages of the late fifteenth and early sixteenth centuries. Boadilla’s street map supports the interpretation that this is part of a local cluster of names connected with exploration and early modern imperial history, including Cristóbal Colón, Hernán Cortés, Francisco Pizarro, Alonso de Ojeda, Francisco de Orellana and Pedro de Valdivia. The station opened with Metro Ligero Oeste on 27 July 2007, serving a modern residential axis west of the historic centre.
