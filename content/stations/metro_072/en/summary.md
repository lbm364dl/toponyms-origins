The station name comes from Pavones, a place-name in Moratalaz rather than from a person. Its immediate urban reference is the Pavones neighbourhood and Calle de la Hacienda de Pavones, where the station opened on Line 9 on 31 January 1980. Historical records show that Pavones was an agricultural hacienda, also written Pabones, spread across lands of the former municipalities of Vallecas and Vicálvaro. A 1964 BOE decree describes the estate in detail, confirming that the place-name survived into the period when Moratalaz was being urbanised.
