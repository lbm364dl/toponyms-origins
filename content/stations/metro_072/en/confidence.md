The place-name origin is strongly supported by official BOE documentation for a Hacienda de Pavones/Pabones in Moratalaz-area lands, by municipal material identifying Pavones as a Moratalaz barrio, and by official transport sources confirming the opening of the Sainz de Baranda-Pavones Line 9 section. The only unresolved nuance is that No separate Metro naming act explicitly saying whether the station was named after the barrio, the street, or the older estate; all three point to the same local toponym.
