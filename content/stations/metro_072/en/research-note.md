For dataset purposes, 'Pavones (neighbourhood)' is acceptable but slightly less precise than 'Hacienda de Pavones / Pavones place-name'. The neighbourhood and street are modern urban survivals of a rural estate name; the BOE's 1964 description is the strongest direct official evidence found. The 1831 'Palacios y Valderribas' reference is useful historical context but should be treated as sourced through local historical compilation unless the original BNE item is consulted directly.
