Artilleros station is named for the nearby Calle del Pico de los Artilleros, one of the Moratalaz street names that preserves the district's pre-urban landscape. The name refers to the artillery firing and manoeuvre ground established on leased land in November 1861. Local research by Ricardo Márquez Ruiz places the military field around the present Avenida del Mediterráneo/A-3 corridor and records its declaration as of public utility in 1907. The last manoeuvre located in that research is from June 1927. The current row is therefore reliable, but should say that the station inherits a street/toponym linked to the range.
