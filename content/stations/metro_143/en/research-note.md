The strongest direct evidence found online connects the station to the present street and the street to the artillery range. The range itself appears to have been around the current Avenida del Mediterráneo/A-3 rather than exactly under the station, so the public explanation should avoid over-locating the firing ground at the station mouth. The current wording's date sequence is consistent with Márquez Ruiz's research, but an official street-naming date was not found.
