Pinar de Chamartín is a descriptive place-name: a pinar is a pine grove, and Chamartín refers to the former municipality of Chamartín de la Rosa. The station opened for Metro lines 1 and 4 on 11 April 2007, with ML1 following on 24 May 2007. Today it lies in Costillares, in Ciudad Lineal, but municipal mapping and local usage still preserve Pinar de Chamartín as a recognizable neighborhood name. The wider place-name also carries a Napoleonic memory: in December 1808 Napoleon stayed in Chamartín, and local tradition attached his name to a large pine in the old grove. That episode enriches the story, but the station name itself is best explained by the pre-existing local toponym.
