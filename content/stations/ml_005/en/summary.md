Álvarez de Villaamil is best explained through the street beside the stop: Calle del Alcalde Álvarez de Villaamil. The name honors Sergio Álvarez Rodríguez de Villaamil, listed by Madrid City Council as mayor of Madrid in 1935. He was a doctor linked to the municipal welfare service as well as a politician during the Second Republic. The station opened with the ML1 light-rail line on 24 May 2007, serving the new northern developments of Sanchinarro and Las Tablas. The current dataset’s attribution to the painter Genaro/Jenaro Pérez Villaamil should be replaced.
