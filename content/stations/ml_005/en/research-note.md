The false painter attribution is understandable because Pérez Villaamil is a much better-known name and the station omits the word Alcalde. However, the relevant Madrid street is explicitly Calle del Alcalde Álvarez de Villaamil, part of a Sanchinarro/Valdefuentes group of streets named for former mayors, and official Madrid material confirms Sergio Álvarez de Villaamil as a 1935 mayor. The remaining archival weak point is the original 2000 municipal street-naming record.
