Ventas station was named for the place where it was built: Las Ventas, historically Las Ventas del Espiritu Santo. The name comes from a roadside venta, and later several ventas and leisure establishments, beside the Abroñigal stream and the road from Madrid toward Alcala de Henares and Aragon. Metro opened the station in 1924, before the present bullring was in regular use. The Plaza de Toros de Las Ventas, begun by Jose Espeliu and completed in 1931 under Manuel Muñoz Monasterio, made the place-name internationally recognizable, but it did not create it.
