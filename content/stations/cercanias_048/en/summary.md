Las Águilas is a place-name station: it serves, and is accessed from, the Las Águilas area of the Latina district. The station was part of the Aluche-Móstoles railway opened in 1976, later absorbed into today’s Cercanías C-5. Local-history material says the neighbourhood was originally grouped with Aluche but soon came to be called Las Águilas in memory of a Pinar de las Águilas in the area. The broader landscape context is secure: this was formerly part of the Carabanchel area, annexed to Madrid in 1948, and reorganised into present districts in the later twentieth century. The specific pinar origin is best treated as probable rather than fully verified until supported by a cadastral, map, street-name, or archival source.
