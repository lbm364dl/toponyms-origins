The station name combines two local place references. 'Parla Centro' points to the central urban area and to the interchange with Cercanias at Parla station. 'Bulevar Norte' refers to the northern boulevard on Calle Real, an important central axis that was also dedicated in 1996 to Francisco Tomas y Valiente. The tram stop belongs to the first phase of the Tranvia de Parla, a system that entered service in 2007. The deeper etymology of Parla is uncertain: a scholarly source considers a Roman personal-name origin, while popular accounts connect the name with 'parlar', to speak.
