Antonio Machado is a Line 7 station opened to the public on 29 March 1999 during the Valdezarza-Pitis extension. Its name matches the nearby Calle de Antonio Machado, part of the literary identity of the Ciudad de los Poetas area in northern Madrid. Antonio Machado Ruiz (Seville, 1875-Collioure, 1939) was one of the major Spanish poets associated with the Generation of '98, author of works such as Soledades and Campos de Castilla. The name is therefore a commemorative personal name, filtered through the local street name rather than a former place-name of the station site.
