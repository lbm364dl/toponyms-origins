The station is best explained as a local-place name: it serves the Acacias area and is associated with the Paseo de las Acacias. The official Madrid street directory lists the paseo in Arganzuela, and nineteenth-century street-name literature already describes the paseo and links its title to the acacia tree. Metro history sources place Acacias in the original Line 5 section inaugurated on 5 June 1968 between Callao and Carabanchel. The exact municipal or Metro naming act has not been located, so the broader etymology is well supported while the formal naming decision remains undocumented.
