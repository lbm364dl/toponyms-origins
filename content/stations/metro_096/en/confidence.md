Official transport and municipal sources verify the current station name, line context, and the existence/location of Paseo de las Acacias, with the historical Callejero dating the paseo to 29 August 1859. A digitized nineteenth-century street-name source supports the botanical origin of the paseo name. Confidence is set to probable rather than fully verified because no original Metro naming resolution was found, and the specific claim about planned nineteenth-century rows of acacias was not directly documented.
