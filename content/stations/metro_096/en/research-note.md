The best evidence chain is: station name Acacias in official transport sources; local street Paseo de las Acacias in the official Madrid callejero; nineteenth-century street-name literature explaining the paseo title through acacia trees. The previous wording's 'tree-lined promenade' wording is plausible but should be kept as a cautious description unless a planting record is found. The current CRTM page lists correspondences in a way that includes connected Embajadores services; for etymology, this should not be confused with Acacias being physically a Line 3 station.
