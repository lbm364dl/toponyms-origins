The station name, location and 2007 opening are well supported by CRTM and Madrid City Council sources, and the two neighbourhood components are clear. The San Fermín colony explanation has consistent local-history and press support, but the available sources did not include a primary municipal naming file for the colony or the station. The Orcasitas surname and Basque derivation are weaker and should be downgraded unless verified in printed toponymic or archival sources.
