The Metro station name is best understood as a practical compound place name: San Fermín plus Orcasur. It was opened with the Line 3 extension on 21 April 2007, at a location intended to serve both neighbourhoods. The San Fermín element predates the station and refers to a housing colony whose postwar street names deliberately echoed Navarre, associated with Federico Mayo Gayarre, the Pamplona-born first director of the Instituto Nacional de la Vivienda. Orcasur, meanwhile, is tied to the old Orcasitas housing settlements and their later remodelling, rather than to a saint or single person.
