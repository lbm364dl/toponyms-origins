The safest revision is to set the station-level etymology to a compound place name and then explain the two components. 'San Fermín' is well supported as a Navarre-themed postwar colony name, but the primary actor Federico Mayo Gayarre should be presented with historical context because he was a Franco-era housing official, not a neutral founder figure. 'Orcasur' should be described through the documented remodelling of older Orcasitas settlements; the surname/landowner and horkatz details should remain a note or lower-confidence subclaim until checked in printed and archival sources.
