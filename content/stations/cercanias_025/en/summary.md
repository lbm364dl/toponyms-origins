Ciempozuelos is a station name inherited directly from the town. Toponomasticon Hispaniae treats the place-name as a transparent compound of cien and pozuelos, with cien meaning not exactly one hundred but an abundance. The municipality's own history connects the name with water supply: springs at Buzanca fed the town fountain, and additional wells were dug because the flow was insufficient. The name is already documented as Çientpoçuelos in 1471, making the water-and-wells explanation old and linguistically well supported.
