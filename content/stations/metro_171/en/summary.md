Peñagrande is best explained as a descriptive place name inherited from the local neighbourhood. In Spanish, peña can mean a large unworked natural stone or a rocky hill, while grande means large. The modern Metro station opened on 29 March 1999 as part of the Line 7 extension from Valdezarza to Pitis, giving direct Metro service to Peñagrande and nearby north-western neighbourhoods. The area's urban history is older: local-history sources trace the Peñagrande colony to 1914 as Colonia El Porvenir-Fuente la Mina, with Peñagrande added to the colony name around 1919. The official Callejero adds modern municipal-road evidence: Camino de Peñagrande is recorded from 1 January 1972, followed by Avenida de Peñagrande from 1 January 1986.
