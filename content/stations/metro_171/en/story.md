Peñagrande station is named after the Peñagrande neighbourhood in Fuencarral-El Pardo, not after a person. The name is a compact descriptive toponym: in Spanish, *peña* is a large natural stone or a rocky hill, and *grande* means large. For public display, the safest reading is therefore “large natural rock” or “large rocky hill.”

The place name belongs to a north-western landscape that was still semi-rural before the twentieth-century expansion of Madrid. Local historical accounts describe late nineteenth-century lomas, dry-farmed fields, tracks, farmhouses and streams around what is now Peñagrande. One older map discussion places the Casa de Beacos where Peñagrande is today, showing that the modern neighbourhood name was not always the dominant label on maps.

The urban colony usually associated with the neighbourhood began in September 1914 under the name Colonia El Porvenir-Fuente la Mina. Local-history research says Peñagrande was added to the colony name in 1919, and by the 1920s the area had a school-chapel, a residents' association and plans for better connections. The first tram reached Peñagrande on 5 August 1932, decades before the Metro station.

The present Metro station opened on 29 March 1999 as part of the Line 7 extension from Valdezarza to Pitis. Regional transport documentation describes the project as bringing Metro access to Valdezarza, El Pilar, Peñagrande, Lacoma and Arroyo Fresno, and locates Peñagrande station around Vereda de Ganapanes. CRTM now lists Peñagrande as a Line 7 station with fare zone A and accessible entrances at Camino de Ganapanes.

The main correction is one of evidence rather than meaning. The literal meaning of the name is secure, but the inspected sources did not prove the specific tradition that one particular large rock served for centuries as a landmark for shepherds and residents. Until a map, cadastral record, municipal file or older local text identifies that feature directly, the etymology should be presented as a descriptive toponym, not as a fully documented landmark story.
