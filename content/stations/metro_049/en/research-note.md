There is an important distinction between the station’s name and Madrid’s current official street Calle del Estrecho de Gibraltar: the official street with that full name is in Ciudad Lineal, not next to Estrecho station. The stronger historical evidence is instead the older local site-name El Estrecho around Cuatro Caminos/Francos Rodríguez, plus the later neighbourhood tradition explaining it through the Tetuán-Gibraltar pun.
