The naming after the barrio/former municipality is well supported by transport and municipal sources. The proposed derivation from canilla/canillas is linguistically plausible and supported by local tradition plus official evidence for historic water galleries around Canillas and Canillejas, but no specialist toponymic source that directly proves the etymology of Canillejas. The official vial rows support the survival of the Canillejas place-name after annexation, but they do not prove the canilla/canillas etymology.
