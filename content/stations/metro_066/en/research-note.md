The best-supported station-specific fact is simple: Metro used the existing barrio/place-name when Line 5 reached Canillejas in 1980. For the deeper etymology, the evidence chain is indirect: RAE supports canilla as a diminutive from canna with spout/tap meanings, Madrid's official water-history page places water catchments near Canillas and Canillejas, and local histories connect those facts to the name. That is enough for a probable public etymology, but not enough for a fully verified linguistic claim.
