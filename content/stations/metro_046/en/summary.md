Prosperidad is a Line 4 station serving Plaza de la Prosperidad, in the Chamartín district. The name belongs first to the neighborhood, whose nineteenth-century origin is associated with Próspero Soynard y Polen. Scholarly and local historical sources describe Soynard as an early landowner-promoter who divided low-value agricultural land near the road to Hortaleza into small plots for modest buyers. A key date is 14 December 1862, when the first sales are recorded to a bricklayer and a carpenter. The etymology is therefore directly place-based for the station, but ultimately personal in the older toponym.
