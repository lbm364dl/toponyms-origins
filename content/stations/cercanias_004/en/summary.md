Madrid-Chamartín-Clara Campoamor is a layered name. The railway station takes Chamartín from the district and former municipality of Chamartín de la Rosa, annexed to Madrid in 1948. The second dedication was made official by Order TMA/1240/2020: Madrid-Chamartín became Madrid-Chamartín-Clara Campoamor, with effects from 24 December 2020. The order explicitly honors Clara Campoamor as promoter and parliamentary defender of women’s voting rights. The older Chamartín element is best treated as probable rather than fully settled for the Madrid site: scholarly toponymy supports an Echa + Martín personal-name formation for related Chamartín names, while municipal material still records several competing popular explanations.
