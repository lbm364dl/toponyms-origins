El País's 16 December 1997 article says Lima was on Line 9, but transport-history sources and the known network chronology indicate that Lima belonged to the old Line 8 before that northern section was integrated into Line 10 in January 1998. This looks like a contemporary press slip rather than evidence against the current Line 10 classification. The 1997 versus 1998 discrepancy is likely due to the difference between the ceremonial renaming and the later line reorganization/map update.
