Multiple independent sources support the origin: Comunidad de Madrid confirms the former Lima name and later Santiago Bernabéu name; contemporary El País coverage documents the 1997 renaming act; Real Madrid documents the stadium's naming for Santiago Bernabéu in 1955 and Bernabéu's presidency. The only unresolved point is whether the dataset should store the ceremonial 1997 date or the later operational/signage year 1998.
