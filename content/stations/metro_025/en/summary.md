Santiago Bernabéu station is named for the football stadium beside Plaza de Lima. The stadium had been named in 1955 for Santiago Bernabéu de Yeste, the Real Madrid player, official and president who promoted the New Chamartín stadium and presided over the club's first great European era. The Metro station opened on 10 June 1982 as Lima, on the old Line 8 built for the north-south Castellana corridor and the 1982 World Cup. On 18 December 1997 regional and municipal officials staged the change from Lima to Santiago Bernabéu as a tribute connected with the stadium's 50th anniversary. Later official pages sometimes give 1998, probably because the line reorganization into Line 10 and signage/map updates followed then.
