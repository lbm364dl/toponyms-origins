The core claim is verified: the station name refers to the parish church of Santa Teresa y Santa Isabel beside the present Glorieta del Pintor Sorolla, and Metro de Madrid's own historical sheet confirms the project name Martínez Campos, the 1919 opening as Iglesia, and the Civil War renaming to Sorolla. The main nuance is that official transport history often describes the original location as Glorieta de Martínez Campos or Martínez Campos (Glorieta de Iglesia), so the older place-name sequence should be stated carefully.
