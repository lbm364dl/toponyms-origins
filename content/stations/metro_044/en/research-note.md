Metro de Madrid’s station sheet uses the wording “Santa Teresa de Jesús y Santa Isabel de Portugal,” while the present parish and Archdiocese commonly identify the parish as Santa Teresa y Santa Isabel and describe Santa Isabel as Santa Isabel de Hungría. For the record’s namesake value, the safest public value is the institutional church name, “Iglesia de Santa Teresa y Santa Isabel,” without specifying which Saint Isabel unless a parish archival source is later consulted.
