The station name is best read first as a local place-name: it serves La Serna, a neighbourhood in the north of Fuenlabrada, and Renfe's station record adds the municipal identifier Fuenlabrada. Behind that neighbourhood name is serna, a historical agricultural word defined by the RAE as a portion of sown land. Toponomasticon Hispaniae treats *SĔNĂRA as a pre-Roman agricultural etymon and notes that the exact linguistic history is complex, although a Celtic explanation is commonly proposed. The station was already being reported in the press as La Serna-Fuenlabrada at its 1983 inauguration, so a claimed earlier name such as Fuenlabrada Apeadero needs stronger documentation before being adopted.
