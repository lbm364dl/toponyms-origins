The core claim is well supported: the station is the Madrid-side gateway and interchange for Line 9 trains toward Arganda del Rey. The deeper etymology of Arganda from Indo-European *arg- is supported by García Sánchez, but the statement that 'del Rey' was simply added in 1996 needs nuance: the royal association goes back to Felipe II's 1583 privilege, while INE confirms that earlier censuses used the municipal name Arganda.
