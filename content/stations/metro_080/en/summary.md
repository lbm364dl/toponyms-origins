Puerta de Arganda is named for its role as the gateway from Madrid toward Arganda del Rey. The station opened on 1 December 1998 as the last stop of the Pavones-Puerta de Arganda extension and was designed to connect Metro with Cercanías at Vicálvaro. In 1999 the next section, operated under the TFM concession, opened from Puerta de Arganda to Arganda del Rey. The place-name Arganda is older than the metro and is best explained as a Celtic or Indo-European name containing *arg-, 'clear, white, bright', a base often used in water-related names.
