The strongest direct evidence for the name’s older origin is local and ecclesiastical rather than archival: the parish explicitly says the estate was already called Virgen del Cortijo and describes the devotional practice in the caretakers’ house. The BOE source confirms later official planning terminology but not the reported 1966 approval. The previous wording’s 'La Colmena' detail is useful cultural context, but it explains a nickname for the colony’s urban form, not the station’s formal name.
