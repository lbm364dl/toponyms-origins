The ML1 station name comes from Colonia Virgen del Cortijo, the residential area it serves between Avenida de Manoteras and Calle Oña. Local parish history says the name predates the modern housing estate: the land was an agricultural property already known as Virgen del Cortijo, with a farmhouse or caretakers’ house where a Marian image was venerated. That image connected the Madrid site with the Virgen del Cortijo devotion of La Rioja, especially Soto en Cameros and Murillo del Río Leza. The station opened with the Pinar de Chamartín-Las Tablas light-rail line on 24 May 2007.
