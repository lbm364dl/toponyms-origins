Virgen del Cortijo station takes its name from the Colonia Virgen del Cortijo, the residential enclave it serves in the north of Madrid. Official transport material places the stop on ML1, the Pinar de Chamartín-Las Tablas light-rail line, and regional infrastructure pages list Virgen del Cortijo among the nine stations opened with that line on 24 May 2007.

The name is not simply a modern street label. The local parish’s historical note says both the parish and the housing were built on an extensive agricultural estate already known as Virgen del Cortijo. On that estate there was a large house for the farm caretakers, and in one room a worn photograph of the Virgin was displayed and visited by neighbours for prayer.

That account links the Madrid devotion to the Virgen del Cortijo of La Rioja. The parish note identifies the photograph as corresponding to the image kept in Murillo del Río Leza, while the present Madrid parish image follows the one from Soto en Cameros. The Soto en Cameros town page describes its Nuestra Señora del Cortijo hermitage as a long-standing sanctuary on the high ground above the village, with medieval and later building phases.

The word **cortijo** also helps explain why the name felt natural on rural land. The RAE defines it as a rural estate with housing and dependencies, typical of large areas of southern Spain. In Madrid’s case, the word appears to have survived in the name of a particular agricultural property before the area was transformed into late-20th-century housing, industry, offices and transport infrastructure.

There is good evidence for the station, the colony, the parish tradition and the existence of later urban planning under the name Plan Parcial de Ordenación "Virgen del Cortijo". The popular account that the neighbourhood was laid out from a 1966 plan and developed by Pryconsa is plausible and widely repeated, but the available sources did not include an official 1966 planning file online; it should be checked in the municipal planning or Archivo de Villa records before being treated as fully verified.
