Pradillo is named for Plaza del Pradillo, the long-established central public space of Móstoles where the MetroSur station was built in 2003. The word points back to prado, a meadow or pleasant open place, from Latin pratum. Municipal information shows the site was already called El Pradillo when the Fuente de los Peces was planned in 1851 and inaugurated in 1852. Local-history accounts add older names such as Plaza del Perezal or Pedreçal and a later form, Pradillo del Caño, but those details should be treated as probable until checked against the original documents.
