Fuenlabrada Central takes its station name from the municipality of Fuenlabrada and from its central interchange position beside the Cercanías station. MetroSur opened on 11 April 2003, and the Community of Madrid describes this stop as placed perpendicular to the Renfe Cercanías station, with accesses on Paseo de Roma and Paseo del Ferrocarril. The deeper origin is the town name Fuenlabrada, analysed by Toponomasticon Hispaniae as a clear Castilian compound of fuente and labrada. The best-supported meaning is a worked or carved stone fountain; the claim that it was made by 'the Moors' is a historical local opinion recorded in the 16th-century Relaciones Topográficas, not independent archaeological proof.
