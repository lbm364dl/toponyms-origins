Los Negrales is a place-name before it is a station name. Adif and Renfe identify the station as Los Negrales in the municipality of Alpedrete, while CRTM places it on line C-8 with access from Calle del Raso. The local EducaMadrid page for Colegio Público Los Negrales explains that the residential area developed gradually from the early 20th century and that its name comes from a nearby pino negral woodland. The botanical label needs care: Spanish dictionaries and forestry literature show that pino negral is a vernacular name, not a single universal scientific term, but a Royal Academy of Engineering publication documents Pinus pinaster as 'pino negral' specifically in Madrid.
