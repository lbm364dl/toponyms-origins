The core claim is well supported: the station takes its name from the Los Negrales residential area, and a local EducaMadrid page says that area was named for a nearby pino negral wood. The wording should be refined because Los Negrales is described locally as extending beyond only Alpedrete, Collado Villalba and Galapagar, and because 'pino negral' is a regional vernacular name that can refer to more than one pine species, although Pinus pinaster is documented under that name in Madrid.
