The station name comes from Los Negrales, the residential area around the stop. Official railway sources list Los Negrales as a station in Alpedrete, and the Madrid transport consortium places it on Cercanías line C-8, with access from Calle del Raso. In everyday geography, however, the name belongs to a broader suburban area near the municipal edges of Alpedrete and Collado Villalba.

The local explanation of the name is botanical. The Colegio Público Los Negrales page on EducaMadrid says that the residential area’s name comes from a nearby wood of pino negral. In Spanish, negral means darkish or darker than related trees or products, and pino negral is a traditional pine name whose exact species can vary by region.

For Madrid, the best botanical support points to Pinus pinaster, also known as maritime pine, resin pine or pino resinero. A forestry lecture published by the Real Academia de Ingeniería notes that Pinus pinaster was known as pino negral in Madrid, among other provinces, while also warning that the same vernacular name is used for Pinus nigra in other regions. That makes the old place-name plausible without pretending that the popular name is botanically unambiguous everywhere.

Los Negrales also belongs to the history of summer settlement in the Sierra de Guadarrama foothills. EducaMadrid describes it as a mostly detached-house residential area whose oldest houses date from the first half of the 20th century, growing gradually from the early 1900s. It also notes that many homes are second residences, so the population rises sharply in summer and holiday periods.

The exact date when the station was assigned the name Los Negrales was not found in an inspected official naming order. The railway context is older: the Villalba-Segovia line opened to service in 1888, while the present stop is now recorded by Adif, Renfe and CRTM under the Los Negrales name. The etymology is therefore best treated as verified for the place-name origin and current station name, but with a note that the precise station naming date remains unverified.
