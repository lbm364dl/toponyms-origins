The La Ballena tram stop takes its name from its immediate surroundings in northern Parla: the Parque de la Ballena and the La Ballena area by Calle Real. The deeper origin is a local legend about the former Laguna de Parla, where muleteers on the Toledo-Madrid road supposedly saw a floating pack saddle and took it for a whale. The Ayuntamiento de Parla includes this story in its urban route as part of the municipality's historical and cultural heritage. The park's large whale-tail sculpture made the old tale visible in the modern city.
