The station name comes from the old Calle del Pacífico, the historic name of today’s Avenida de la Ciudad de Barcelona. Metro de Madrid’s historical material explains that the old road to Vallecas was renamed Calle del Pacífico, alongside other nearby street names linked to Spanish naval actions in the 1865 war against Chile and Peru. The Metro station opened on 8 May 1923 on Line 1’s Atocha-Puente de Vallecas extension, so it inherited an already established local toponym. After the Spanish Civil War, the street name was changed to Avenida Ciudad de Barcelona, but the station and neighbourhood preserved Pacífico. The best-supported reading is therefore not the adjective “peaceful,” but a nineteenth-century commemorative military toponym.
