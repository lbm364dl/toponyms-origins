The strongest station-specific source is Metro de Madrid’s “Huellas del pasado” PDF, because it directly ties the station’s inherited toponym to the former Calle del Pacífico and to the Chile-Peru war context. A BOE-hosted Gaceta de Madrid issue confirms the street name was already current by 18 November 1866, but not the act that created it. Some secondary sources give 1865 or 1866 as the street-naming year and 1944 as the later change to Ciudad de Barcelona; those dates should be treated as useful leads until checked in municipal street-name documentation.
