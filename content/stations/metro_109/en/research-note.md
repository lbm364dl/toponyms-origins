The best inspected bridge source is the municipal museum catalogue, which gives a 1729 design by Pedro de Ribera for the bridge over the Abroñigal on the Camino de Vallecas. Local-history pages repeat the 1731 completion, masonry solution, 65,000 reales and 1751 repair by Manuel de Villegas, but these should be treated as secondary until checked against the Archivo de Villa citation. The 1206 claim in the input appears to be a weak inherited detail from material on Valnegral/Moratalaz and Calatrava lands; it is not needed to explain the station name.
