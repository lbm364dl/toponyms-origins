The core claim is correct: the station name comes from the Puente de Vallecas place-name, ultimately the bridge over the Abroñigal on the road to Vallecas. However, the precise 65,000-reales cost and the 1206 claim need caution: the strongest available source documents Pedro de Ribera's 1729 design, while the 1206 material appears to concern Valnegral/Moratalaz rather than the station name directly.
