San Cristóbal is a Line 3 Metro station opened with the Legazpi-Villaverde Alto extension on 21 April 2007. The Community of Madrid describes that extension as placing a station opposite San Cristóbal de los Ángeles, so the immediate naming source is the neighbourhood rather than a newly created Metro name. The deeper name is religious: San Cristóbal is Saint Christopher, a saint traditionally treated as protector of travellers, transport workers and drivers. The neighbourhood itself grew from one of Madrid’s major poblados dirigidos, planned from 1958 and built in phases from 1959 on land formerly occupied by the La Norah brickworks, whose chimney remains a local landmark.
