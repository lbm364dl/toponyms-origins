The input claim is strongest when reduced to the station's direct naming chain: Henares station is named from the Henares hydronym, and that hydronym is a Romance plural of henar. Toponomasticon Hispaniae explicitly says the hydronym is relatively recent and replaced an earlier name, which makes the Arabic/Guadalajara note plausible as historical context but not necessary for the station card. Contemporary MetroEste reporting also notes that Henares opened in the 2007 extension while Hospital del Henares did not initially enter service, a useful chronology detail if the dataset tracks opening and naming separately.
