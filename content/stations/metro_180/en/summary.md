Henares station, on Line 7 in San Fernando de Henares, takes its name from the nearby Henares River and the wider landscape named after it. Toponomasticon Hispaniae explains Henares as the plural of the common noun henar, a place populated by hay or hayfields. The word ultimately belongs to the Latin family of fenum, 'hay', although the collective place-name form is considered Romance rather than ancient Latin. Historical documentation shows earlier spellings such as Senares, probably from a redivision of los henares. A secondary tradition connects the Islamic-period name Wad al-Hayara/Guadalajara with the river or valley, but the best-supported direct etymology for the modern station name is the river-name Henares.
