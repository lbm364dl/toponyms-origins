Cantoblanco Universidad is named for the Cantoblanco area on Madrid's northern edge and for the Universidad Autonoma de Madrid campus there. The UAM was created in 1968 and its Cantoblanco campus opened on 25 October 1971; Renfe inaugurated the rail branch and station serving the university on 3 December 1975. The simple reading of Cantoblanco as 'white stone' is plausible as folk interpretation, but a specialist study of Madrid Celtic toponyms argues that the name probably is not based on identifiable white stones in the area. For public use, the safest explanation is that the station is named after the established place-name Cantoblanco plus the university campus, while the deeper etymology remains probable rather than certain.
