Pitis is best understood as a station named after the place where it was built: the Pitis area between Mirasierra, Arroyo del Fresno and the approaches to El Pardo. Municipal land inventories still record rural-style descriptions such as Pitis, Huertas de Pitis and Hoya del Rencajo o Pitis, confirming that it was a local place-name rather than a name invented for Metro. The railway station was already known as Pitis before Metro line 7 reached it on 29 March 1999. No authoritative etymology for the word itself was found; pine-based or Greek-derived explanations are interesting but unsupported.
