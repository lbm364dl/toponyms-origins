The basic place-name explanation is sound: the Metro station is named for Villaverde Bajo and the well-known road/transport crossing called El Cruce. However, the previous wording overstates the certainty of the Latin phrase 'villa viridis' and contains a likely date error for the early railway history: the Madrid-Aranjuez railway opened on 9 February 1851, not 1861; COAM separately identifies a notable MZA halt at Villaverde Bajo from 1893 and a larger replacement opened in 1931.
