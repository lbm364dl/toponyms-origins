Iglesia Centro is a descriptive religious place-name: it identifies the tram stop by the old parish church in central Parla. The Consorcio Regional de Transportes lists the stop at Calle Real, 28, and a municipal planning document describes it as located between the affected Calle Real plots and the Iglesia de Nuestra Señora de la Asunción. The church itself is one of Parla's historic landmarks, dated by the municipality to the sixteenth century and largely rebuilt during the twentieth century. A stronger art-historical source confirms that Juan de Borgoña contracted an altarpiece for the church of Parla on 24 April 1522, although that work has not survived.
