The core claim is well supported for the station: MLO places Montepríncipe station by Avenida de Montepríncipe at the southern edge of the Montepríncipe residential development. The deeper explanation linking the place-name to the Monte de Boadilla and the Rúspoli princely lineage is plausible and locally documented, but I did not find a primary naming act explicitly saying that the development was coined from the Rúspoli title.
