Montepríncipe is best explained first as a place-name: the ML3 station was named for Avenida de Montepríncipe and the residential development beside it. Metro Ligero Oeste describes the station as being at the southern edge of that development, in the municipality of Alcorcón and close to Boadilla del Monte. The older background is the Monte de Boadilla, a former wooded estate acquired by the Infante Luis Antonio de Borbón in 1761 and later inherited through the Chinchón-Godoy-Rúspoli line. Local community history says Carlos Rúspoli y Caro sold part of the estate for the Monte de las Encinas and Montepríncipe developments in the 1960s. The idea that the name combines the woodland, monte, with the princely Rúspoli connection is likely, but should be marked as an inference unless an original planning or developer naming document is found.
