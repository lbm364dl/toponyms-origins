The station name is best explained as a local place name in Pozuelo de Alarcón, not as a commemorative dedication. Official transport and municipal pages place the stop by the M-502/Carretera de Carabanchel, next to Hospital Quirón and the residential area of Prado de la Vega. Linguistically, prado is a meadow or irrigated pasture, from Latin pratum, while vega is a low, flat, fertile land generally watered by a river or canal. Archaeological sources show that nearby Boadilla has an older La Vega toponym and late antique/Visigothic sites along the Arroyo de la Vega, but the evidence found so far supports that as context rather than the direct naming act for this specific stop.
