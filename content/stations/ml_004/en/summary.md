Antonio Saura is an ML1 Metro Ligero stop in Sanchinarro, in the Valdefuentes area of Hortaleza. Its name matches the nearby Calle del Pintor Antonio Saura, documented in municipal and transport sources, and refers to Antonio Saura Atarés (Huesca, 1930-Cuenca, 1998). Saura was a self-taught painter, writer, illustrator and stage designer who helped renew Spanish art after the Civil War. In 1957 he co-founded the El Paso group, which promoted Spanish Informalism and contemporary European and American painting languages. The station opened with the Pinar de Chamartín-Sanchinarro-Las Tablas light-rail line on 24 May 2007.
