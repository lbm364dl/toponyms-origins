Vicente Aleixandre station is named after the Spanish poet Vicente Aleixandre (1898-1984), a member of the Generation of 1927 and winner of the 1977 Nobel Prize in Literature. Metro and the Community of Madrid changed the former name, Metropolitano, for service on 1 December 2018. The official explanation joined two ideas: Aleixandre had lived nearby at Calle Vicente Aleixandre 3, and the old name could be confused with Estadio Metropolitano on Line 7. The name also ties the station to Velintonia, the poet's house and a major meeting place for several generations of Spanish poets.
