The core claim is verified: the station was renamed from Metropolitano to Vicente Aleixandre on 1 December 2018 because the poet lived nearby and to avoid confusion with Estadio Metropolitano. The Nobel quotation and literary identity are correct, but the claim that he was bedridden for much of his life is overstated, and the change is better described as a specific local and wayfinding decision than a broad artist-honouring programme.
