The station-name etymology is unusually strong because the official renaming note gives both the commemorated person and the operational reason for replacing Metropolitano. A useful nuance is that Metropolitano itself was not arbitrary: the surrounding Parque/Colonia del Metropolitano was developed by the Compania Urbanizadora Metropolitana, and Aleixandre's Velintonia stood in that same urban landscape. As of 4 June 2026, the strongest update to the input row is the 13 May 2026 official BIC Intangible Heritage declaration for Velintonia, following earlier BIP protection in 2022 and the Community's later acquisition of the property.
