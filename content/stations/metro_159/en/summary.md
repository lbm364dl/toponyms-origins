The station name is best explained as a local descriptive toponym inherited from the Colonia Alto del Arenal. In Spanish, alto can mean a raised site or high ground, while arenal means a sandy expanse. Official regional material records the Colonia Alto del Arenal as a social-housing estate built in the late 1950s and allocated in 1961 by the Obra Sindical del Hogar. The Metro station opened much earlier than the current row says: 7 April 1994, as part of the Line 1 extension from Portazgo to Miguel Hernández. The exact documentary act assigning the station name was not found, so the station-name link to the colony is probable rather than fully proven by a naming decree.
