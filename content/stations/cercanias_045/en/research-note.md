The previous wording's Banu Salim and Middle March details are plausible historical context but were not needed to verify the station name and should not be foregrounded unless backed by a specialist medieval source. The most useful revision is to separate the immediate station etymology, which is simply the city name, from the deeper and somewhat more nuanced city-name etymology: Arabic Wad al-Hayara / Wadi al-Hijara, traditionally 'stone' related and linked by municipal sources to Arriaca.
