The core claim is well supported: the station is named for the city of Guadalajara, whose medieval Arabic name is commonly given as Wad al-Hayara or Wadi al-Hijara and understood as a stone-related hydronym/toponym. The row should be tightened: authoritative municipal material presents the Arabic name as a translation of the older Arriaca, not merely as a descriptive nickname for the gravelly Henares.
