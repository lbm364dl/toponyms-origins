El Carrascal is a Line 12 MetroSur station on Avenida Rey Juan Carlos I in Leganés. Its name comes from the surrounding El Carrascal neighbourhood or urban sector, not from a person. In Spanish, carrascal means a place or wood populated by carrascas; the RAE defines carrasca as a usually small holm oak or a clump of it, from the pre-Roman root karr-. Urban-history sources place El Carrascal within Leganés's planned eastward growth: the residential estate was approved in 1974, after the earlier Zarzaquemada plan of 1968. The precise municipal act that assigned the station name was not found, so the best-supported statement is that Metro adopted the established local place name when MetroSur opened in 2003.
