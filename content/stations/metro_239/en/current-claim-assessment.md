The main claim is well supported: the MetroSur station takes the name of the El Carrascal area of Leganés, and the word carrascal means a place or wood with carrascas. The 1981 neighbourhood-development detail is plausible but is weaker than the linguistic and station-location evidence; stronger available sources document the 1974 approval of the El Carrascal residential estate rather than the first occupied buildings.
