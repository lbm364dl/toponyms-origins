Azuqueca station carries the shortened name of Azuqueca de Henares, the Guadalajara municipality where it stands. Toponomasticon Hispaniae identifies Azuqueca as an Arabic place name from sukayka, meaning a small road, path, or alleyway, and considers the 'small market' theory less convincing. The second element, Henares, comes from the river name, which itself derives from henar, a meadow or place with hay. The railway station belongs to the Madrid commuter network, but the name preserves a much older landscape of roads, river plain, and medieval settlement.
