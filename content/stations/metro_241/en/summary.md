The Metro station name is modern and functional: Leganés Central identifies Line 12’s stop at the central railway interchange of Leganés, beside the C-5 station on Calle Virgen del Camino. MetroSur opened on 11 April 2003, and Comunidad de Madrid describes this station as the Leganés stop that enables interchange with Cercanías. The older name Leganés is traditionally linked to a muddy or silty lagoon at the place of settlement, an explanation already attributed to local memory in the sixteenth-century Relaciones Topográficas. However, Toponomasticon Hispaniae warns that the derivation is not straightforward and lists other possible explanations, so the deeper etymology should be presented as probable or traditional rather than fully verified.
