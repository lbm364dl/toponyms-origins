El Escorial station is named directly after the municipality where it stands. The older place name has two main scholarly explanations: a derivation from Latin scoria, meaning slag or waste, and a derivation from Latin aesculus/esculus, connected with oaks, holm oaks, or gall oaks. The most direct current toponymic authority consulted, Toponomasticon Hispaniae, favours the scoria explanation and points to the historical Bosque de la Herrería, formerly the Dehesa de las Ferrerías de Fuentelámparas. Municipal and older Cervantes-centre summaries give more weight to the tree-name theory, so the deeper etymology should be displayed as probable rather than absolutely settled.
