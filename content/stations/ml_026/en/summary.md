Cocheras is a functional, descriptive station name. Metro Ligero Oeste identifies it as the ML3 stop on Calle Edgar Neville and as the closest stop to the company's depot and offices. The depot is not only a parking place for trams but a maintenance centre for the fleet. The station opened with Metro Ligero Oeste on 27 July 2007, a few weeks after light rail returned to Madrid in May 2007. Its name also preserves an older transport word: coche originally meant a carriage, and RAE derives it from Hungarian kocsi.
