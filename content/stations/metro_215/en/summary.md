La Fortuna station opened on Line 11 on 5 October 2010 and was named for the Leganés district it serves. The district was created around 1960 on the Madrid-Leganés edge by Domingo Dos Santos, known as "El Portugués". Comunidad de Madrid material on the Line 11 extension states that Dos Santos named the barrio after his Madrid-born wife, Fortunata González Arroyo. The station name is therefore place-derived, with a personal origin one step behind the barrio name.
