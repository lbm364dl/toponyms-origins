Lista is named through an older Madrid street name rather than through the current street signage above it. The station opened on 17 September 1932 on the Goya-Diego de León branch, then part of Line 2B, near the former Calle de Lista. That street was dedicated to Alberto Rodríguez de Lista y Aragón, a Sevillian intellectual of the late Enlightenment and early liberal nineteenth century. In 1955 the street was renamed José Ortega y Gasset, but the Metro station kept the older name. Since 1958 the station has belonged to Line 4.
