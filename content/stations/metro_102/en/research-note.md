The existing wording's broad narrative is sound, but the most precise explanation is indirect: the Metro station is named after the former street name, and the former street was named after Alberto Rodríguez de Lista y Aragón. The station itself was on the Goya-Diego de León branch opened in 1932 as Line 2B and only became a Line 4 station in 1958. The claim about Bécquer is plausible in literary sources but more nuanced than the San Mateo-only narrative, since Bécquer’s connection belongs to Lista’s later Sevillian teaching and influence rather than the 1821 Madrid San Mateo cohort.
