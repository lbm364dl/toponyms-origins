Hospital Severo Ochoa is a descriptive-eponymic MetroSur station name: it identifies the stop by the nearby Hospital Universitario Severo Ochoa in Leganés. The hospital opened for care in 1987 and was officially inaugurated in March 1988 by Severo Ochoa himself, accompanied by national government officials. Ochoa, born in Luarca in 1905 and deceased in Madrid in 1993, shared the 1959 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Arthur Kornberg for discoveries on the biological synthesis of RNA and DNA. The station opened with Line 12, MetroSur, on 11 April 2003.
