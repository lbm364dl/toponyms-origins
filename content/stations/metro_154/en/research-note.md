The station's name should be treated as a two-step eponym: operationally it identifies the hospital serving this part of Leganés, while culturally it preserves the memory of Severo Ochoa. Comunidad de Madrid's Line 12 page interestingly labels the mural section simply 'Severo Ochoa' and describes 'ADN y Lunas', which suggests the station's decorative program intentionally reinforced the scientist rather than only the hospital as a wayfinding landmark.
