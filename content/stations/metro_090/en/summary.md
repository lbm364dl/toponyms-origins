Sevilla is not named directly for a Madrid neighbourhood, but for Calle de Sevilla, the short street linking Plaza de Canalejas and Calle de Alcalá. The street had previously been Calle Ancha de los Peligros, paired awkwardly with another nearby street called Angosta de los Peligros. A historical study based on municipal records says Madrid City Council changed the name on 4 May 1849 to avoid that duplication. The metro station opened with Line 2's first Sol-Ventas section on 14 June 1924, so the station inherited an established street name.
