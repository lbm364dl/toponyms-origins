A useful distinction for dataset cleanup is direct versus ultimate naming. Directly, Metro’s station is named after a place: La Elipa. Ultimately, the place-name may preserve a personal name, Phelipa/Felipa, but accessible evidence is uneven. The strongest documentary item found is not the Phelipa story itself but the old form “Helipa, hoy Elipa” in a 1510 mayorazgo as reported by Álvarez y Baena and repeated in later research. Recent Vicálvaro research also warns that later noble claims around La Elipa y Canaleja included exaggerated or unsupported jurisdictional assertions, so the public text should avoid implying that every part of the noble-origin story is equally proven.
