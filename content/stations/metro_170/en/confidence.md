The immediate station-name origin is verified by transport and municipal sources: the station serves and is named for La Elipa. The deeper personal etymology is probable because multiple local/toponymic sources connect La Elipa with Phelipa/Felipa de Vargas and because archival-derived sources document the old Helipa/Elipa estate in the Vargas-Luján patrimony, but the available sources did not include an inspected primary document that directly states the Phelipa naming act.
