The station name comes from Calle Estrella Polar in Parla Este. Parla's municipal inventory lists this street in the Residencial Este area alongside streets named for planets, stars, constellations, and zodiac signs. The tram stop also fits its immediate setting: the Estrella Polar platforms appear on the line as Estrella Polar Norte and Estrella Polar Sur, near Avenida de las Estrellas and Avenida de los Planetas. The astronomical name refers to Polaris, Earth's current North Star, which appears almost fixed in the northern sky because it lies close to the north celestial pole.
