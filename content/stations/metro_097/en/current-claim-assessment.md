The existing claim is substantially correct: the station takes its name from the Marqués de Vadillo, Francisco Antonio de Salcedo y Aguirre, the Madrid corregidor closely associated with the Puente de Toledo and Pedro de Ribera's early-18th-century works. It should be corrected from c.1660 to 1646, and the bridge chronology should note that official municipal pages give both 1727 and 1732 as completion dates depending on whether the main bridge or later completion phase is meant.
