Santa María de la Alameda-Peguerinos is best explained first as a transport name: it identifies a railway station in Santa María de la Alameda that also serves Peguerinos, across the Ávila side of the Guadarrama mountains. Adif uses the full compound station name, while current Cercanías public information often shortens the stop to Santa María de la Alameda. The Santa María element is associated with the parish church of Nuestra Señora de la Alameda, although the municipality's own history notes uncertainty and a possible origin in an early settler's surname. Peguerinos is probably connected with peguero or peguera, words documented by the RAE for people and places involved in extracting or making pitch from pine wood.
