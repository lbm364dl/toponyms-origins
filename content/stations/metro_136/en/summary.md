The station name is best explained through the local urban toponym Las Musas: the official Madrid street gazetteer records Calle de las Musas, and municipal planning material identifies Colonia Las Musas in district 20, San Blas-Canillejas. The name ultimately refers to the Muses, the sister goddesses of poetry, music, history, dance and other arts and knowledge in Greco-Roman mythology. Nearby street names such as Lucano, Pitágoras, La Ilíada and Troya show a wider classical and literary naming cluster. Metro de Madrid’s own archival material records Las Musas as part of the first Line 7 section, inaugurated on 17 July 1974 between Pueblo Nuevo and Las Musas, with the station initially serving as the line terminus. The exact municipal act choosing the station name was not found, so the direct place-name link is verified by location and official toponymy rather than by a naming decree.
