The most important revision is to distinguish direct and ultimate naming layers: direct = local street/colony; ultimate = mythological Muses. The official Madrid GIS query returned Calle de las Musas with TOP_DT_FCH_ALTA = -157766400000 milliseconds, equivalent to 1965-01-01 UTC, but GIS high-dates can be administrative normalizations and should not be treated as the original street-naming vote without archival confirmation.
