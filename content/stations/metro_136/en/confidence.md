The direct place-name is supported by official Madrid street GIS data for Calle de las Musas, municipal planning references to Colonia Las Musas, and Metro archival documentation for the 1974 Line 7 terminus. The mythological meaning of the Muses is supported by a standard reference source. The only caveat is that I did not find the original Metro naming act explicitly stating 'named after Calle de las Musas.'
