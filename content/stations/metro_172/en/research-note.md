There is a one-day discrepancy in sources for the 1975 Line 7 extension: the Comunidad de Madrid page gives 18 March 1975 for the Pueblo Nuevo-Avenida de América section, while the CRTM historical map and station-specific secondary sources give 17 March 1975 for Parque de las Avenidas. For this station row, the station-specific CRTM historical map date is the best value to retain unless a primary inauguration document is found.
