Parque de las Avenidas station is named after the surrounding Parque de las Avenidas area in La Guindalera, district of Salamanca. The name is better understood as a modern urban toponym than as the name of an old historical park: architectural sources describe it as a 1956 residential estate promoted by CIOHSA after Spain's limited-rent housing legislation. Its street pattern explains the name, with Avenida de Bruselas, Avenida de Bonn, Avenida de Brasilia and other nearby roads forming the identity of the neighbourhood. The present municipal park on Avenida de Brasilia and the station on Avenida de Bruselas both preserve that place name. The official Callejero adds only a cautious clue: it has a one-day Plaza del Parque de las Avenidas row dated 18-19 October 1960, which points to municipal nomenclature activity but needs the underlying file for interpretation.
