La Moraleja station opened with MetroNorte in 2007 and bears the name of the nearby La Moraleja area of Alcobendas. The old place-name predates the modern luxury development: an 1778 map already refers to the bosque llamado de La Moraleja between Alcobendas and Hortaleza. Toponomasticon Hispaniae treats Moraleja as a likely phytotoponym, built from Latin MORA with collective and diminutive suffixes. The modern urbanization was ordered as a garden-city development from 1946, preserving the prestige of the older rural name.
