The station's etymology is a modern compound name rather than a deep historical coinage. 'Arganzuela' contributes the district identity and a long toponymic history; 'Planetario' contributes the immediate landmark. The station is near the Planetario, although the Planetario's own current public-transport page highlights Méndez Álvaro rather than Arganzuela-Planetario, likely because of Cercanías and interchange access. The station lies in the broader Legazpi/Delicias-Metales area, but official sources consistently place it in the district of Arganzuela.
