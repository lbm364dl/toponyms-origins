Arganzuela-Planetario is a modern compound station name: it identifies the district and points riders toward one of its clearest local landmarks, the Planetario de Madrid. Official Madrid sources place the station on Line 6 between Legazpi and Méndez Álvaro and record its inauguration on 26 January 2007. The planetarium itself is an Ayuntamiento de Madrid facility in Parque Tierno Galván, inaugurated on 29 September 1986 and later renewed technologically in 2016-2017. The Arganzuela part of the name reaches further back, to the old Dehesa de Arganzuela, documented by the city as a historic landscape from the late fifteenth century.
