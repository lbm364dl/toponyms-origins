El Bercial is a MetroSur station on Line 12, opened with the line on 11 April 2003 and named for the neighbourhood in north-west Getafe. The neighbourhood name is older than the modern housing district: local archival material says municipal records preserve clear references to the place-name from the eighteenth century. The best-supported etymology connects bercial with berceo or barceo, also called albardín, an esparto-like grass of Spanish steppe landscapes. A local school page notes another idea, that the modern name may derive from an older map form, Hervestial, but this remains less securely demonstrated.
