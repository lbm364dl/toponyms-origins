The core claim is well supported: the station is named after the municipality, and the municipality's name is a transparent Castilian toponym meaning a deep or low-lying sheepfold/pastoral enclosure. The 13th-century Segovian shepherd story is plausible and appears in official municipal history, but it should be presented as tradition or belief rather than a fully documented foundation fact; the 'stained by dung' explanation overstates the lexical evidence.
