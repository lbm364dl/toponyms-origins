Móstoles station is named for the city of Móstoles, on the C-5 line southwest of Madrid. The place-name is securely medieval: an official local-history account cites an October 1144 document referring to turrem de Monsteles, between Freguezedos and the road from Madrid to Olmos. One established explanation derives the name from late Latin monisterium or monisteriis, connected with monasteries and perhaps a Mozarabic form. Other specialists have proposed alternatives, including an old landscape term meaning a small pasture or an anthroponym behind the phrase 'Torre de Mósteles'. The safest public wording is that the station is named after the municipality, whose own name has several plausible but unresolved etymologies.
