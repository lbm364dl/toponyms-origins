Mirasierra-Paco de Lucía is a compound transport name. 'Mirasierra' anchors the station in the northern Madrid neighbourhood and old residential colony of that name, while 'Paco de Lucía' honors Francisco Sánchez Gómez, the Algeciras-born flamenco guitarist who had a family home in Mirasierra. The Metro station on Line 9 had been associated during planning with Calle Costa Brava, but on 27 February 2014 the regional government announced that it would carry Paco de Lucía's name. Metro opened the station on 25 March 2015; the Cercanías station above it was inaugurated on 5 February 2018 and entered service the following day.
