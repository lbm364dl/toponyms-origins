The naming in honor of Paco de Lucía is confirmed by contemporary press citing the Community of Madrid's Council of Government announcement, and the 2018 Cercanías station name and intermodal role are confirmed by CRTM, Adif, Renfe and Comunidad de Madrid sources. The only weaker element is the exact historical etymology of 'Mirasierra', which is transparent and locally repeated but not tied here to a primary naming document.
