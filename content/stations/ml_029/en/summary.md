The station name is local rather than commemorative: Metro Ligero Oeste places Ventorro del Cano at the junction of Calle del Tranvía and Calle Arroyomolinos, inside the industrial estate of the same name. The older place-name comes from a ventorro, a small or humble roadside inn, remembered in Alcorcón local history as a stop, post-house, carriage-changing point and crossroads. The best available source says that, by the episode set in 1828, the inn was run by José 'El Cano', whose byname gave the place the name still used today. The site remained recognizable through a surviving well until 1971, and official regional documentation still refers to the paraje and ruins of the ventorro.
