The station's name is best classified as a place-based name after the Teatro Real rather than a broad historical label. The previous wording's namesake value should remain Teatro Real, but the public copy should avoid implying that the theatre was built directly on 'royal washhouses'; official heritage sources distinguish the public Caños del Peral washhouses, the later Coliseo/Teatro de los Caños del Peral, and then the Teatro Real.
