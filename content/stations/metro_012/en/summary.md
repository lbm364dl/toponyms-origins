Ópera station is named for the Teatro Real, the opera house that stands next to Plaza de Isabel II and opposite the Royal Palace. The station opened as Isabel II in 1925, matching the square above it, but it was renamed Ópera on 24 June 1931 during the Second Republic. In 1937 it briefly became Fermín Galán, again following the politically renamed square, before returning to Ópera on 1 April 1939. The theatre itself opened on 19 November 1850 with Donizetti's La favorite and remains the clearest reason for the station's present name.
