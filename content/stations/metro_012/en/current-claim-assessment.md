The core claim is right: the station name refers to the nearby Teatro Real, Madrid's opera house. However, the chronology needs correction because the station was first renamed Ópera on 24 June 1931, then Fermín Galán from 5 June 1937 to 1 April 1939, and then Ópera again; the previous wording also omits the Ramal service and overstates the 'royal washhouses' point.
