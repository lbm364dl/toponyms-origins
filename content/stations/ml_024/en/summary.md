José Isbert is a Metro Ligero Oeste ML3 station in Ciudad de la Imagen, Pozuelo de Alarcón, located on the street of the same name. The name points to José Ysbert Alvarruiz, better known professionally as José Isbert or Pepe Isbert, a Madrid-born Spanish actor active from the early twentieth century until the 1960s. He became especially famous through mid-century Spanish cinema, including Luis García Berlanga's Bienvenido, Mister Marshall and El verdugo. The station opened with Metro Ligero Oeste on 27 July 2007, in an audiovisual district whose street names and institutions reinforce the film connection.
