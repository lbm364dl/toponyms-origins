The best-supported formulation is that the stop is named after the street, and the street is almost certainly part of Ciudad de la Imagen's audiovisual naming environment. Several nearby names also evoke film figures or cinema uses, such as Juan de Orduña, Luis Buñuel, Fernando Rey, Edgar Neville, Ciudad del Cine, and the Filmoteca Española area. I did not find a primary municipal street-naming resolution, so the exact date when Calle José Isbert was approved should remain flagged for archival review.
