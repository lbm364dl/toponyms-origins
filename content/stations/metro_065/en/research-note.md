The previous wording is strongest as a station-name explanation, not as a final etymology of the word Barajas. Caridad Arias is the most station-relevant modern source found because it directly addresses the Madrid toponym; however, his pre-Roman proposal should still be presented as a scholarly argument rather than settled consensus. The Community of Madrid page gives 14 June 1999 for the Campo de las Naciones-Aeropuerto de Barajas section, while contemporary press and transport-history listings separate Barajas station as opening on 7 September 1999.
