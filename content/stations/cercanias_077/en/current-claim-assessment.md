The core claim is well supported: the Cercanías stop takes the municipality's name, and municipal sources explain Los Molinos as a toponym derived from water-powered flour mills on the Guadarrama. The wording should be nuanced because the 1546 Álvaro de Mena license predates the Monastery of El Escorial works, and another published transcription places that license in 'la Foz' on the Manzanares then called Guadarrama, so it should not be presented as a simple, conclusive origin document for the town without qualification.
