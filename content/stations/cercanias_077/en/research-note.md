The municipal narrative is coherent and probably right, but it currently mixes several chronological layers. Fernando Colón's 1517-1523 reference suggests the place-name existed before the 1546 license; the Monastery of El Escorial works, often invoked as an economic driver, began in 1563 and therefore cannot explain the 1546 document. A stronger dataset wording would say that the name refers to historic water mills on the Guadarrama and that later records, especially the 1751 Catastro, confirm substantial milling activity.
