Los Molinos is first of all a station name borrowed from the municipality. The town's name is traditionally and officially explained by the flour mills that used the water of the Guadarrama in the upper valley. Municipal history cites a 1546 license for a cube mill and later records show milling as a real local activity; the Catastro de Ensenada of 1751 records seven flour mills, one of them ruined. The rail stop itself belonged to the Villalba-Segovia line, opened in 1888, and historical railway material records it as Los Molinos-Guadarrama.
