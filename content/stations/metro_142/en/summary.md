Vinateros station is named for Calle del Camino de los Vinateros, where its entrances stand in Moratalaz; the official historical Callejero records that street from 1961 and a short-lived Camino de los Vinateros row in 1949. The older road and nearby place-name are documented in nineteenth-century cartography of the area, especially in references to the 1862 IGN and military plans used by local historians. In Spanish, vinatero means someone involved in wine production or trade, which fits the traditional explanation that wine from the Arganda area and beyond came toward Madrid along this corridor. The broad link with Madrid wine country is well supported, although the precise act by which the street acquired the name has not been found.
