The immediate origin from Calle/Camino de los Vinateros is strongly supported by CRTM station access data, the station's location, and official Callejero rows for the road/street names. Historical cartographic references support the older road/toponym, and the RAE plus wine-history sources support the occupational meaning. Confidence is probable rather than verified because no official street-naming decree or Metro naming rationale explicitly tying the road name to wine merchants was found.
