The source row's main narrative is strong for the Alto/Bajo distinction, but it compresses several layers. The direct station etymology is simply the existing neighbourhood/historic core; the 1954 date belongs to Villaverde's annexation and the Alto/Bajo naming distinction, while 2017 belongs to the official administrative barrio renaming from San Andrés. Barreiros is useful context for Villaverde's industrial identity, but the COAM guide says the huge 1,300,000 m2 complex resulted from later reforms, expansions and new construction after the initial acquisition, so it should not be phrased as if a 1,300,000 m2 factory was simply built in 1954.
