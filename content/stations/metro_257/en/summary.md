The station is named after Villaverde Alto, now officially Villaverde Alto, Casco Histórico de Villaverde. Madrid City Council material identifies it as the oldest nucleus of Villaverde and says that, when Villaverde was integrated into Madrid in 1954, the name Villaverde Alto was used in contrast with Villaverde Bajo, the new railway-side area across the old Andalucía road. The deeper etymology of Villaverde is less certain than a simple 'green town' translation: Toponomasticon Hispaniae links the verde element to Latin viridis, while Madrid-focused commentary cites both the greenery explanation and a theory connected with the former Santiago el Verde. The transport name itself is therefore secure, but the ultimate origin of Villaverde should be presented as probable rather than fully settled.
