The direct claim is well supported: the station takes its name from Villaverde Alto, the historic core of Villaverde, so called after Villaverde was incorporated into Madrid in 1954 to distinguish it from Villaverde Bajo. The literal Latin formula 'villa viridis' and some industrial details are weaker: 'verde' is securely linked to Latin viridis, but published Madrid sources also note an alternative explanation connected with Santiago el Verde.
