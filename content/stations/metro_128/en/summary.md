Mirasierra is best treated first as a place-name station: Metro named it for the Mirasierra neighbourhood in Fuencarral-El Pardo. The neighbourhood name is descriptive and is commonly explained through the mountain views enjoyed from its higher ground. The older urban story belongs to the Colonia Mirasierra, whose origins are traced by COAM-derived architectural material to a 1927 Sociedad de Casas Baratas satellite-city project. A 1954 housing plan then reshaped the estate, adding higher buildings and fixing green areas and plot conditions. The station itself opened on 28 March 2011 as Line 9 was extended north from Herrera Oria.
