San Francisco is a Line 11 station in Carabanchel, opened on 18 December 2006 as part of the extension from Pan Bendito toward La Peseta. Its name is best explained by the local San Francisco area, documented on Madrid municipal mapping as the Colonia de San Francisco and cited in contemporary reports as one of the neighbourhoods served by the new extension. The ultimate eponym is probably Saint Francis of Assisi, founder of the Franciscan orders, but the available sources did not include a direct municipal naming file proving why the colony itself was called San Francisco. The existing row should therefore be changed from a confidently saint-only explanation to a place-name explanation with religious origin marked as probable.
