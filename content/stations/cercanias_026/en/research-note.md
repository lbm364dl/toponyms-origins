For public wording, the safest structure is two-tiered: the station-name origin is the town of Aranjuez, while the town-name etymology is probable and contested. The previous wording's railway note is useful but should be sharpened to 'first railway from Madrid' and 'second on the Peninsula'. The Ara Iovis theory is historically interesting because it goes back at least to Sarmiento's 1755 letter, but the available modern discussion treats it as weak and often repeated without strong evidence.
