El Barrial-Centro Comercial Pozuelo is named for two immediate landmarks: the El Barrial area and the Pozuelo commercial centre associated with Hipercor. The word barrial derives from barro, and the RAE defines it as clayey or muddy ground. Contemporary press coverage shows that Hipercor Pozuelo opened in September 1994 and that a Cercanías stop at the El Barrial junction was being completed as part of that development. Operator and transport-authority sources now use the compound station name and list it on the C-7/C-10 western corridor.
