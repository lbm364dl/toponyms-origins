The best-supported public explanation is not that the station is directly named after the Arroyo del Soto alone, but after the local El Soto area where station, neighbourhood, park, and arroyo names converge. Renfe's own page is unusually valuable because it records the former name Villaviciosa de Odón and the 1984 opening, although it does not give a precise current-name adoption date. The municipal history page also cautions against oversimplifying the 1808 Bando story: Juan Pérez Villamil drafted the circular, the two mayors signed it, and later tradition amplified a partly mythic version. That episode is useful background for Móstoles but should not dominate this station's etymology.
