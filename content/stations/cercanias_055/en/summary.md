The station name joins the municipality, Móstoles, with El Soto, the western local area where the station stands. The Real Academia Española defines soto as a wooded place, especially one in a riverbank or floodplain setting, from Latin saltus, 'wood, forest'. In Móstoles, the name is anchored in a real local landscape: the Arroyo del Soto, the Via Verde Arroyo del Soto, and the municipal Parque Natural El Soto. Renfe also records an important naming history: the station opened in 1984 under the name Villaviciosa de Odón, despite being in Móstoles, before taking its present local name.
