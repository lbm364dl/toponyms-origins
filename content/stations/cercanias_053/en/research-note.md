The strongest direct chain is station name -> Avenida de las Retamas / Las Retamas area -> retama shrub. Authoritative evidence was found for the word's Arabic etymology and for the station's location, but not a municipal naming act. The previous wording's 2003 date is supported by Renfe, yet contemporary press gives 31 July 2002 as the inauguration date; this may reflect inauguration versus public opening rather than a true contradiction.
