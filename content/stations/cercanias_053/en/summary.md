Las Retamas is named for a local Alcorcon place-name built on retama, the Spanish word for a broom shrub. The RAE derives retama from Andalusi Arabic ratama, ultimately from classical Arabic ratamah, and defines it as a common Spanish shrub with yellow flowers. Transport sources place the station on Avenida de Mostoles at its junction with Avenida de las Retamas, which makes the street/local area the safest immediate source of the station name. Press coverage shows the stop was inaugurated as Las Retamas on 31 July 2002, while a Renfe location page says it opened to the public in 2003. The broad botanical meaning is secure, but the claim that abundant retamas specifically covered this exact terrain remains an inference unless a municipal naming file is found.
