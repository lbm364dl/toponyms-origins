Puerta del Ángel is best explained as a place-name inherited by the metro station from the surrounding neighbourhood and plaza. The place-name comes from a former access gate to the Casa de Campo, which in turn was associated with the Hermitage of the Santo Ángel de la Guarda near the Puente de Segovia. The hermitage was founded in 1605 by the porters or mace-bearers of Madrid’s municipal council to house an image of the Holy Guardian Angel formerly linked with the Puerta de Guadalajara. Sources agree on the name-chain but differ on details of the older Puerta de Guadalajara fire, commonly dated either 1580 or 1582 in secondary accounts. The station itself belongs to the 1995 closure of Line 6 between Laguna and Ciudad Universitaria, not to the 1981 section of the line.
