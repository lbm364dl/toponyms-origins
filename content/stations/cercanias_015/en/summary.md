The station name is best explained as a local place-name: Puente Alcocer, at Calle Alcocer in Villaverde. Renfe's station sheet records the station as Puente Alcocer, code 35704, on line C-5, with its postal address at Calle Alcocer 54. The deeper element Alcocer is a well-attested Iberian toponymic form: Toponomasticon Hispaniae links Alcocer and related names to Andalusi qasar, 'palace, alcazar', and its diminutive qusayr, 'small alcazar'. What is not proven is the previous wording's more specific suggestion that an Islamic watchtower stood at this precise Madrid bridge.
