The immediate naming source is well supported: official and transport-history sources identify Valdecarros as the line 1 terminus opened in the Congosto-Valdecarros extension, and Madrid municipal sources document Valdecarros as a major development area in Villa de Vallecas. The deeper etymology is only probable because the transparent val + de + carros reading is linguistically strong, while the Navarrese theory found online is tied to Salamanca and has not been verified for Madrid.
