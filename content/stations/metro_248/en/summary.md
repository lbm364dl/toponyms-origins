Valdecarros is the southern terminus of Metro line 1, opened with the Congosto-Valdecarros extension on 16 May 2007. Its name refers to the Valdecarros development area in Villa de Vallecas, one of Madrid's southeastern urban expansion zones, even though the station itself stands at the edge of the Ensanche de Vallecas. Linguistically, Valdecarros reads as val de carros, 'valley of carts', from val, an apocopated form of valle, and carro. A Navarrese-resettlement theory is found for the Salamanca municipality of Valdecarros, but No direct evidence that it explains the Madrid station name.
