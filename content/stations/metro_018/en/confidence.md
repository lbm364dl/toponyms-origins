The name change from Norte to Príncipe Pío is documented in contemporary transport reporting from 22-23 December 1994, and official/regional transport sources place the interchange under the old Príncipe Pío possession and the former Estación del Norte. RAH/Metro and Patrimonio Nacional sources directly connect the Príncipe Pío name with Francisco Pío de Saboya y Moura. The only caution is that the station is named through the established place name, not necessarily by a formal dedicatory act to the individual.
