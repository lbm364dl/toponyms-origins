The station name is local and topographic: Metro used the name of the Glorieta del Mar de Cristal, the roundabout above the station. The Community of Madrid states directly that the Line 4 station is located in the roundabout from which it takes its name, and the CRTM lists the access at Gta. del Mar de Cristal, 1. Madrid’s official street data records the roundabout as an official vial from 31 May 1990, with addresses added from 2 July 1992. No available source explains why the roundabout was called “Mar de Cristal”; the best-supported context is the surrounding Hortaleza street-name pattern based on seas and maritime names, not a proven biblical allusion.
