The core claim is verified: the station is officially described by the Community of Madrid as taking its name from the Glorieta del Mar de Cristal. The deeper explanation of the phrase remains undocumented, but the biblical theory is weaker than the local urban evidence: the roundabout became an official vial in 1990 inside a Hortaleza area with many sea-themed street names, some recorded in the municipal street inventory from the 1950s.
