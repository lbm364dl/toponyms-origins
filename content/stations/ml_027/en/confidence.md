The station's location and name are documented by CRTM and Metro Ligero Oeste, and the Retamares place-name is independently present in municipal geography and the military-base name. The botanical etymology is strongly supported by RAE definitions of retama and retamar. Confidence is kept at probable because the available sources did not include a primary station-naming resolution or a historical toponymy source specifically deriving Pozuelo's Retamares from retamar.
