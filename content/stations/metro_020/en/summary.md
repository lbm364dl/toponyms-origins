The Metro name comes from the urban toponym Calle de Guzman el Bueno, beside the station area, and ultimately from Alonso Perez de Guzman (1256-1309). The Real Academia de la Historia identifies him as lord of Sanlucar, alcaide of Tarifa, and founder of the house of Niebla. His celebrated fame is tied to the 1294 siege of Tarifa, when the infante Don Juan, allied with Muslim forces, used Guzman's captured son to try to force a surrender. Medieval and later sources turned his refusal into one of Spain's most famous loyalty legends: the father would rather lose his son than betray the king's fortress. The station opened on Line 6 on 13 January 1987 and became an interchange with Line 7 on 12 February 1999.
