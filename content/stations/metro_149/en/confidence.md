The station rename from Elvas to Opañel on 27 June 1983 is well supported by CRTM historical mapping and contemporary El País reports. The immediate reason, naming it for the neighbourhood rather than the distant Calle Elvas, is also directly supported. The link from the barrio to Arroyo de Opañel is plausible and now supported by official Opañel vial rows from the 1960s, but the available sources did not include a primary naming act or historical gazetteer proving the deeper chain; the pre-Roman hydronym idea is speculative for this specific name.
