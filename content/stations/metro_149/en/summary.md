The station opened on Line 6 as Elvas when the Pacífico-Oporto section entered service in May 1981. In June 1983 it was officially renamed Opañel because Elvas street was not close enough to the station after changes to the line's route, while Opañel was the established neighbourhood name. The neighbourhood name is commonly connected with the Arroyo de Opañel, and the official historical Callejero records Opañel in Carabanchel vial names such as Camino del Opañel in 1963 and Calle del Arroyo Opañel from 1969. A proposed pre-Roman water-name origin based on *up-/*op- is plausible only as a broad comparison: Francisco Villar's article discusses that hydronymic family but does not identify Opañel itself.
