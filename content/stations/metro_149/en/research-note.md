The dataset should distinguish three layers: the station name, the neighbourhood name, and the proposed deep etymology. The station layer is solid: Elvas was renamed Opañel on 27 June 1983 because Opañel was the barrio name and Calle Elvas was not actually adjacent enough. The arroyo layer is plausible and supported by living municipal toponymy, but needs a stronger historical naming source. The pre-Roman *op- theory should be demoted to a note unless a specialist source explicitly discusses Opañel.
