O'Donnell station, opened on Line 6 in 1979, is named after the nearby Calle de O'Donnell. The street commemorates Leopoldo O'Donnell y Joris, 1st Duke of Tetuán and Count of Lucena, a Spanish soldier-politician who led the Liberal Union and presided over the Council of Ministers. His title of Duke of Tetuán was linked to Spain's 1859-1860 war in Morocco, in which he commanded the Spanish army. The street's urban origin belongs to Madrid's late-19th-century expansion after the Cerca de Felipe IV was removed, not to the demolition of medieval walls.
