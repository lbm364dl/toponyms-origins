The station name comes from its immediate setting in the Prado del Espino area of Boadilla del Monte, today a business park and formerly described in planning documents as an industrial estate or urban sector. The toponym is older than the light-rail stop: official planning and hydrological documents also refer to the Barranco or Arroyo de Prado del Espino. In Spanish, prado means a wet or irrigated meadow or pasture, and espino is a thorny rosaceous tree, commonly understood as hawthorn or espino blanco. The station opened with Metro Ligero Oeste's ML3 service in July 2007. The literal botanical reading is sound, but no inspected source directly proves that hawthorn grew on the exact station plot.
