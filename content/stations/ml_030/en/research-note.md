The best-supported update is to shift the record wording from a purely descriptive plant origin to a place-name origin: the station is named for the Prado del Espino area, which is independently attested in urban planning and hydrological documents. The botanical explanation of espino as hawthorn/espino blanco is linguistically valid, but the historical naming motive remains inferred. Planning documents found vegetation in the barranco area, but the consulted excerpt names brambles, rushes and reeds rather than espino, so it should not be used as proof of former hawthorn growth.
