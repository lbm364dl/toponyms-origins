The strongest station-specific evidence is Telefónica's own annual report, because it directly ties the new metro station to Distrito C and gives the 14 million euro investment. The name itself is best treated as a road-derived odonymic station name, not as a direct station dedication to Telefónica; however, the road and campus vocabulary clearly belong to the communications identity of the company. The opening-date conflict likely reflects inauguration versus public service or later station-list convention, but I did not find a definitive Metro de Madrid page accessible enough to settle it.
