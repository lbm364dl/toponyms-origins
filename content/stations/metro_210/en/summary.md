The station name comes directly from Ronda de la Comunicación, the street and address associated with Telefónica's headquarters campus in Las Tablas. That campus was developed as Distrito C, a large corporate complex designed by Rafael de la Hoz and later known as Distrito Telefónica. Telefónica's own 2006 annual reporting says the company invested 14 million euros in the new underground station, showing how closely the stop was tied to the campus mobility plan. The name is therefore best understood as a modern descriptive/corporate toponym: a road name built around the idea of communications, serving a telecommunications company's headquarters.
