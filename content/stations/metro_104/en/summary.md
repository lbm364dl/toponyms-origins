República Argentina is a Line 6 station named after the Plaza de la República Argentina, the large roundabout at Joaquín Costa and nearby streets in El Viso, Chamartín. The name is a civic homage to Argentina rather than a direct reference to the Hispanoamérica neighbourhood. Madrid's municipal heritage catalogue explains that the plaza was the site chosen for a 1949 competition for a Monument to the Argentine Nation. The monument was reduced over time into the Fuente de los Delfines, finally reassembled in 1969 after road works, while the metro station opened with the first Line 6 section in October 1979.
