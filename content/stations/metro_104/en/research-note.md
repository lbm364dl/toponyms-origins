The most important record issue is not the Argentina etymology but the urban-context wording. Plaza de la República Argentina is in El Viso according to Madrid municipal records; nearby Chamartín does include American-themed names, especially north/east of the station, but the immediate plaza is surrounded by a mixed odonymy of rivers, people, institutions and international references. The fountain evidence is valuable because it proves the plaza's name was understood officially as a tribute to the Argentine Nation before the metro station existed.
