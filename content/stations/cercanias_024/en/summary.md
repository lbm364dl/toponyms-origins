Valdemoro is a place-name carried by the municipality and then by its railway station, one of the intermediate stops on the Madrid-Aranjuez line opened on 9 February 1851. Toponomasticon Hispaniae analyses the name as a Castilian compound of valle plus moro, with moro used adjectivally as 'dark' or 'brownish'. That gives the sense 'dark valley' or 'dusky valley', probably referring to some feature of the terrain. Other explanations exist, including a literal 'valley of Moors', an Arabic origin, blackberries, or ruined walls, but they are less directly supported for this station name.
