Estadio Metropolitano is a Line 7 station named for Atlético de Madrid's stadium beside Avenida de Arcentales. Until June 2017 the stop was called Estadio Olímpico, reflecting the earlier Olympic use imagined for the La Peineta stadium site. The Comunidad de Madrid and Atlético presented the new station name as a reference to the club's new home and as a tribute to the historic Stadium Metropolitano, where Atlético played from 1923 to 1966. The stadium's commercial prefix has changed from Wanda to Cívitas and now Riyadh Air, but the Metro station keeps the stable public name Estadio Metropolitano.
