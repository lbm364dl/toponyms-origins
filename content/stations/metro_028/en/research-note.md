The strongest concise etymology is two-layered: the Metro station was renamed for the adjacent Atlético stadium, while the stadium's 'Metropolitano' element revives the club's old 1923-1966 Stadium Metropolitano. The previous wording's phrase 'itself named after the Metro company that funded it' is directionally plausible but should be softened: Atlético's official history specifically names the Otamendi brothers, creators of Metro de Madrid, as promoters and records the creation of Sociedad Stadium Metropolitano, rather than giving a simple direct funding statement by Metro de Madrid as today's operator.
