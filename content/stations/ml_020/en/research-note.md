The strongest evidence concerns the transport name, not the original symbolic naming of the avenue. The station is unquestionably named from the avenue, and the avenue is embedded in a European-themed street cluster, but no inspected source proves that the name was chosen because Spain joined the EEC in 1986. Municipal archive material suggests the area's deeper administrative name was Plan Parcial Ampliación Casa de Campo; this is valuable context and should replace overconfident narrative about a 1986 naming impulse unless a primary naming file is found.
