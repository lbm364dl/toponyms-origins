The direct station-name origin is verified by official transport sources: CRTM lists Coslada Central on Line 7 with Cercanías connections, Comunidad de Madrid documents the Line 7 extension to Coslada and San Fernando, and Renfe describes the Coslada-Coslada Central interchange from May 2007. Confidence is lower only for the remote etymology of Coslada, which sources explicitly treat as disputed.
