Coslada Central is a place-name station: it identifies the Line 7 Metro stop serving the central area of Coslada and linking directly with the Cercanías station named Coslada. The MetroEste extension to Coslada and San Fernando de Henares entered service in May 2007, making this stop one of the key interchange points on the eastern side of the Madrid network. The municipality’s name is much older than the station, but its origin is not settled. Sources record explanations from Latin or Romance words connected with stone, flint, scrubland or extent, and a pre-Roman/Celtic comparison with coslo/cosla, “hazelnut.” The safest public wording is therefore to say that the station is named for the municipality, while Coslada’s own etymology remains debated.
