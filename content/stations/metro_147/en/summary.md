Valdezarza is primarily a place-name station: Metro's own infrastructure material describes the 1999 extension as connecting the network with the peripheral neighbourhood of Valdezarza. The neighbourhood is officially listed by Madrid City Council as Barrio 94 in the district of Moncloa-Aravaca, and the official historical Callejero records late-twentieth-century Valdezarza vial names such as Camino and Callejón de Valdezarza. The deeper meaning of the name is very likely descriptive: val is an apocopated form of valle, while zarza is a bramble or thorny shrub, a word the RAE derives from older sarza of pre-Roman origin. No direct official naming decree or local toponymy study was found that explicitly glosses Valdezarza, so the 'bramble valley' reading should be presented as probable rather than fully proven.
