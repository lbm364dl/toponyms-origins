Retiro is a place-name station: it was named for the Parque del Buen Retiro, located directly beside the station entrance area near Puerta de Hernani and Puerta de Alcalá. Metro de Madrid’s historical file records that the station opened on 14 June 1924 on the inaugural Sol-Ventas section of Line 2, and that the project name Independencia-Velázquez was changed to Retiro during construction. The deeper name comes from the royal quarters at the monastery of San Jerónimo el Real, later expanded into the Casa Real or Real Sitio del Buen Retiro under Felipe IV and the Count-Duke of Olivares. The park passed to Madrid’s municipality after the 1868 revolution and is now part of the UNESCO World Heritage property Paseo del Prado and Buen Retiro, inscribed in 2021.
