Aviación Española opened on Line 10 on 22 December 2006 between Colonia Jardín and Cuatro Vientos. The most direct contemporary account explains the name as a tribute to the former Aviation barracks of Campamento, an area then tied to the planned Operación Campamento redevelopment. The station also sits beside Avenida de la Aviación and near Madrid-Cuatro Vientos, one of Spain's foundational aviation sites. Aena traces Cuatro Vientos back to the military decision of January 1911 to acquire land for a pilots' school, with the first aircraft arriving shortly afterward. The name is therefore best read as a historical and local aviation reference, not as a single event or solely as a station named after the airport.
