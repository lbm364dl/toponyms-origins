The 2022 Comunidad de Madrid PDF is about Cuatro Vientos station, not Aviación Española station. It is still useful for the broader Cuatro Vientos aviation context, but it should not be used as proof that Aviación Española station was named after the airport. The source even says the airport gave its name to the Line 10 station inaugurated in 2003, which refers to Cuatro Vientos, not this 2006 station.
