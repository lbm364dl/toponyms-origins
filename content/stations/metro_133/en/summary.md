The station name comes directly from the former Capilla del Carmen, not from a street or modern neighbourhood label. That chapel opened for public worship in 1928 on the old Carretera de Aragón 40, now Calle de Alcalá 246, and served the poor settlement that had grown east of the Abroñigal stream. In 1940 it became the first seat of the Parroquia Espíritu Santo, which moved to its present church on Calle Ricardo Ortiz in 1968. The chapel itself no longer survives; the station name is one of the clearest traces left on the city map.
