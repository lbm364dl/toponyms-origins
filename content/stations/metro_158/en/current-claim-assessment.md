The core claim is right: the station is named for Avenida de la Paz, the eastern M-30 corridor over the former Abroñigal valley. The wording should be corrected on dates: Madrid's official callejero records Avenida de la Paz as active from 26 November 1965, not 1991; the M-30/Avenida de la Paz opened in phases, with the first M-30 sections in 1974 and further Avenida de la Paz works in 1976-1977.
