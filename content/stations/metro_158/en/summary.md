Avenida de la Paz station was named for the nearby Avenida de la Paz, the eastern sector of the M-30 between Avenida del Manzanares and Avenida de Burgos. Madrid's official callejero gives the street-name record an effective date of 26 November 1965, well before the Metro station opened with the Line 4 extension to Esperanza in January 1979. The road followed the old Abroñigal valley, a stream corridor later canalised and buried beneath the ring road. The word 'Paz' looks abstract, but Luis Miguel Aparisi Laporta's municipal toponymy work, as reported in El País, identifies it as an allusion to the post-Civil War 'peace' used in Franco-era commemorative naming.
