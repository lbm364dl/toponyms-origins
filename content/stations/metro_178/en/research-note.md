The previous wording's claim is defensible but should not make the station appear to be directly named after Ferdinand III. The most accurate hierarchy is: station named for the municipality; municipality named through the Real Sitio founded under Fernando VI; San Fernando refers to Fernando VI's onomastic saint, Ferdinand III; Henares is a later river-name addition from 1916. Some secondary sources phrase the town as named in homage to Fernando VI himself, while others specify the saintly patron; these are not necessarily contradictory but should be presented as layered rather than flattened.
