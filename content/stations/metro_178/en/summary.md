San Fernando is best explained first as a place-name: the Metro station serves the historic centre of San Fernando de Henares. The older settlement was incorporated into the Crown in 1746 as Torrejón de la Ribera, then reshaped around the Royal Cloth Factory and the new Real Sitio de San Fernando. The San Fernando element reflects Fernando VI's royal foundation and his name-day saint, Ferdinand III of Castile and León. The present municipal form San Fernando de Henares replaced the earlier fluvial form San Fernando de Jarama in 1916, even though the Jarama was historically the more immediate river.
