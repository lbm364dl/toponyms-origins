The core claim is well supported: the station name is a shortened railway form of Villalba/Villalba de Guadarrama in the municipality of Collado Villalba, and the municipal toponym is authoritatively explained by Toponomasticon Hispaniae as collado plus Villalba, from villa + alba. The railway-junction context is also supported, but the detail about medieval Segovian transhumant shepherds is weaker than the available evidence and should be softened.
