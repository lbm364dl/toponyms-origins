The Cercanias station is best understood as named for the Villalba railway settlement within Collado Villalba, officially also listed by CRTM and Adif as Villalba de Guadarrama. Toponomasticon Hispaniae explains Collado Villalba as a transparent compound: collado describes a low hill or pass, while Villalba is villa plus alba. In this kind of Spanish place name, alba need not mean that the village was physically white; it can express an auspicious idea of a clean, pure, prosperous settlement. The railway made the name especially visible after the Madrid-El Escorial line reached the area in the 1860s and the station district grew apart from the older village.
