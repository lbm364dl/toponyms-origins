The wording is broadly right that the station name comes from the Moncloa place-name and historic estate, but it overstates the 'Mons Clovis' explanation. The strongest available sources support a Madrid estate formerly called Fuente el Sol becoming La Moncloa/Monclova through the Counts of La Monclova; the deeper Latin origin of Monclova is not well supported by the authoritative available sources.
