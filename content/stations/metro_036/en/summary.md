Moncloa station takes its name from the Moncloa area around Plaza de Moncloa and Calle de la Princesa. Historical sources trace the Madrid place-name to an estate once known as Fuente el Sol, which became associated with Antonio Portocarrero and his descendants, Counts of La Monclova. In 1660 Gaspar de Haro y Guzmán acquired the huerta de La Monclova and the neighboring huerta de Sora, a chain that later fed into the Real Sitio and Palacio de la Moncloa. The station opened on Line 3 on 17 July 1963, not in 1973; Line 6 arrived at Moncloa on 10 May 1995 and Line 3 was relocated to a new station site in 2006.
