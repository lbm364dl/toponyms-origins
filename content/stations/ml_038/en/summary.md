The stop is named directly after Parla's bullring, usually referred to as the Plaza de Toros or Plaza de Toros Municipal. The name is descriptive rather than commemorative: it points passengers to a nearby public landmark associated with local festivals and bullfighting events. The current bullring was built in 2003 and appears in municipal and technical documents as a fixed facility, not simply as a seasonal portable ring. The tram opened to the public in 2007, so the stop name was already a natural local reference when the line began service.
