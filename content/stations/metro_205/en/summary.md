The Line 10 station is a civic memorial to Joaquín Vilumbrales López, who was a physics and chemistry teacher at Instituto Los Castillos and later mayor of Alcorcón. Alcorcón City Council identifies him as professor, director of that institute, and mayor from June 1999 until his death on 9 September 1999. Contemporary press records that the station had first been planned as Los Castillos, but was named Joaquín Vilumbrales at the proposal of mayor Pablo Zúñiga. The station opened with the Line 10 extension to Puerta del Sur on 11 April 2003.
