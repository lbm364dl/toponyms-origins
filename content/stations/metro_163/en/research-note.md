The strongest public-facing evidence is the CRTM historical chronology plus the Aluche book excerpt. Together they support the etymology as an operational railway name, but they also correct the dataset: Empalme was a Suburbano station before it was Line 10 and much later Line 5. The date conflict is worth preserving for human review because CRTM gives 4 February 1961 while Andén 1 and the Aluche excerpt point to a later opening around April 1961.
