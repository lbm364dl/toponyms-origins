Empalme is a practical railway name: it marks the place where two systems met. The Ferrocarril Suburbano from Plaza de España to Carabanchel was built to connect with the Madrid-Almorox railway, a narrow-gauge line that had linked Madrid with the southwest since 1891. Transport-history sources describe the stop as planned for that interchange and say it was initially called or proposed as Madrid-Empalme or Goya-Empalme before settling simply as Empalme. The station later passed from the Suburbano to Line 10 in 1981 and to Line 5 in 2002.
