Conservatorio has one of the clearest names on MetroSur: it points to a nearby public music school, the Conservatorio Profesional de Musica de Getafe. The station is not named for Madrid's historic Real Conservatorio Superior de Musica, but for the Getafe professional conservatory in the same immediate area.

The institution behind the name stands on Avenida de las Arcas del Agua, 1. The Community of Madrid describes it as a public music education centre in its regional network, and its own practical information lists MetroSur station "Conservatorio" as the metro connection. The station access is only a short distance away, at Avenida de las Arcas del Agua by Senda de Mafalda.

The chronology is compact. The conservatory was inaugurated in 2000, and its current official denomination, Conservatorio Profesional de Musica de Getafe, was established by Decree 42/2001 of 22 March, published in the regional gazette on 29 March 2001. MetroSur then opened on 11 April 2003, bringing Line 12 to Getafe and the other large municipalities of southern Madrid.

The name also tells a small urban story about Sector III. In the station's surroundings, transport maps mark not only the conservatory but also civic, educational and cultural facilities such as the Jorge Luis Borges municipal library and the Jose Hierro poetry centre. The stop's name therefore works as a practical landmark: it identifies a public cultural institution rather than a historical person or an older place-name.

The evidence for the origin is strong because official regional transport and education pages align: the Line 12 page places the station near the conservatory, the education page names the conservatory and gives the station as its MetroSur access, and the CRTM station page fixes the station's location. The main correction is to remove the Real Conservatorio Superior de Musica de Madrid from the dataset, since that is a different Madrid institution and is not the source of this station name.
