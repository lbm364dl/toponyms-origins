The station name is descriptive and local: it identifies the nearby Conservatorio Profesional de Musica de Getafe. The Community of Madrid lists the conservatory at Avenida de las Arcas del Agua, 1 and explicitly gives MetroSur station "Conservatorio" as its metro access. The station itself is listed by CRTM at Avenida de las Arcas del Agua, 9, by Senda de Mafalda, and the regional Line 12 page describes it as located near the conservatory. The conservatory was inaugurated in 2000 and its current official name was established by Decree 42/2001 of 22 March, published in the BOCM on 29 March 2001. No evidence was found that the station ever bore another name.
