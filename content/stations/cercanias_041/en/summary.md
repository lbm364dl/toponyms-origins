Alcalá de Henares station is named directly for the municipality of Alcalá de Henares. The place-name Alcalá belongs to a large Iberian family of names from Andalusi Arabic qaláʿa, classical Arabic qalʿa, meaning 'fortress' or 'castle'. In this city, that name is tied to the medieval Muslim fortress known as Qalʿat Abd al-Salam, built across the Henares in the ninth century. The Henares element refers to the river; Toponomasticon Hispaniae explains it as the plural of henar, a place with hay, rather than as an Arabic word for river. The older Roman city was Complutum, a crucial historical layer but not the immediate source of the modern station name.
