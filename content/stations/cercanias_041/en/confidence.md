The station-to-city naming is directly supported by the current Adif/Renfe station context, the Arabic etymon of Alcalá is supported by Toponomasticon Hispaniae, and the local fortress context is supported by municipal tourism and heritage material. Confidence is not absolute on the exact medieval Arabic form attached to the local fortress because sources use Qalʿat Abd al-Salam and Qalʿat Nahr in different contexts.
