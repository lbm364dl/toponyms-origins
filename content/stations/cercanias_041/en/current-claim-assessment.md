The core claim is correct: the station is named for the municipality, and Alcalá comes from Andalusi Arabic qaláʿa/qalʿa, 'fortress, castle'. The claim is weaker where it treats Henares as possibly Arabic; the best toponymic source found derives Henares from Spanish henar, ultimately Latin fēnu, 'hay'. Complutum is relevant historical context, but its exact etymology should be treated cautiously.
