Quintana is named directly after Plaza de Quintana, the square above the station at Calle de Alcalá in Ciudad Lineal. The station opened in 1964 on the Ventas-Ciudad Lineal extension and was incorporated into Line 5 in 1970. The strongest local evidence points behind the square to an older area-name: Barrio de la Quintana and the estate or place called Quinta de la Quintana, recorded near Arroyo Calero. A dedication to Manuel José Quintana is plausible only as a later or secondary interpretation, because I did not find an official naming record tying this Ciudad Lineal plaza to him.
