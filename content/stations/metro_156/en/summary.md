Manuel de Falla is a Line 10 station in Alcobendas, with its access on Calle Manuel de Falla. The name honours Manuel de Falla y Matheu, born in Cádiz in 1876 and deceased in Alta Gracia, Argentina, in 1946. Falla fused Spanish musical tradition with modern European language in works such as El amor brujo, Noches en los jardines de España, El sombrero de tres picos, El retablo de maese Pedro, and Atlántida. The station opened on 26 April 2007 as part of MetroNorte, the northward extension serving Alcobendas and San Sebastián de los Reyes.
