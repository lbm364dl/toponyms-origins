Avenida de Guadalajara is a Line 2 station opened on 16 March 2011 with the extension from La Elipa to Las Rosas. Contemporary Madrid sources place it under the junction of Avenida de Guadalajara and Avenida de Canillejas a Vicálvaro, and explicitly say it takes its name from the avenue. The avenue-name points to Guadalajara, northeast of Madrid on the Henares, a city whose Arabic medieval name is recorded in forms such as Wad al-Hayara or Wadi al-Hiyara. Authoritative heritage and municipal sources connect that Arabic name with an older stone-related place-name and with the meaning 'river/valley of stones'.
