The stop name comes from Parla's Calle Jaime I El Conquistador, a municipal street name recorded in the town's public property inventory. Jaime I, called the Conqueror, was born in Montpellier in 1208 and died in Alcira in 1276; the Real Academia de la Historia identifies him as king of Aragon, Mallorca and Valencia, count of Barcelona and Urgel, and lord of Montpellier. His reign is especially associated with the incorporation of Mallorca and Valencia into the Crown of Aragon. In Parla, the name also fits a cluster of streets dedicated to Spanish monarchs, including Reyes Católicos, Felipe II, Alfonso XIII and Isabel II.
