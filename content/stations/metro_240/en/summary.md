The station name comes from the Casa del Reloj, a municipal administrative building of the Ayuntamiento de Leganés on Avenida de Gibraltar. The Community of Madrid's MetroSur documentation states directly that the station is next to the town-hall building that gives it its name. Municipal and official gazette sources confirm the address, Avenida de Gibraltar 2, and show the building functioning as an administrative and citizen-service site before MetroSur opened. An archival description of the Leganés municipal archive adds useful chronology: the new administrative building, Casa del Reloj, was inaugurated in 1994. The station opened with Line 12, MetroSur, on 11 April 2003.
