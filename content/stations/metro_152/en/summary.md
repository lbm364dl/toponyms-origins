Juan de la Cierva is a Line 12 MetroSur station in Getafe, located on Avenida de España in the neighbourhood of the same name. Its name ultimately commemorates Juan de la Cierva y Codorníu (1895-1936), the civil engineer and aeronautical inventor from Murcia who created the autogiro. Getafe’s own virtual museum notes that an avenue and a neighbourhood bear his name because some of his test flights were made in Getafe. The best-supported account is therefore an indirect station name: neighbourhood first, inventor behind the neighbourhood name. The exact official date when the neighbourhood or station name was assigned was not located.
