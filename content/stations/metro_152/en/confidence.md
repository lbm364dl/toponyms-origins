The station location source states that Juan de la Cierva is in the neighbourhood that gives the station its name, and Getafe’s own museum entry explicitly says an avenue and a neighbourhood carry Juan de la Cierva Codorníu’s name. Multiple authoritative or specialist sources support the underlying biography and autogiro connection. The remaining uncertainty concerns the exact municipal act/date and the fine chronology of the C-4 flights, not the station-name origin.
