Blasco Ibáñez is a place-name by way of an urban street name: the ML1 station stands at Calle del Príncipe Carlos, next to Calle de Vicente Blasco Ibáñez. The honouree is Vicente Blasco Ibáñez, born in Valencia in 1867 and remembered as a novelist, journalist and republican politician. He served as a national deputy for six legislatures between 1898 and 1907 and founded the newspaper El Pueblo. His international fame grew through novels such as Los cuatro jinetes del Apocalipsis, whose 1921 Hollywood adaptation helped make Rudolph Valentino a major silent-film idol. The station entered service with ML1 on 24 May 2007 in the Sanchinarro area of Valdefuentes, district of Hortaleza.
