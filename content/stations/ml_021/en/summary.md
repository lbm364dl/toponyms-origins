Berna is an ML2 stop of Metro Ligero Oeste in Pozuelo de Alarcón, opened with the system on 27 July 2007. Its immediate name source is Calle Berna, which the Pozuelo town hall identifies as part of the ML2 corridor near the end of the line toward Aravaca. Municipal maps place Calle Berna next to Calle Suiza and among other European place names such as Alemania, Berlín, Inglaterra, Londres, Austria, Hungría, Polonia and Irlanda. The deeper reference is Bern, the Swiss federal city and seat of the Swiss government and parliament. The broad European naming pattern is clear, but the exact municipal date when Calle Berna itself was named remains to be confirmed from local street-naming records.
