The best public explanation should lead with the modern campus, then treat Somosaguas as the older place-name. The water explanation is stronger than the precise 'somo + aguas' segmentation because Jiménez de Gregorio cites Sumas Aguas and early testimony about many good waters; Toponomasticon Hispaniae supports the broader somo/summus family only indirectly by listing Somosaguas as a cognate of Somosierra.
