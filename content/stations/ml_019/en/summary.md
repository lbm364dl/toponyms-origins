The Metro Ligero station is named for the nearby Campus de Somosaguas, one of the main campuses of the Universidad Complutense de Madrid in Pozuelo de Alarcón. UCM documentation places the creation of the Somosaguas campus in 1968, and the Faculty of Economics building is dated to that year. The older name Somosaguas belongs to the local landscape and appears in historical discussion as Sumas Aguas, explained in early-modern testimony as a place of many good waters. The wider history includes the medieval hamlet of San Juan de Sumas Aguas or San Juan de Somosaguas, later depopulated, and a remembered mineral spring at Somosaguas.
