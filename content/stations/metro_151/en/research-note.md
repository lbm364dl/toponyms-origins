The station's immediate naming context appears to be local commemoration in Leganés: the CRTM and Community of Madrid sources locate the station on Avenida Rey Juan Carlos I, and the municipal source places the Centro Cívico Julián Besteiro at number 30 with MetroSur access by the station. However, the accessible official transport pages do not explicitly say 'the station was named after the civic centre' or publish a naming decree, so the safest formulation is that the station commemorates Julián Besteiro Fernández and sits next to local facilities bearing the same name.
