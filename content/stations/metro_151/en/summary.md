The station name commemorates Julián Besteiro Fernández, one of the major figures of Spanish democratic socialism in the early twentieth century. Besteiro was educated at the Institución Libre de Enseñanza, became a university professor, joined the PSOE and UGT, and served as president of the Constituent Cortes of the Second Republic. Congress records give his presidency as interim from 14 to 26 July 1931 and definitive from 27 July 1931 to 9 October 1933. After the Civil War he was tried by a Francoist military court, sentenced to thirty years in prison, and died in Carmona prison on 27 September 1940. The station opened on 11 April 2003 as part of Line 12 MetroSur, in Leganés, on Avenida Rey Juan Carlos I near the Centro Cívico Julián Besteiro.
