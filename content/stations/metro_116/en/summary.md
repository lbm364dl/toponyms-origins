Aluche station is named after the surrounding Aluche neighbourhood in the district of Latina. The strongest explanation traces the place-name to the Arroyo Luche, a former tributary of the Manzanares that crossed what are now Aluche, Lucero and Puerta del Ángel. The Prado's catalogue for José Jiménez Fernández's 1873 landscape explicitly identifies the Arroyo Luche as the stream that gave the modern neighbourhood its name. Local history also preserves the memory of orchards and irrigated land around Luche, while today's Parque Aluche includes an artificial watercourse recalling the stream's route. The idea that 'Aluche' came from a map abbreviation 'A. Luche' should be treated as an unverified later theory, not as the main explanation.
