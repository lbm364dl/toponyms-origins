The station is named indirectly through Calle del Alcalde Sainz de Baranda, the Retiro-area street that commemorates Pedro Casto Sáinz de Baranda y Gorriti. The Real Academia de la Historia identifies him as constitutional mayor of Madrid in 1812 and 1820, and later scholarship places him at the head of the municipal government during several exceptional liberal and wartime moments. The station opened on Line 6 in the first Cuatro Caminos-Pacifico section in October 1979. Its role as an interchange dates from 31 January 1980, when the first Line 9 section from Sainz de Baranda to Pavones entered service.
