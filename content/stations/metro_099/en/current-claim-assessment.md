The wording is broadly correct: the station name comes from Calle del Alcalde Sainz de Baranda, honoring Pedro Casto Sáinz de Baranda y Gorriti, Madrid's constitutional mayor in 1812 and 1820. The transport chronology needs nuance: Line 6 opened here in October 1979, but the Line 9 interchange began on 31 January 1980.
