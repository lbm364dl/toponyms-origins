Aeropuerto T4 is a descriptive station name: it identifies the stop serving Terminal 4 of today's Aeropuerto Adolfo Suárez Madrid-Barajas. Aena records T4's inauguration on 4 February 2006 and its first flight on 5 February 2006. Metro line 8 was extended to the terminal on 3 May 2007, while the Cercanías link to the T4 opened in September 2011 and entered public service the next day. The terminal itself is also a major architectural landmark by Richard Rogers Partnership and Estudio Lamela, known for its undulating roof, bamboo-lined interior and coloured structural supports.
