Argüelles is named for Agustín de Argüelles Álvarez (1776-1844), a liberal politician from Ribadesella and one of the leading figures of the Cortes of Cádiz. Metro de Madrid states that the station name refers to him and that the wider area was known by that name around the former location of his statue, near Princesa, Alberto Aguilera and Marqués de Urquijo. Argüelles helped draft the preamble and much of the 1812 Constitution, defended press freedom, opposed judicial torture, and condemned the slave trade. The line 3 station opened in 1941; line 4 followed in 1944, and line 6 arrived in 1995.
