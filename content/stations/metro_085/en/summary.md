Las Tablas is a place-name station: it takes its name from the Las Tablas residential area in the district of Fuencarral-El Pardo. Official transport pages identify Las Tablas as a Line 10 and ML1 interchange, while municipal planning documents use the name UZI 0.08 Las Tablas for the urban development. The development was not simply created by the 1997 General Plan: municipal tracking material records definitive approvals in 1995 for the PAU and partial plan, with later urbanisation and building in the 2000s. The likely linguistic background is agricultural, because the RAE records 'tabla' as a strip or quadrangular piece of land for cultivation. No source found directly states that Madrid's Las Tablas was named from those farm plots, so the deeper etymology should be marked probable rather than fully verified.
