Toponomasticon Hispaniae adds useful linguistic nuance absent from many popular accounts: labrada is not necessarily agricultural here, even though the word often developed that sense; in this place name it is best read through the stoneworking sense. The municipal page says the first inhabitants drank from Fuente de Fregacedos, while the 1576 text more generally says a nearby old fountain gave the place its name. Renfe’s public filming page says the station joined Cercanías in 1989, while other secondary railway accounts give 20 June 1876 for the older railway station on the Madrid-Torrijos route; that earlier date was not needed to verify the name origin.
