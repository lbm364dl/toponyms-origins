The station’s current name, C-5 service and location are confirmed by Adif/Renfe, and contemporary press confirms Doce de Octubre opened among the new C-5 stations in September 1989. The hospital’s former name and late-1980s change are supported by official hospital history and parliamentary records. Confidence is 'probable' rather than 'verified' because I did not find an inspected primary renaming order proving the exact 12 October 1988 date or an official naming note explicitly saying the station was named after the hospital, though the inference is very strong.
