Doce de Octubre is a hospital station name: the Cercanías stop serves the Hospital Universitario 12 de Octubre in Usera and appears under that name in Renfe/Adif station records. The hospital opened on 2 October 1973 as Ciudad Sanitaria 1º de Octubre, a name tied to the Franco regime’s anniversary of 1 October 1936. In the late 1980s, during Spain’s democratic period, the hospital changed to 12 de Octubre; a parliamentary record shows the new name was already in use by 25 October 1988. The date also had an official national meaning after Law 18/1987 declared 12 October Spain’s National Day. The Cercanías station opened with the Atocha-Villaverde Alto C-5 works in September 1989.
