The station-name claim is stronger if framed as a layered name: station -> hospital -> date. The existing wording overstates the exactness of the 12 October 1988 renaming unless a formal renaming instrument is added. Wikipedia supplies that exact date and the Columbus/Discovery framing, but the more authoritative available evidence supports a late-1980s change and the national legal significance of 12 October from Law 18/1987. The previous wording also mixes hospital-building details from different sources: Comunidad de Madrid says 18 floors above ground, three below, 75,000 square metres and 250 opening beds, reaching 1,250 beds in 1974.
