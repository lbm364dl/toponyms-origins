The main claim is well supported: the station takes its name from the nearby Hospital 12 de Octubre, whose name replaced the earlier Franco-era Ciudad Sanitaria 1º/Primero de Octubre in the late 1980s. The exact hospital renaming date of 12 October 1988 is plausible but stronger available sources were found only for 'late 1980s' and for the new name being in use by 25 October 1988.
