The station is named after Paco de Lucía, the stage name of Francisco Sánchez Gómez, one of the most influential flamenco guitarists of the twentieth century. On 27 February 2014, shortly after his death in Mexico, the Madrid regional government announced that the future Line 9 station then associated with Calle Costa Brava would bear his name. The station opened to passengers on 25 March 2015 as the northern terminus of Line 9 and later became linked with the Cercanías stop Mirasierra-Paco de Lucía. Its large urban-art mural reinforces the naming as a visible public tribute to the musician.
