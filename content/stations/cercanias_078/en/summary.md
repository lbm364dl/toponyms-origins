Cercedilla station is named for Cercedilla, the municipality in the Sierra de Guadarrama. The best-supported etymology, given by Toponomasticon Hispaniae, understands Cercedilla as a diminutive of nearby Cerceda. Cerceda belongs to a family of names derived from a late or Romance continuation of Latin quercus and quercetum, referring to oaks or oak woods. Historical documents record forms such as Zerezedilla and Cerecedilla before the modern spelling was fixed. The station name therefore preserves a local place-name whose deeper origin points to the wooded Guadarrama landscape.
