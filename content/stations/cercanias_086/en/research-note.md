For dataset purposes, the station's direct etymology should be kept simple: named after the historic place Vallecas. The richer medieval and linguistic material belongs in explanatory copy, with the Corriente theory marked as the best-supported hypothesis found rather than a settled fact. The Fuero evidence is strong for Balecas, but sources differ in whether they emphasize an aldeas reference or the carasal/carrascal de Balecas passage; this should be normalized only after checking a critical edition.
