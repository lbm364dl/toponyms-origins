Vallecas is a place-name inherited from the former municipality of Vallecas, annexed to Madrid in December 1950. Its oldest known written form is Balecas, recorded in material associated with the Fuero de Madrid of 1202. Local historians cite the Arabist Federico Corriente for the interpretation ballaykas, 'valley areas', from a Romance form related to Latin vallis plus an Andalusi Romance suffix. Other explanations, especially 'Valle de Kas', are important as local folklore but are not as well supported linguistically. The current Cercanias stop preserves a railway name already used by the older Vallecas station and now connects with Metro line 1 at Sierra de Guadalupe.
