Esperanza is best explained as a station name taken from the surrounding Colonia Virgen de la Esperanza, a large residential estate in Canillas, today in the district of Hortaleza. The COAM guide documents the scheme as Barrio de Nueva Esperanza / Colonia Virgen de la Esperanza: an urban layout from 1965 and housing by Antonio Camuñas Paredes and José Antonio Camuñas Solís in 1969-1971. DOCOMOMO gives the current name as Colonia Virgen de la Esperanza and dates the housing phase to 1969-1973. The station itself appears in official transport records as a Line 4 stop with access at Calle de Andorra, 51, and the Community of Madrid dates the Alfonso XIII-Esperanza extension to 5 January 1979. No primary document naming the station was found, so the link is highly probable rather than independently proven by a formal naming file.
