The station is correctly linked to the Orcasitas neighbourhood, and the neighbourhood is well attested as deriving from the Orcasitas landowning family, especially Pedro Bernardo Orcasitas Ruiz. However, the row confuses or overstates several details: a 1878 birth date is impossible for the Pedro Bernardo who was acting mayor in 1873-1874, the land-donation story is not securely sourced, and the Basque surname etymology is explicitly described as problematic by Auñamendi.
