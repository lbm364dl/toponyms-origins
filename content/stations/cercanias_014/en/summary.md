Orcasitas is a neighbourhood name before it is a station name. Madrid toponymic scholarship identifies the barrio as a reference to Pedro Bernardo Orcasitas Ruiz, owner of land in the area and acting mayor of Madrid during the First Republic period. Architectural and neighbourhood-history sources broaden this to the Orcasitas family, major landowners on land that became a migrant and working-class settlement from the 1940s to the 1960s. The current station name therefore commemorates the local place-name rather than a direct railway dedication. Claims about an exact Basque meaning of the surname should be treated cautiously, because a Basque reference work marks the etymology of Horcasitas/Orcasitas as problematic.
