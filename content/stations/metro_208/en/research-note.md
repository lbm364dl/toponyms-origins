The best-supported update is to treat 'Begoña' as a two-stage name: a Madrid neighbourhood/housing-estate name used for the station, ultimately derived from the Marian dedication of Our Lady of Begoña in Bilbao. The available Madrid sources explain the estate as official rehousing and mass-housing policy, not as a specifically Basque workers' colony, although the Hermandad de Begoña records devotional links between Madrid, the Basque congregation, and a parish in the Begoña neighbourhood.
