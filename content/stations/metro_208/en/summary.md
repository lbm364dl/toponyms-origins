Begoña is best understood as a local place-name adopted by Metro de Madrid from the surrounding Virgen de Begoña neighbourhood. The housing estate was planned from 1957 and built in the late 1950s and 1960s as part of Madrid's public rehousing operations. Its name refers to Our Lady of Begoña, the Marian devotion centered on the Bilbao sanctuary, traditionally known as the Amatxu and venerated as patroness of Bilbao and Biscay. The popular explanation that Begoña means something like 'at the foot of the hill' is not secure; modern name guides describe the place-name's etymology as disputed and reject that segmentation as baseless. The station itself opened with the old Line 8 in 1982 and later became part of Line 10.
