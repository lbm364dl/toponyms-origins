Los Espartales station is named after its immediate urban setting in Getafe Norte, a northern neighbourhood of Getafe developed mainly in the 1990s. The name is a landscape toponym: in Spanish, an 'espartal' is a place where esparto grass grows. Esparto, also called atocha, supplied fibre for rope, mats and other everyday goods, and its traditional working has documented cultural importance in the southeast of the Madrid region. The station opened with MetroSur on 11 April 2003 and still appears under this name in official transport listings.
