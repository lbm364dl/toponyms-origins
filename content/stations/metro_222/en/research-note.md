The safest revision is to separate two layers: station naming and municipal toponymy. The station is named for the central Alcorcón interchange; the Cercanías infrastructure is officially Alcorcón while the Metro stop uses Alcorcón Central. For the municipal etymology, Galmés gives a stronger linguistic basis for the corco/quercus explanation than the municipal Arabic hill account, but the latter is important local historiography and should remain in the public note as a competing theory.
