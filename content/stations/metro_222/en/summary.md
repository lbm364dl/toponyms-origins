Alcorcón Central is a place-name station: it identifies the MetroSur stop serving the central transport node of Alcorcón, beside the Cercanías station and close to the town centre. The municipality documents the place-name by 28 July 1208 in the expression Cañada de Alcorcón. Local historical writing often explains Alcorcón from Arabic words meaning hill, mound or pass, fitting the old settlement on a rise. A philological line, however, is stronger for the name itself: Alcorcón can be read as a Mozarabic hybrid with Arabic al- plus corco, a Romance word related to Latin quercus, meaning oak, holm oak or cork oak. The town's pottery tradition is historically important, but it should be treated as local context rather than the most reliable origin of the name.
