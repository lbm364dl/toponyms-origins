The station name is direct and modern: **Avenida Sistema Solar** takes its name from **Avenida del Sistema Solar**, the avenue beside the stop in Parla Este. The Consorcio Regional de Transportes de Madrid lists the station as a surface stop at Avda. del Sistema Solar, 9, and the line diagram for the Parla tram includes the stop under the shortened station form, Avenida Sistema Solar.

The avenue's name belongs to the astronomical vocabulary used across this part of Parla Este. Around the stop, the street map shows names such as Avenida de los Planetas, Avenida de las Estrellas, Calle Planeta Tierra, Calle Planeta Venus, Calle Estrella Polar and several constellation streets. In that setting, 'Sistema Solar' works as an umbrella idea: the system that contains the planets and many of the celestial references used nearby.

This is not an inherited medieval or rural place-name. Parla Este is a planned urban expansion on the eastern side of Parla, developed through the Consorcio Urbanístico Parla Este. A BOE notice from 2000 already refers to the urbanization project for PAU 4, 'Residencial Este', and Parla's own demographic documentation describes Parla Este as a new-construction neighborhood whose development began at the start of the twenty-first century.

The tram reinforced that planned geography. The Parla light rail line links the old urban center with the newer eastern expansion, and the stop names in Parla Este often echo the surrounding street grid: Tierra, Venus, Estrella Polar and Avenida Sistema Solar are all examples of the transport map adopting the neighborhood's astronomical vocabulary.

The origin is therefore secure at the station level: the stop is named for its avenue. What remains less directly documented is the exact municipal act that assigned the astronomical street names, and the precise date when Avenida del Sistema Solar itself was named. Until that street-naming record is inspected, the safest public wording is to describe the station as named after the avenue within Parla Este's modern astronomical naming scheme.
