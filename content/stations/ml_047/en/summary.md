Avenida Sistema Solar is a descriptive stop name taken from the street where the Parla tram station stands: Avenida del Sistema Solar. The official CRTM station page locates the surface stop at Avda. del Sistema Solar, 9, and the current line diagram includes the stop as Avenida Sistema Solar. The name also fits Parla Este's wider astronomical naming pattern, visible in nearby streets such as Avenida de los Planetas, Avenida de las Estrellas, Calle Planeta Tierra, Calle Planeta Venus, and Calle Estrella Polar. The neighborhood itself was developed in the early twenty-first century through the Parla Este urban consortium, so this is a modern planned toponym rather than an older rural place-name.
