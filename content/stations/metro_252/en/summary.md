La Almudena is a Line 2 station opened with the La Elipa-Las Rosas extension on 16 March 2011. Its immediate name source is the nearby Cementerio de Nuestra Señora de la Almudena, officially listed by Madrid City Council on Avenida de Daroca and described by the regional project page as lying next to the station. The cemetery is named for the Virgen de la Almudena, patroness of Madrid. The name Almudena is tied to Arabic al-mudayna, the citadel or fortified enclosure of medieval Madrid, and to the traditional story of a Marian image hidden in the city wall and rediscovered after Alfonso VI took Madrid. Those legendary details should be presented as tradition rather than documented history.
