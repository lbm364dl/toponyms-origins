The station's present name, location, line, and 2007 opening are supported by transport and contemporary sources, and the Jarama River is the relevant local place-name. However, the available sources did not include an official Metro de Madrid or CRTM naming-resolution document explicitly stating 'named after the river', so the station-level etymology is best marked probable rather than fully verified. The hydronym etymology itself is explicitly uncertain in Toponomasticon Hispaniae.
