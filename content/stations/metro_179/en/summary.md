Jarama is a Line 7 station in San Fernando de Henares, opened with the MetroEste extension on 5 May 2007. Its name points to the Jarama River, which runs through the eastern side of the Madrid region and has long shaped the landscape and identity of San Fernando. Historical sources even show that the municipality was once known as San Fernando de Jarama before adopting its present name in 1916. The river name is much older than the metro station, but its etymology is not settled. Toponomasticon Hispaniae, written by Emilio Nieto Ballester, treats the safest explanation as uncertain and leans toward a plant-name origin connected with jaramago/saramago, possibly influenced in Castilian by jara.
