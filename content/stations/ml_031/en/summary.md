Cantabria is named for Avenida de Cantabria, the road beside the ML3 stop in Boadilla del Monte. Metro Ligero Oeste describes the station as located next to the avenue and opposite Banco Santander's Finance City. Santander's own material confirms that Ciudad Grupo Santander is on Avenida de Cantabria and that the bank began in 1857 in Santander, initially tied to trade through the port of Santander. A local article on Boadilla's street names says the roads around the campus recall places from the bank's region of origin, including Cantabria, Altamira and Picos de Europa. That motive is persuasive, but it should be treated as probable until a municipal naming file or council agreement is consulted.
