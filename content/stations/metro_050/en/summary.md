The station is named for Valdeacederas, a historical place and street name in Tetuán. The old Calle de Valdeacederas was renamed Calle del Capitán Blanco Argibay in 1949, but Metro kept the older name when the Line 1 station opened on 4 February 1961. The word is best read as a Castilian rural toponym: val, an apocopated form of valle, plus de acederas, 'of sorrels.' Sorrel is a sour-tasting plant whose Spanish name comes from Latin acetaria, related to acetum, 'vinegar.' The broad etymology is strong, although the exact local botanical history still deserves a primary archival source.
