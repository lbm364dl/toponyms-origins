The Metro station is named for Cruz del Rayo, a long-standing place-name in Chamartín that became especially visible through the Colonia Cruz del Rayo. The colony was built in 1928-1929 and is identified by COAM and Madrid municipal sources as Madrid’s first colony to follow, even if loosely, Ebenezer Howard’s garden-city model. The name itself predates the Metro and appears in official property and housing documents as a site name. Madrid’s municipal account preserves the traditional explanation: a cross in the area was struck by lightning. That last detail should be presented as legend, while the link to the historic colony and older toponym is well supported.
