The station name is geographical rather than commemorative. Metro and transport sources place Alto de Extremadura on Line 6, with accesses at Plaza Alto de Extremadura and Paseo de Extremadura, and a metro chronology records its opening on 10 May 1995 with the Laguna-Ciudad Universitaria section that made Line 6 circular. The older place behind the modern name was known as the Altos de Extremadura, and a Madrid Río historical report notes that this high ground was formerly called Cerro Bermejo because of its reddish clays. The 'Extremadura' element comes through the road: the present Paseo de Extremadura corresponds to earlier paths west of the Manzanares, including the camino de Alcorcón or de Extremadura.
