Tribunal is a place-name station named after the Tribunal de Cuentas, Spain's supreme public-sector audit institution, located at Calle Fuencarral 81. Its headquarters was designed by Francisco Jareño y Alarcón and built between 1860 and 1863. Metro de Madrid archive material shows that the station was drawn in 1918 as Hospicio, referring to the former Real Hospicio de San Fernando across Fuencarral, now the Museo de Historia de Madrid. The best-supported correction is that Hospicio should be treated as a documented project or early name, not as a name confidently lasting until the 1940s without further archival proof.
