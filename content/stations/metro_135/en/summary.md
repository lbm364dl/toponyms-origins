Simancas is a Line 7 station serving the Simancas and Amposta area of San Blas-Canillejas. A municipal heritage record states directly that the station takes its name from the Simancas neighbourhood. The Madrid name is best understood as a place-name reference to Simancas in Valladolid, a historic town on the Pisuerga. Toponomasticon Hispaniae identifies the ancient form as Septimanca, probably a Latin adaptation of a pre-existing Celtic or Indo-European name whose meaning is unknown. The famous tale of seven maidens who cut off their hands is a folk etymology, not the best-supported linguistic origin.
