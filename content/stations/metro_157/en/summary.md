Marqués de la Valdavia station was opened as part of MetroNorte on Line 10 and was named after the nearby Calle Marqués de la Valdavia in Alcobendas. A municipal street-history publication states that the street received this name by council agreement on 15 February 1958, replacing the older Carretera del Plantío, in honor of Mariano Ossorio Arévalo, III Marquess of Valdavia. Ossorio Arévalo was a politician who served as president of the Provincial Deputation of Madrid from 1947 to 1965 and held offices during the Franco period. The marquessate itself was granted by Alfonso XII to Josefa Lamadrid Cosío, and its territorial name comes from La Valdavia in Palencia. The station name was locally controversial before opening because Alcobendas's mayor argued that Parque de Cataluña better described its physical location.
