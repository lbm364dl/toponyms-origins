The most important record fix is not the noble-title background but the direct honoree. The station inherited a street name; the street-history source explicitly identifies the 1958 honoree as Mariano Ossorio Arévalo, not his father Mariano Osorio de Lamadrid. Spelling varies between Osorio and Ossorio in sources; the Congress record notes that he also appears as Ossorio, while local and many biographical references use Ossorio Arévalo.
