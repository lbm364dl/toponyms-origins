The station’s current name combines two local place names. Galapagar is best explained from Spanish galápago, a terrapin or turtle-like freshwater reptile, with the collective suffix -ar, meaning a place where such animals were abundant. La Navata points to the local district around the station and is plausibly formed from nava, a pre-Roman landscape word meaning flat, treeless, sometimes marshy land, with a diminutive nuance. Transport sources confirm the current official name Galapagar-La Navata; a railway history source says the station was renamed from La Navata in the 1990s.
