Estrella opened with the first section of Madrid Metro Line 9, between Sainz de Baranda and Pavones, on 31 January 1980. Its name refers to the barrio of La Estrella, beside the station's Retiro-side access and Calle de la Estrella Polar. The strongest available explanation for the barrio name is not a generic 'Star Street', but the former connection of the land with Seguros La Estrella. The celestial theme was then reinforced in the local street grid: Sirio, Cruz del Sur, Lira, Pez Volador, Pez Austral, Perseo, Piscis and Estrella Polar.
