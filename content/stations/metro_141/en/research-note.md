The key dataset update is to keep the station-level etymology as a place name but change the underlying explanation. The station is not named after the ordinary Spanish word 'star' in isolation, nor after Calle de la Estrella in Centro; it is named for La Estrella, the Retiro barrio beside the station, whose name is probably tied to Seguros La Estrella and then reinforced by a planned celestial street-name cluster. The press used 'La Estrella' for the served neighbourhood at the time of the Line 9 opening, while current municipal cartography uses 'Estrella' as Barrio 33.
