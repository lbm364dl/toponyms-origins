The station name comes from Plaza de Tirso de Molina, and ultimately from Fray Gabriel Téllez, the Madrid-born Mercedarian friar and Baroque dramatist known as Tirso de Molina. Metro de Madrid's museum material records that the station opened on 26 December 1921 as Progreso, because the square was then Plaza del Progreso. A metro chronology gives the formal station name change from Progreso to Tirso de Molina on 10 July 1939. The choice connected the postwar name of the square with the old Mercedarian convent that once occupied the site and with the writer who belonged to that order.
