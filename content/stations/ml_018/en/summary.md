Dos Castillas station takes its name from Vía de las Dos Castillas, the road on which the stop is located in Pozuelo de Alarcón. Official transport sources support this immediate origin, while the deeper meaning points to the historical expression "the two Castiles": Old Castile to the north and New Castile to the south. New Castile historically included Madrid, Toledo, Ciudad Real, Cuenca and Guadalajara, while modern autonomous communities no longer map neatly onto those older regions. The exact municipal act that named the road was not found, so the station-road link is verified but the street's historical motivation should be treated as probable.
