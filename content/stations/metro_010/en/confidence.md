The station-level naming is secure because the station is documented under Plaza de Chamberí from the inaugural Line 1 period. The deeper etymology is only probable because authoritative and scholarly sources agree on a Chambéry/Savoy connection but disagree on the transmission mechanism, and some official summaries still repeat a chronologically weak Napoleonic explanation. The Callejero row strengthens the plaza-name link, but the Chambéry transmission mechanism remains unresolved.
