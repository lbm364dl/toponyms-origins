Parque Parla Este is a descriptive tram-stop name. It refers to the large park associated with the Parla Este urban development, documented in municipal property records as Parque del Universo on parcels of the PAU-4 Bis Residencial Este. Contemporary press reports show that the park, formerly referred to as Parque de Parla-Este, was renamed Parque del Universo in 2008 to match the astronomy-themed street names chosen for much of the neighbourhood. The tram stop opened with the first Parla Tram service in 2007, so its name preserves the earlier, practical place label rather than the later official park name.
