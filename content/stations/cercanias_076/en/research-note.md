There is a useful distinction between the station name origin and the deeper place-name etymology. For the station, the evidence is direct: it bears the municipality's name. For the municipality, collado and mediano are clear Spanish words with RAE support, but the claim that the whole expression originally meant a medium-sized livestock enclosure appears to be an extrapolation from pastoral Sierra usage rather than a directly attested origin for Collado Mediano. Note also a date inconsistency in local material: one municipal route page says villazgo in 1623, while the book extract and other local histories give 1630, specifically 26 June 1630.
