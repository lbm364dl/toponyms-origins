Collado Mediano station takes its name from the Madrid municipality it serves. The Spanish word collado comes from Latin collis and means both a low raised ground and a gentle pass through a mountain range. Mediano means intermediate or moderate, so the safest reading is a modest or intermediate collado in the Sierra de Guadarrama setting. Local historical accounts connect the town with medieval disputes between Segovia and Madrid, the Real de Manzanares, livestock routes, and a railway stop that began operating in 1888. The more specific livestock-enclosure interpretation should be treated as possible local interpretation, not as the best-proven etymology.
