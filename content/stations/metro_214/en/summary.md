Hospital Infanta Sofía is the northern terminus of Madrid Metro Line 10, beside the public hospital of the same name in San Sebastián de los Reyes. When MetroNorte opened in 2007, the station and the still-new health complex were known as Hospital del Norte. Order 45/2008 of the Comunidad de Madrid changed the hospital's official name to Hospital Infanta Sofía, and later transport chronologies record the metro station's matching change from Hospital del Norte to Hospital Infanta Sofía on 8 August 2008. The eponym is Infanta Sofía de Borbón y Ortiz, born in Madrid on 29 April 2007, the second daughter of Felipe and Letizia.
