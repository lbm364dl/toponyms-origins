The hospital's legal renaming is better sourced than the station's own renaming. Order 45/2008 is dated 25 January 2008 and published on 13 February 2008; MadridMobilite says the hospital name would be acquired from 3 August, while several station chronologies and encyclopedic station pages give 8 August 2008 for the metro station. For dataset purposes, keep 2008-08-08 as the station naming date but flag it for confirmation from Metro de Madrid archival notices.
