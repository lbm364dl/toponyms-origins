The existing claim is substantially correct: the station took the current name from the nearby Hospital Universitario Infanta Sofía, formerly Hospital del Norte, and the hospital's name was officially changed by the Comunidad de Madrid in 2008. The main nuance is that the station is directly named after the hospital, not directly by a separate royal dedication, and in 2008 Sofía was the daughter of the then Prince of Asturias, not yet King Felipe VI.
