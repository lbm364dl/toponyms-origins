The immediate naming source is verified by current transport and planning sources: Somosaguas Sur is an official station and urban-planning area in Pozuelo de Alarcón. The deeper place-name explanation is probable rather than fully verified because the strongest direct historical wording emphasizes abundant waters, while the somo + aguas interpretation is supported indirectly by RAE and Toponomasticon material rather than by a dedicated authoritative entry for Somosaguas.
