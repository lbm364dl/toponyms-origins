Somosaguas Sur is a place-name stop: it takes its name from the Somosaguas Sur sector of Pozuelo de Alarcón, where the ML2 station is located. The deeper origin lies in the older Somosaguas toponym, known from the former hamlet of San Juan de Somosaguas, also recorded as San Juan de Sumas Aguas. Sixteenth-century historical-geographical material explained that name through the many good waters in the place, while modern toponymic evidence makes a connection with old Spanish somo, from Latin summum, plausible. The safest public explanation is therefore “the southern Somosaguas area,” with Somosaguas itself referring to a watery, elevated landscape rather than to a person.
