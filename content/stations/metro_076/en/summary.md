The station name is directly geographical: it identifies the Metro stop serving the Hospital Universitario 12 de Octubre, at Avenida de Córdoba and Glorieta de Málaga. The hospital itself opened on 2 October 1973 as Ciudad Sanitaria 1º de Octubre, a name tied to the Franco regime's 1 October commemorative date. The official hospital history says the name changed to 12 de Octubre at the end of the 1980s, although press usage shows 'Ciudad Sanitaria 12 de Octubre' already in 1981 and an exact legal renaming act was not found. The present name connects the hospital to 12 October, the date established by Law 18/1987 as Spain's National Day. The Metro station opened with the Line 3 extension to Villaverde Alto on 21 April 2007.
