Ciudad de la Imagen station is named after the nearby Ciudad de la Imagen business park in Pozuelo de Alarcón. Municipal and transport sources identify the area as an audiovisual-themed office and leisure complex, and Metro Ligero Oeste places the stop beside calle Juan de Orduña, near ECAM and the Carrefour Ciudad de la Imagen centre. The project was already being promoted in 1990 as the officially named Centro de Actividades Audiovisuales, on public land in Pozuelo, with production, commercial and support uses. Today the name is supported by the concentration of institutions such as ECAM, Telemadrid and Filmoteca Española's Centro de Conservación y Restauración de Fondos Fílmicos Carlos Saura.
