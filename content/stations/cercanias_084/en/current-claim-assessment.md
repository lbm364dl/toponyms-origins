The row is broadly right that the station takes its name from the municipality and that the toponym is built from tres plus canto, meaning a stone or rock. The strongest source found, Toponomasticon Hispaniae, treats the boundary-stone interpretation and the meeting of Colmenar Viejo, Alcobendas and Madrid as probable, not fully proven; the claimed pre-Roman or Celtic origin of canto is overstated because the word's origin is uncertain.
