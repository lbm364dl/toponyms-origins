Tres Cantos is a transparent Castilian place-name made from tres, 'three', and canto, a word that can mean a piece of stone or rock. Toponomasticon Hispaniae, the best direct toponymic source found, says that in this case canto probably refers to a boundary marker, and that the three stones probably marked the meeting of the terms of Colmenar Viejo, Alcobendas and Madrid. The modern municipality is much younger than the name: it was planned as a new town in the 1970s and became independent from Colmenar Viejo on 21 March 1991. The Cercanías station took the same local name when the railway connection to Tres Cantos entered service in 1991. The exact original stones and their location are not firmly documented in the authoritative sources consulted.
