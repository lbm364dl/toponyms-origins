San Nicasio is a Line 12 MetroSur station in Leganés, located in the San Nicasio neighbourhood near Avenida del Mar Mediterráneo. Its name is therefore topographic at station level, but the neighbourhood itself is religious in origin. Leganés has venerated San Nicasio since at least the late 16th century, when local records described the saint's feast as a vow made because of pestilence. The present hermitage, now a parish church, is an 18th-century building associated with Ventura Rodríguez and the Marqués de Leganés. The main correction to the existing row is that the Leganés tradition points to San Nicasio of Rouen, not San Nicasio of Reims.
