The core claim is well supported: the station is in and takes its name from the San Nicasio neighbourhood of Leganés, whose identity is centred on the old hermitage/parish and the town's patron saint. The current row likely misidentifies the Leganés saint as Nicasius of Reims, died c. 407; local ecclesiastical and historical sources identify the patron celebrated on 11 October as San Nicasio, bishop and martyr of Rouen, and preserve a plague-protection origin for the devotion.
