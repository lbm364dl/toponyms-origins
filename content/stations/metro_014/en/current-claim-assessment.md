The core claim is correct: the name refers to King Alfonso XIII and his role in the early Madrid Metro is well documented. Several details need tightening: the present station opened with the Diego de León-Alfonso XIII extension in March 1973, the best official source found says the king contributed one million pesetas rather than 1.45 million, and the 40-second figure refers only to the Cuatro Caminos-Ríos Rosas segment of the inaugural journey.
