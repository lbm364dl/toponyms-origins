Alfonso XIII is best understood as a station name taken from its urban setting: the Line 4 stop lies by López de Hoyos and Clara del Rey, close to Avenida de Alfonso XIII. That avenue commemorates Alfonso XIII, King of Spain, whose name was also tied to Madrid Metro from the beginning: the original company was the Metropolitano Alfonso XIII and he officially opened the first line on 17 October 1919. The current station was not part of that first line; it opened on 26 March 1973 with the Diego de León-Alfonso XIII extension. The existing dataset should keep the person etymology, but separate the 1973 station from the 1919 royal inauguration story.
