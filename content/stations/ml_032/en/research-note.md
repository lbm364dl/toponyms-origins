The strongest station-specific evidence is modern and administrative: the transport operator, CRTM and the municipal fairground ordinance all converge on the same physical relationship between the stop and the recinto ferial. The broader Boadilla etymology in the previous wording overreaches for this station and should be reduced to contextual nuance; Toponomasticon Hispaniae is the better authority and presents uncertainty between two non-Arabic etymological lines rather than a firm Arabic derivation.
