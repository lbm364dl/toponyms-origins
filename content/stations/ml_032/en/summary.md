Ferial de Boadilla is named for the nearby recinto ferial, the public fairground of Boadilla del Monte. Metro Ligero Oeste describes the ML3 station as being next to the Villaviciosa road and close to the town fairground, while the CRTM places its access at Ctra. de Boadilla-Villaviciosa, 13A. A 2016 municipal ordinance defines the recinto ferial as the physical space where fair activities take place during Boadilla's patron-saint festivities. Those fiestas are held in honour of Nuestra Señora del Rosario and take place in October. The broader name Boadilla del Monte has a complex toponymic background, but it is secondary here: the station name refers to the fairground, not directly to a person or medieval origin story.
