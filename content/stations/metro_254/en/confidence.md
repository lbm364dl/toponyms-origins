The direct station naming is secure because official and local transport sources place the station in the Ciudad de los Ángeles/Los Ángeles area, and the Line 3 extension opened in 2007. The architectural and urban history of the neighbourhood is well supported by COAM, Madrid Film Office and an academic article. Confidence is lowered from verified to probable because the 1958 popular-contest origin was found only in local/reused accounts rather than a primary municipal record, and because the Cerro's exact 'geographic centre' status is contested.
