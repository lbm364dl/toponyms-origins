Ciudad de los Ángeles station takes its name from the neighbouring residential area in Villaverde, officially within the Los Ángeles barrio. The neighbourhood was developed mainly from the mid-1950s through the 1960s as a satellite city of housing for migrant and industrial workers, especially those linked to nearby factories such as Barreiros. Local accounts say the name Ciudad de los Ángeles was chosen by popular vote in 1958 because of the area's proximity to the Cerro de los Ángeles in Getafe. That hill does contain the shrine and hermitage of Nuestra Señora de los Ángeles and is widely described as a traditional or symbolic centre of the Iberian Peninsula, although the exact geographic-centre claim is methodologically contested. The station opened with the Legazpi-Villaverde Alto extension of Line 3 on 21 April 2007.
