La Peseta station is named after Avenida de la Peseta, where its access is located. The avenue is part of the PAU de Carabanchel street-name scheme, which used names of Spanish currencies in the years when Spain changed from the peseta to the euro. The peseta had been established by government decree on 19 October 1868 and remained Spain's monetary unit for more than 130 years. Euro notes and coins entered circulation on 1 January 2002, but the peseta remained legal tender alongside them until 28 February 2002. Local records also show that neighbourhood groups wanted the Line 11 station to be called Salvador Allende, but the authorities chose La Peseta.
