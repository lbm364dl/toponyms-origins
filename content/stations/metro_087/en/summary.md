Antón Martín is named after the Plaza de Antón Martín, where the Line 1 station opened on 26 December 1921. The plaza’s name comes from Antón Martín, a Cuenca-born follower and successor of San Juan de Dios. In 1552 he founded the Hospital de Nuestra Señora del Amor de Dios, later popularly called the Hospital de Antón Martín or Hospital de San Juan de Dios, beside Calle de Atocha. The hospital became one of Madrid’s major charitable and medical institutions, and the old “Plaza del Hospital” was already associated with Antón Martín by the 17th century.
