The Line 8 station was inaugurated on 15 January 2007 and named for the Pinar del Rey area it serves in Hortaleza. The deeper place-name points to a public tree-planting festival held on 26 March 1896 at the Cerro del Centinela, today Pinar del Rey. Alfonso XIII was expected to attend, but because he was ill, the infantas Isabel and Eulalia planted the tree assigned to him. Official regional and municipal accounts identify that event as the origin of the park and the modern toponym. The earlier record claim about a Crown-owned royal pine forest should be replaced or heavily qualified.
