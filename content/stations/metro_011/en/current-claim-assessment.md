The core claim is well supported: the Metro station takes its name from Plaza del Callao, and the plaza commemorates the 2 May 1866 naval combat at Callao, Peru. Stronger sources refine the chronology: Madrid City Council agreed the plaza name on 13 June 1866, while the Metro station opened later, in 1941 for Line 3 and 1968 for Line 5. The battle's result should be described carefully because Spanish and Peruvian traditions both claimed victory.
