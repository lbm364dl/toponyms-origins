Callao is a station name inherited from the square above it, not directly from a Madrid neighborhood. The plaza was named for the naval combat fought on 2 May 1866 at the Peruvian port of Callao, during the Spanish-South American or Chincha Islands War. A recent article in the Spanish Navy's Revista General de Marina gives the most precise naming date found: on 13 June 1866 Madrid City Council agreed to call the future square Plaza del Callao. The Metro station opened as Callao on Line 3 in 1941, and became an interchange when Line 5 arrived in 1968. The episode remains historically charged because Spanish and Peruvian commemorations interpreted the outcome differently.
