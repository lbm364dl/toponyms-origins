Alpedrete station carries the name of the Madrid municipality where it stands. Toponomasticon Hispaniae, in an entry by E. Nieto Ballester, treats Alpedrete as an Arabized form, with the article al-, of late Latin *PĔTRĒTU: 'place of stones', 'stone field', or 'quarry'. That explanation matches the town's long association with granite quarrying, although the etymology itself should not be reduced to a modern reference to those quarries alone. Municipal material also records local traditions that derive the name from Roman Ad Petrum or from Arabic Al Pedrete, but the academic account is more precise and better supported.
