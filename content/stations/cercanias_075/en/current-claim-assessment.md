The existing explanation is substantially correct: the station is named for the municipality, and the best scholarly source explains Alpedrete as an Arabized form, with article, of late Latin *PĔTRĒTU, meaning a stony place, stone field, or quarry. The wording should be tightened because the Arabic element is an article added to a Romance/Latin-derived place word; the municipal 'Ad Petrum' and 'Al Pedrete' stories are local traditions, not the strongest linguistic explanation.
