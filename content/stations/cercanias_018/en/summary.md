Getafe Industrial is a descriptive railway name: it identifies the station with the industrial estates and factories around eastern Getafe. The station began as Getafe on the Madrid-Aranjuez railway, opened in 1851, and later became Getafe-Alicante to distinguish it from the other Getafe station on the Madrid-Badajoz line. The modern name reflects the industrial landscape around the railway, including historic firms such as Uralita and CASA and today's Airbus Defence and Space presence beside the air base. The aviation story is important context, but it should be presented carefully: Getafe was one of the first four Spanish air bases created in 1920 and the first in the Central/Madrid region.
