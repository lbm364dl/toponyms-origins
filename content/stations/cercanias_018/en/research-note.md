The strongest station-specific evidence is the CRTM historical railway plan, because it gives the name sequence for the exact station: Getafe in 1851, Getafe-Alicante from 1879, and later Getafe Industrial. The Museo virtual de Getafe page supports the same sequence but was available only through source notes. A separate station-opening question should distinguish the 1851 Madrid-Aranjuez railway station from the modern Cercanias service brand.
