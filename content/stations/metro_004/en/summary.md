La Latina is a Line 5 station opened with the Callao-Carabanchel section on 5 June 1968. Its name comes from the traditional Madrid area around Plaza de la Cebada, not from a modern administrative district of the same name. That place-name is traced by Madrid's official tourism and heritage sources to Beatriz Galindo, known as La Latina because of her command of Latin. The old Hospital de La Latina, associated with Galindo and Francisco Ramírez de Madrid, helped anchor the nickname in this part of the city. Recent scholarship supports the name connection but urges caution about later legendary claims that made Galindo a formal royal Latin teacher.
