The most important dataset change is not to weaken the origin, but to make it more precise. The inherited row leans on the chapel as the source of San José; the strongest available sources show that the estate was already called Quinta de San José in the 1913 deed, and that the chapel dedication followed or reinforced that name. The current marquis's centenary essay explicitly presents the Quinta explanation and treats the chapel explanation as oral tradition or a possible secondary hypothesis.
