San José de Valderas station is named after the Alcorcón neighbourhood of the same name, not directly after an individual saint alone. The name comes from the former estate known as the Quinta de San José and from the Marquises of Valderas, who acquired land there in 1913 and built the castle-like palaces completed around 1917. One of the buildings served as an oratory or chapel dedicated to Saint Joseph, reinforcing the name. The modern neighbourhood developed on the estate after its sale in 1957, with the first stone of the housing development laid on 1 April 1958. The Valderas title itself refers to the Leonese town of Valderas, a medieval place-name best explained as val de eras, 'valley of threshing floors'.
