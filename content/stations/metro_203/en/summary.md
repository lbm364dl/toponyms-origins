The station name comes from Rivas Futura, the surrounding urban, business and retail development in Rivas-Vaciamadrid. Contemporary reports describe the stop as serving the residential and commercial area of Rivas Futura, including business parks, industrial areas and the H2Ocio/H2O shopping and leisure complex. The commercial park had opened in 2006, but the Metro station itself entered service on 11 July 2008. Its name therefore reflects a modern planned place rather than an older toponym, person or historical event. The broader context is Rivas-Vaciamadrid's rapid transformation from a small postwar town into one of the fastest-growing municipalities in the Madrid region.
