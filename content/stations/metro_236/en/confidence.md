The station's location and current transport role are confirmed by CRTM and Comunidad de Madrid sources; Getafe municipal pages confirm the Getafe Norte context and 1990s development; the BOE documents Sector UP-I "El Casar" in 1991; and RAE plus Toponomasticon Hispaniae support the linguistic meaning of casar. The only inference is applying the general toponymic explanation of El Casar/casar to this Getafe sector, which is reasonable but not backed by a dedicated scholarly entry for the Getafe microtoponym.
