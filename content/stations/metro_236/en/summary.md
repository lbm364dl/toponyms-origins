The station name comes from El Casar, a sector or area of Getafe Norte and the nearby Avenida del Casar. Official municipal material confirms that Getafe Norte was developed in the mid and late 1990s, while a BOE notice shows the urban-development name Sector UP-I "El Casar" already in use in 1991. Linguistically, casar is not the verb "to marry" here, but an old Spanish noun derived from casa, meaning a cluster of houses not large enough to be a town. Toponomasticon Hispaniae explains the same formation as casa plus the collective-abundancial suffix -ar. The station opened with MetroSur in 2003 and became a larger interchange when Line 3 was extended to El Casar in 2025.
