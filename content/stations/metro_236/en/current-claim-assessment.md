The existing explanation is broadly correct: the station is named for the El Casar area/Avenida del Casar in Getafe Norte, and casar is a Spanish common noun for a group of houses too small to form a town. The main refinement is that official Getafe sources treat Getafe Norte as the neighbourhood; El Casar appears as a sector/area within it, already documented as urban-development Sector UP-I in 1991.
