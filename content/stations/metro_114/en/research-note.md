There is a useful distinction between the station etymology and the neighbourhood etymology. For dataset purposes, the station's named_after value should remain the Lucero neighbourhood, but the broader explanation should avoid presenting the celestial reading as the historical cause of the neighbourhood name. The inspected sources also indicate that any station opening_year value of 1983 is misleading for Lucero itself; 1983 belongs to the Oporto-Laguna Line 6 extension, while Lucero entered the network with the 1995 circular closure.
