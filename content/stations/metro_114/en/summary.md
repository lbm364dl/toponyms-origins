Lucero is a place-name station: it serves and names the Lucero neighbourhood in the Latina district. The current Metro station was part of the Line 6 circular closure between Laguna and Ciudad Universitaria, put into service in May 1995. The older neighbourhood name is attested before that: a 1954 municipal bulletin refers to the existing barrio del Lucero near the Carretera de Extremadura and the Colonia de los Ferroviarios. Linguistically, lucero in Spanish means Venus or a bright star, but no available source proves that the Madrid neighbourhood was named for a celestial observation point, a finca, or a person.
