The central claim is well supported: the station name is a place-name referring to Porto/Oporto, Portugal, very likely through the nearby Avenida de Oporto and the local Oporto transport node. The row should be tightened because I did not find a primary naming file explicitly saying the station was named after the avenue, and Line 6 only reached Oporto in 1981, after the Line 5 station opened in 1968.
