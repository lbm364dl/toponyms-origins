The station name points to Oporto, the traditional Spanish form of Porto, Portugal. In Madrid, the immediate urban reference is the Avenida de Oporto and the Oporto area in Carabanchel, documented in the official municipal street list. The transport history is layered: Oporto opened with Line 5 on 5 June 1968, while Line 6 reached the station in 1981. The Portuguese name Porto means 'port' or 'harbour', and the Spanish form Oporto preserves a fused Portuguese article from o Porto, 'the port'.
