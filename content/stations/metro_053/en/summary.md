The station name points to Oporto, the traditional Spanish form of Porto, Portugal. In Madrid, the immediate urban reference is Avenida de Oporto and the Oporto area in Carabanchel; the official historical Callejero records Avenida de Oporto from 8 January 1954. The transport history is layered: Oporto opened with Line 5 on 5 June 1968, while Line 6 reached the station in 1981. The Portuguese name Porto means 'port' or 'harbour', and the Spanish form Oporto preserves a fused Portuguese article from o Porto, 'the port'.
