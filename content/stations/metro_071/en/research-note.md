For public wording, the strongest wording is that the station is named after the Madrid street, which in turn commemorates Palos de la Frontera in Huelva. This avoids implying that Metro independently chose the Huelva town in 1949. Official Madrid documentation is especially useful because it ties together the false Palos de Moguer form, the 1642 adoption of Palos de la Frontera, the 1979 street correction, the Metro renaming, and the later 2022 barrio correction; however, it appears to give 1947 for the station opening, which conflicts with Comunidad de Madrid Line 3 chronology and Metro photographic archive metadata indicating 26 March 1949.
