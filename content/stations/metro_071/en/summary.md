The station is named for Palos de la Frontera, the municipality in Huelva associated with the first Columbian voyage. Its old Port of Palos was the river port from which Columbus, the Pinzón brothers and their crews sailed on 3 August 1492. When Metro opened the station on Line 3 on 26 March 1949, it followed the Madrid street name then in use: Palos de Moguer. Madrid later corrected the street name to Palos de la Frontera, and Metro's change took effect on 30 May 1986 after an agreement with Palos de la Frontera's town council.
