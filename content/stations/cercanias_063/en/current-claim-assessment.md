The wording is substantially correct: the station is named for Pozuelo de Alarcón, whose name combines a well-related toponym with the Alarcón surname added after the 1632 seigneurial purchase. The main correction is linguistic: Toponomasticon Hispaniae treats Pozuelo as a Castilian diminutive of pozo, not necessarily a direct survival from Latin puteolu; the Muslim-well explanation is a local tradition rather than the best-documented etymology.
