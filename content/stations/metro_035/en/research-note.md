The station’s name is best treated as a local Madrid toponym that points to the Philippines, not as a direct commemorative act by Metro independently honoring the country. The Gea/Madripedia street entry is useful but contains a likely chronological slip about Magellan in 1522; for public-facing text, the safer chain is Villalobos in 1543 for the name, Legazpi in 1565 for colonization, and the 1898 Treaty of Paris for the end of Spanish sovereignty.
