Alvarado is a place-based station name with a personal origin behind it. Metro de Madrid's historical material places the station under Bravo Murillo, by Calle de Alvarado, and the Madrid street is identified in station-name references as dedicated to Pedro de Alvarado y Contreras. Alvarado was a captain under Hernán Cortés, later governor and captain general of Guatemala, and a highly violent figure in the conquest of Mesoamerica. The station opened on 6 March 1929 as part of the Cuatro Caminos-Tetuán extension of Line 1.
