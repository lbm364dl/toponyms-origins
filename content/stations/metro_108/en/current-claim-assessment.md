The core claim is supported: the station takes its name from nearby Calle de Alvarado, understood in Madrid station-name references as referring to Pedro de Alvarado. The biographical details need more precision: 'Aztecs' should preferably be 'Mexica/Nahua', and his death is better described as resulting from injuries after being run over by a horse near Nochistlán during the Mixtón War, not simply in a failed Jalisco campaign.
