The station name honors Eugenia María Guzmán y Portocarrero, better known as Eugenia de Montijo, born in Granada on 5 May 1826 and deceased in Madrid on 11 July 1920. She became Empress of the French by marrying Napoleon III in 1853, but in Carabanchel her name is also local: the Montijo family was linked to the old Quinta de Miranda, a large estate in Carabanchel Alto. Madrid's street-name records list Calle de Eugenia de Montijo in Carabanchel from 1945, and the metro station opened on Line 5 on 27 October 1999 between Carabanchel and Aluche. The surviving public landscape is fragmentary, especially the Parque Eugenia de Montijo and its pond, but it preserves a trace of the former aristocratic estate.
