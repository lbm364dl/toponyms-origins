The strongest station-specific evidence is the RAH-Metro page, which directly pairs EUGENIA DE MONTIJO station with Eugenia María Guzmán y Portocarrero. For display, avoid implying that the station is named only after the street or only after the person: the public station name clearly matches a person, while the local mechanism is the pre-existing Carabanchel street and park. The Carabanchel estate material is well supported by the Museo de San Isidro PDF, but the surviving pond in the current park is recorded by the City as a circa-1970 feature, so it should not be described too simply as an untouched nineteenth-century remnant.
