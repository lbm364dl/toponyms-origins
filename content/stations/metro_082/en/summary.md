La Poveda is a surface station on Madrid Metro Line 9 in the La Poveda neighbourhood of Arganda del Rey, opened with the Arganda extension on 7 April 1999. The name comes from the older local place-name, not from a person or a modern institution. Local histories explain La Poveda as a former area of poplars or white poplars, while toponymic studies group forms such as La Poveda, La Pobeda and El Pobo with Populus, the poplar genus and the vernacular pobo. The modern neighbourhood grew around the sugar factory whose first stone was laid on 13 January 1900, and whose worker housing formed the original residential nucleus.
