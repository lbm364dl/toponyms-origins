Las Suertes is a Line 1 station in the Ensanche de Vallecas, opened on 16 May 2007 as part of the Congosto-Ensanche de Vallecas extension. Its name is best understood through Avenida de Las Suertes, a nearby avenue officially named by Madrid City Council in 2006. In Spanish, suerte is not only luck: the dictionary also records it as a bounded piece of arable land. That makes the station name a likely survival of Vallecas's agricultural landscape, although the specific municipal act names the avenue without explaining the older origin of the toponym.
