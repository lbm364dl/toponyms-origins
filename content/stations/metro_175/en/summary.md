Barrio del Puerto is a place-name station: it was named for the modern Coslada neighbourhood of the same name, on Avenida de España. The deeper origin is the Puerto Seco de Madrid-Coslada, an inland logistics terminal that made the municipality a freight gateway despite having no sea. A 1998 report on Coslada's urban plans stated that the new Barrio del Puerto would owe its name to the Puerto Seco planned in the town. The station opened with MetroEste on 5 May 2007, when Line 7 was extended toward Coslada and San Fernando de Henares.
