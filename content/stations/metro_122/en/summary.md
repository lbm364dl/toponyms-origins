Ciudad Universitaria station is named after the university district around it, not after a person. The place name means 'University City' and refers to the planned campus created to concentrate Madrid's scattered university faculties in a modern north-western site. The project was formally launched by Alfonso XIII through the Junta Constructora de la Ciudad Universitaria on 17 May 1927, with Modesto López Otero as technical director. The metro station opened on 13 January 1987 as the terminus of a Line 6 extension from Cuatro Caminos, giving the campus a direct rapid-transit link.
