Valdebernardo is best explained first as a place-name: the station serves the Valdebernardo sector of Vicálvaro and opened with that name on 1 December 1998, when the Line 9 extension reached the district. The name was already being used for the land before the new neighbourhood was built, and official planning documents in 1989 called the PAU-4 development “Valdebernardo norte y sur.” In Spanish toponymy, “val” is an apocopated form of “valle,” so the name transparently means “Bernardo’s valley.” Some secondary sources identify Bernardo as Bernardo Gómez, from Barcelona, linked to former land ownership, but no primary source proving that detail. The official Callejero now pushes the urban record earlier than the PAU: Calle de Valdebernardo is recorded from 28 November 1969, well before the 1989 Valdebernardo norte y sur planning documents.
