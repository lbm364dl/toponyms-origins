The best-supported update is to split the etymology into two levels: the station is named after the Valdebernardo place/neighbourhood, while the deeper anthroponymic explanation of Bernardo remains unverified. The official and architectural sources show continuous use of Valdebernardo for the PAU and land before the station; they do not identify Bernardo. The 2017 barrio creation should be kept as administrative context, not as the date when the name arose.
