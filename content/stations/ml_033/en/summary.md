Boadilla Centro is a place-based station name. Metro Ligero Oeste describes the stop as being on the M-516, near the commercial area of Boadilla del Monte, the old town, and the El Palacio and Zoco shopping centres. The deeper name, Boadilla del Monte, is older and less straightforward: Toponomasticon Hispaniae treats Boadilla as part of a wider Boad-/Bovad-/Bobad- series and leaves two possible origins open, Germanic *BÛWITHA or Latin-Romance *BŎVĀTA. The del Monte element is clearer: it was added formally after older records that simply say Boadilla, and it refers to the genuinely wooded, hilly terrain around the town.
