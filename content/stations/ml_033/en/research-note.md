Toponomasticon Hispaniae supersedes the older row’s weaker mix of Wikipedia and generic Toponhisp references: it now has a direct Boadilla del Monte entry dated 09/04/2026. That entry is especially useful because it separates the uncertain Boadilla root from the clearer del Monte addition and notes that 1751 still has Boadilla alone, while 1829 has Boadilla del Monte. The station name itself should remain classified as a place/descriptive central-location name rather than as a direct commemoration of the palace or the Infante.
