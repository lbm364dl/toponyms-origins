Baunatal is one of Madrid Metro's place names that points outside Spain. It refers to Baunatal, in Hesse, Germany, a twin city of San Sebastián de los Reyes since 28 August 1990. When the northern extension of Line 10, MetroNorte, opened on 26 April 2007, one of San Sebastián de los Reyes's new stations received this name as a municipal homage to that German partner city. The German town itself is a modern municipality formed in the 1960s from older villages in the Bauna valley, strongly associated today with Volkswagen's Kassel/Baunatal components plant. The station name is therefore not an old Madrid toponym but a recent civic and international-link name.
