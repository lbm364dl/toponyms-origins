The direct origin of the station name is the German town of Baunatal, in the state of Hesse. San Sebastián de los Reyes and Baunatal established their town-twinning relationship on 28 August 1990, and the Madrid-area station uses that foreign place name as a civic tribute rather than as a local medieval or landscape toponym.

The name also fits the local urban setting. The station serves the area around Plaza de la Universidad Popular and Avenida de Lomas del Rey, close to streets and facilities associated with the Baunatal name in San Sebastián de los Reyes. Contemporary coverage of the MetroNorte opening described Baunatal as the first station in San Sebastián de los Reyes and explicitly called its name an homage to the German city with which the municipality was twinned.

The transport chronology is clear. MetroNorte, the northern extension of Line 10, opened to the public on 26 April 2007, with stations in Alcobendas and San Sebastián de los Reyes. Baunatal was listed among the San Sebastián de los Reyes stations on the new Line 10b/MetroNorte section, then operated as part of Line 10 with the familiar transfer arrangement at Tres Olivos.

Behind the German place name is a young city with older roots. Official Hessian historical data records Baunatal as a new municipality name from 1964 and a city from 1966, formed from villages including Altenbauna, Altenritte, Kirchbauna and Großenritte. The name evokes the Bauna valley, and the municipality's modern growth was closely tied to the arrival of Volkswagen's Kassel plant in the Baunatal area in the late 1950s.

The main naming explanation is well supported, but a few details need caution. The station did not receive its name because Baunatal was an ancient Madrid place; it reflects a late-20th-century international municipal relationship. Sources differ in how they describe the exact station siting and in current Volkswagen employment figures, so public text should avoid over-precise claims such as saying the station is simply 'beneath Avenida de Baunatal' or fixing a single workforce number without dating the source.
