The station name is a practical place-name: Rivas identifies the municipality, while Urbanizaciones points to the residential developments that expanded west of the old town. Community of Madrid documentation says the station serves a residential area made up of urbanizaciones and local facilities, and a municipal planning document identifies its location as Covibar. The name was already fixed for the Line 9 extension before it opened on 7 April 1999. Broader explanations of Rivas-Vaciamadrid's name are useful background, but they should not replace the station's more direct origin.
