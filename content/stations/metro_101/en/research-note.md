The strongest station-specific source is Metro's own 2024 historical PDF, which is more precise than the current row because it explains the name via the street and gives useful planning context: two projected stations once used the name Velázquez, one near Alcalá/Velázquez and the present Line 4 station on Goya. The PDF has a small chronology tension: it lists 24 March 1944 in the station data but also says the official inauguration was held on 23 March and service opened the next day. For public display, the cleanest wording is that the station opened with the 1944 Line 4 inaugural section, with official inauguration on 23 March and public opening on 24 March.
