Velázquez station opened with Line 4's first Argüelles-Goya section in March 1944. Metro de Madrid's historical material locates it on Calle Goya between Calle Velázquez and Calle Castelló and explains the name through Calle de Velázquez. That street is dedicated to Diego Rodríguez de Silva y Velázquez, the great Spanish Baroque painter born in Seville in 1599 and dead in Madrid in 1660. Velázquez entered royal service in Madrid in 1623 and became the defining painter of Philip IV's court. His Las Meninas, painted in 1656 in the Alcázar, remains one of the Prado's central works and one of the most studied paintings in European art.
