The core claim is well supported: the station is named for Calle de Cartagena, which in turn refers to Cartagena in Murcia. The main corrections are geographic: the station is at Avenida de América and Calle de Cartagena, between Prosperidad/Chamartín and La Guindalera/Salamanca, not in Ciudad Lineal; the official street listing places Calle de Cartagena in Chamartín and Salamanca.
