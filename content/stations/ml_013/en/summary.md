The ML2 stop is named after Prado del Rey, the area of Pozuelo de Alarcón where it stands, beside the RTVE studios. In Spanish, 'prado' means a meadow or irrigated pasture and comes from Latin 'pratum'; 'del Rey' means 'of the king'. The old landscape clue is reinforced by the nearby Arroyo de Prado del Rey and by the area's position next to the historic Casa de Campo, long a royal estate and hunting landscape before becoming public property in 1931. The modern fame of the name comes from RTVE, whose Prado del Rey studios opened on 18 July 1964.
