The strongest direct evidence is for a station named after a present-day area and street/toponym, not for a documentary origin story of the medieval or early-modern place name. The RTVE association is secondary for etymology but important for public recognition. A better dataset wording would avoid making the stream, Crown pasture, and royal hunting ground sound equally verified; the stream and Casa de Campo boundary are attested, while exact Crown ownership of the station site remains to be checked.
