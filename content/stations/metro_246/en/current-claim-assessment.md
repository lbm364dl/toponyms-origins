The main claim is well supported: the station takes its name from the district and historic town of Villa de Vallecas, part of the former municipality of Vallecas annexed to Madrid in 1950. The deeper etymology of Vallecas is genuinely contested; the Corriente explanation is plausible but should be presented as a scholarly hypothesis, not a settled fact. The previous wording also appears to carry the wrong opening year: this station belongs to the March 1999 Line 1 extension, not the 2007 Ensanche extension.
