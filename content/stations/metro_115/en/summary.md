The transport name Laguna is best understood as a local place-name inherited from Calle/Camino de la Laguna rather than as the name of an official Madrid neighbourhood. Municipal and archive sources show that the street name is present today and that an older Camino de la Laguna was already remembered in 1958. The Spanish word laguna comes from Latin lacuna and means a natural, usually freshwater, body of water smaller than a lake. The surrounding Latina-Carabanchel landscape was historically crossed by streams, but I did not find an authoritative source proving a specific former lagoon at the station site.
