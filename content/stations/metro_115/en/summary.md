The transport name Laguna is best understood as a local place-name inherited from Calle/Camino de la Laguna rather than as the name of an official Madrid neighbourhood. Municipal and archive sources show that the street name is present today, while the official historical Callejero records Camino de la Laguna from 1927 and Calle de la Laguna from 27 February 1974. The Spanish word laguna comes from Latin lacuna and means a natural, usually freshwater, body of water smaller than a lake. The surrounding Latina-Carabanchel landscape was historically crossed by streams, but the available sources did not include an authoritative source proving a specific former lagoon at the station site.
