Parque Polvoranca is a C-5 Cercanías station in Leganés, opened in 2004 to serve new urban growth in the municipality. Its name points directly to the nearby Parque de Polvoranca, known officially in regional environmental material as Parque Forestal Polvoranca, the largest periurban park created by the Community of Madrid. That park, in turn, inherits the name of the former village or estate of Polvoranca, remembered today by the ruins of San Pedro Apóstol. Older explanations link the name with forms such as Loranca/Loranque and with the idea of a populated place, but the available scholarly source treats this as conjectural, not proven.
