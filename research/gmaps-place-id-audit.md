## Notes

- **subway_station** and **train_station** entries open Google Maps with the full metro/rail line overlay showing connecting lines, schedules, etc.
- **transit_station** entries open the correct Place card with reviews, photos, and address, but without the metro line overlay. This is a Google Maps internal classification we cannot change.
- All Metro Ligero and Tranvía de Parla stations are classified as `transit_station` by Google -- this is expected since they're light rail, not heavy metro.
- A few metro stations (Gran Vía, Tirso de Molina, Príncipe de Vergara, etc.) are also only `transit_station` in Google's database despite being actual metro stations.
